On this day in 1939, No 5 Initial Training Wing of the Royal Air Force was formed at the Eversfield Hotel in Hastings, in No 54 Group.

The first intake of 250 trainees arrived on 22 November, with another 250 arriving a week later and a further 500 in the week commencing 9 December 1939.

Hastings and St Leonards were early hosts of such units. No 3 Initial Training Wing had formed in Hastings and St Leonards on Sea on 18 September 1939, with its headquarters at Marine Court in St Leonards on Sea, and No 14 Initial Training Wing was later formed as a self accounting unit at Marine Court in Hastings on 23 September 1941.

The No 5 wing moved to Torquay, with its headquarters at Castle Chambers, Higher Union Street, on 24 June 1940, and was finally disbanded on 1 March 1944.
