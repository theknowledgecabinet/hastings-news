On this day in 1937, the Hastings by-election was held. The contest resulted in a victory for the Conservative candidate, Maurice Hely-Hutchinson.

This election was necessitated by the resignation of the incumbent Conservative MP, Eustace Percy. The local electorate turned out to select a new representative for the constituency, with the Conservative party retaining the seat through Hely-Hutchinson’s win.

The event marks a specific moment in the political history of Hastings, where the departure of a sitting member triggered a direct vote. It highlights the continuity of Conservative representation in the area during this period, as the party successfully held the seat following Percy’s departure.
