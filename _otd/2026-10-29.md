On this day in 1869, Frederick North, the long-serving Liberal MP for Hastings, died, aged 69.

North had sat for Hastings across three spells: from 1831 to 1835, 1854 to 1865, and 1868 to 1869, according to Wikipedia. He was also a Deputy Lieutenant and Justice of the Peace for Norfolk.

His daughter Marianne North, according to Wikipedia, became a notable traveller and botanical illustrator. North's Seat, the highest point in Hastings, is named in his honour, and France can be seen from it on a clear day.
