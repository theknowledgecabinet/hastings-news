Michael Foster made his maiden speech on 21 November 1997. He had won the Hastings and Rye seat at the 1997 General Election, where he became the second least expected Labour MP in the landslide. In that election, he defeated the new sitting Conservative MP, Jacqui Lait, by 2,560 votes. Foster served as the Member of Parliament for Hastings and Rye from 1997 to 2010. He was a British lawyer and Labour politician who also served in government as a Minister for Equalities from 2009 to 2010. Foster was born in Hastings, Sussex, and attended the local Hastings Secondary School for Boys and the Hastings Grammar School. He later attended the University of Leicester, where he received a Master of Laws degree. He was admitted as a solicitor in 1980 and worked as a partner of Fynmores Solicitors in Bexhill-on-Sea, specialising in employment law, until 1998.
