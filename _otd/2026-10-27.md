This week in local history: on 28 October 1891, St Leonards held the opening ceremony of its pier.

The pier was hailed as a masterpiece of design and construction, according to the Hastings History website. Its main feature was a 750-seat semi-Moorish pavilion at the landward end. It was 900 feet long, but only 25 feet wide along its central section.

In November 1896 both piers staged 'animated picture' shows, making them the first cinemas in the town, with St Leonards showing a week before its Hastings neighbour. When the pier reopened in 1909 as the "American Palace Pier", about 4,000 lamps were fixed all over it, making it a big attraction in the evenings, and a new pavilion was built as a roller skating rink at the seaward end that summer.

When the Second World War began in 1939, St Leonards Pier closed and never reopened to the public.
