On this day in 1964, Kevin Ball was born in Hastings. He went on to have a professional football career, according to Wikipedia, with his senior career running from 1981 to 2002 across five clubs: Coventry City, Portsmouth, Sunderland, Fulham and Burnley.

Ball began his career at Coventry City, where he did not make a first team appearance, and joined Portsmouth in 1982, making his first team debut at 17 years old and going on to make 105 appearances for the club. In 1990 he was signed by Sunderland, where he played 340 games and scored 21 goals. He then played for Fulham and Burnley, spending two seasons at the latter before retiring.

At Sunderland he was named Player of the Year for the 1990 to 91 and the 1996 to 97 seasons, played in the 1992 FA Cup Final, and captained the club to First Division titles and promotion to the Premier League in both 1996 and 1999. In total he made 545 senior appearances. He was known for his tough tackling and combative playing style.
