On 25 October 1823, the new town hall in the High Street opened its ground floor to the market traders, with 14 stalls taking up their positions.

The foundation stone of the building had been laid just over four months earlier, on 2 June 1823. The market floor offered meat on Wednesdays and Saturdays, and fruit, fish, eggs and the like on other days, with the market closed on Sundays.

As the town's needs changed, the ground floor took on other roles, becoming home to a police station and a fire station. The building is now the Old Town Hall Museum.
