Neill Michael Daunt was born in Hastings on 23 October 1909. He became a British test pilot, a role defined by severe accidents he was lucky to survive, including one for which he had no recollection. His early life was marked by loss, with his father, a General Practitioner, dying in 1920 and his eldest brother, Barry, killed in World War One in 1918. Daunt studied engineering at St Catharine's College, Cambridge, where he learned to fly with the Cambridge University Air Squadron.
