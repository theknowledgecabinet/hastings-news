On this day in 1919, Captain Leslie William Burbidge transferred to the Royal Air Force's unemployed list. Born in Bexhill-on-Sea in 1891, Burbidge had served as an observer and gunner in Bristol F.2 Fighters with 20 Squadron during the First World War. He was credited with six aerial victories, earning the distinction of a flying ace.

His service included a harrowing incident in September 1918, where he collided with another aircraft while flying with Thomas Traill. Burbidge climbed out onto the opposite wings, which stabilized their broken plane enough for Traill to be able to crash-land. This act of gallantry earned him the Distinguished Flying Cross. After serving in 22 Squadron and later in 12 Squadron in Cologne, Germany, he transferred to the unemployed list.
