On 11 November 1940, an air attack reached The Lawn, a road of semi-detached houses in St Leonards.

According to the raid damage records, every one of the five pairs of semi-detached houses on The Lawn suffered some damage in that raid. Most of the houses were unoccupied or their residents away at the time. The damage listed for one house included 'many slates broken, large number of windows, frames etc. damaged'. The information given on the damage was usually even less.

Volunteers at Hastings Museum and Art Gallery are now transcribing the typed reports onto a spreadsheet. The reports consist of lists by street of the houses damaged in each raid.
