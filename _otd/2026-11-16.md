On Monday 15 November 2010, the ASDA Superstore in Silverhill officially opened its doors. The development, situated on the former Marshall Tufflex site, included a café, a pharmacy, and a large car park. A petrol station was added to the site later, opening in 2014.

The opening followed a period of controversy regarding the original plans. While a poll set up by ASDA showed 72.8% support for the project, some residents argued the store would cause extra congestion and pollution. Consequently, the plans were revised in 2009 before construction proceeded.

This modern addition to the Silverhill landscape has required infrastructure adjustments to manage the increased activity. The development led to minor changes at the four-way junction in Silverhill, including new and improved street lighting, extra traffic lights, and a bus layby to accommodate heavier traffic flows.
