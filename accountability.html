Who represents you

Accountability

Who turns up, how they vote and how they take part: the area's two MPs and {{ A.councillors.size }} councillors, counted from Parliament's and the councils' own records and updated every few hours.

How to read this
  • Everything here is counted from published records; nothing is written by AI, and the summaries use fixed wording filled in with the numbers.
  • Attendance is meetings a councillor was expected at as a committee member, as the council recorded them, over the last 12 months. Councillors in senior posts sit on more committees, and someone elected recently has fewer meetings, so each rate is shown with the counts behind it. No rate is given for fewer than {{ A.min_expected }} meetings.
  • Apologies are recorded when a councillor tells the council in advance they can't attend, often for illness, caring or work. The records don't say why.
  • Votes. Most council votes are a show of hands, and nobody's vote is recorded unless a named ("recorded") vote is called. MPs' votes are all recorded. A missed Commons vote can be for constituency or other business; Parliament doesn't record why.
  • Party figures add up the councillors' meetings, so a party's rate is its members' meetings attended out of meetings expected.
