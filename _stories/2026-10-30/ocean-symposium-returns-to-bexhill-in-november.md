Bexhill is to host an Ocean Symposium and Marine Exhibition at St Peter's Community Centre on Saturday, November 14.

The annual event, from 10am to 4pm, is co-hosted by the United Nations Association Climate and Oceans, the Sussex Dolphin Project, Sussex Underwater and community supporters, with this year's theme "Reimagine: Beyond the World We Know, A New Relationship with our Ocean".

The day will feature talks from expert speakers, short underwater films and a range of stalls, with sea and river pollution and citizen science high on the agenda. The local marine ecology group Strandliners will be represented.

Tickets are available through Eventbrite.
