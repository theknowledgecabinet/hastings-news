The Mermaid Inn in Rye, a Grade II listed building, is said to be one of the UK's most haunted inns, according to The Argus.

The inn dates back to the 12th century, with the current structure rebuilt in 1420 and cellars dating back to 1156. Visitors and staff alike have reported unexplained phenomena throughout its rooms, and some refuse to enter certain areas alone.

Room 17 is said to turn icy cold without warning, with a rocking chair reported to move on its own, and chambermaids reportedly clean it in pairs. Room 5 is haunted by a "Lady in White", and guests have sent in photographs showing mysterious orbs.

The inn's guest book has included the names of Lord Alfred Douglas, Henry James and Dame Ellen Terry, among others, the paper said.
