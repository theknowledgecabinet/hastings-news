Monellis Sourdough Pizza, a family-run pizzeria in Hastings Old Town, has been named the best in the world for 2026, topping The World's 100 Best Pizzerias, a list compiled by international travel and venue-ranking platform Worth a Journey.

The restaurant, founded by Frankie Ruffolo and his wife Francesca Elia in December 2019, came first ahead of pizzerias in Italy, Japan and the United States. Mr Ruffolo and Ms Elia said: "We are genuinely surprised, deeply honoured and incredibly proud."

The heart of the pizzeria is a sourdough starter dating back to 1923, passed down from Mr Ruffolo's grandmother in Mendicino, Calabria. The dough is fermented for up to seven days, using traditional Italian methods.

A spokesperson for Worth a Journey said its rankings are calculated using a proprietary algorithm analysing millions of data points from hundreds of thousands of venues worldwide, with adjustments informed by in-person evaluations.
