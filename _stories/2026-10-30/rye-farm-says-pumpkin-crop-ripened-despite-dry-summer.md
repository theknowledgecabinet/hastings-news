Pumpkin growers in Rye and the Sompting area near Lancing say they have produced a strong Halloween harvest, despite what the BBC described as the UK's hottest summer on record.

At Tibbs Farm near Rye, partner Phil Wheeler said the pumpkins were planted on south-facing land which had not previously been ploughed, and that avoiding weeds was an important factor in achieving a strong crop. Although the pumpkins did not grow as large as in some years, Wheeler said most ripened successfully: "They weren't big but they were ripened."

Farms in Sompting and Rye are welcoming visitors picking pumpkins in the run-up to Halloween, the BBC reported.
