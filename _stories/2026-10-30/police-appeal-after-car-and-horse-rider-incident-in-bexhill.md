Rother Police are appealing for information following a road traffic incident in the Broad Oak Lane area of Bexhill.

A spokesperson for Sussex Police said the incident was between a car and a horse rider, sometime between 1pm and 6.30pm on 6 September. The horse, described as white with black spots, was uninjured.

Police are asking anyone who saw the incident or who may have dashcam footage of it to come forward.

To help, email collision.appeal@sussex.police.uk quoting serial 433 of 19/10.
