Hastings Borough Council's Cabinet will meet on Monday 9 November to discuss a range of reports, the council said.

The agenda covers financial updates, including fees and charges for 2027-28, a report on the external funding the council has received for heritage and leisure work, and an update on the Age Friendly strategy.

Cllr Glenn Haffenden, leader of the council, said the reports show the breadth of work being done by the council's officers, and that the council was pleased to be proposing to freeze many of its fees and charges to help residents with the increasing cost of living. He said the council had been successful in receiving external funding to invest in sport, heritage and leisure assets.
