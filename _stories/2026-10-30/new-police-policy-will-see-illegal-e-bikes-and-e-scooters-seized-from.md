Sussex Police and Surrey Police have introduced a tougher policy under which riders caught using illegal electric vehicles in public could have their vehicles seized and destroyed.

Police are warning riders to think twice before taking illegally used electric vehicles onto public roads and spaces, with the new "no returns" procedure coming into effect on Sunday 1 November across Sussex and Surrey.

The policy will apply to e-scooters being used illegally on public roads or in public places, e-bikes that do not comply with Electrically Assisted Pedal Cycle regulations, and e-motorcycles being ridden unlawfully. It follows growing concerns from residents about the use of illegal and high-powered electric vehicles, which police say are increasingly linked to road traffic offences, antisocial behaviour and criminal activity.
