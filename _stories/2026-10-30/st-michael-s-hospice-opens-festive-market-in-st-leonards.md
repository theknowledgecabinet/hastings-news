St Michael's Hospice is holding a two-day Festive Market at its Upper Maze Hill site in St Leonards on Saturday, November 14, and Sunday, November 15.

The market closes at 4pm on Saturday and 3pm on Sunday, with stalls selling local handmade crafts, jewellery, gifts and decorations, along with live music, festive refreshments and games for families with young children.

A Santa's Grotto cabin in the hospice garden is the highlight for children, where visitors can share their Christmas wishes, take a photo and receive an early treat; booking early is recommended to avoid disappointment.

There is also a Festive First Look evening on Friday, November 13, from 6 to 8pm, an adults-only browse before the crowds arrive. Spaces are limited. The event is sponsored by Percy Walker Solicitors and TE Connectivity.
