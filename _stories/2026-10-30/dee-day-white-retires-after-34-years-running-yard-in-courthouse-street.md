Dee Day White is retiring after running a popular antique and bric-a-brac mews and yard in Courthouse Street, Hastings Old Town, for the past 34 years.

He opened his first shop, Anvil Antiques at 38 High Street, 61 years ago, he said, and after about 20 years bought a property in Courthouse Street where he opened a tea room and antique shop. With the help of friend Ian Porter, who bought the undertakers in the High Street with its large yard and outbuildings at the rear, he opened it as an arcade market that grew over the years.

He and his wife Beverley have decided to retire and spend more time together. The yard will continue to run on the same system, with Kelly taking over the till in place of Dee and Bev, he said.
