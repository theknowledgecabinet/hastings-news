Eight residents of Hastings, Bexhill and St Leonards who were arrested under the Terrorism Act have sent an open letter to Helena Dollimore MP, renewing their call for her to meet them on film to defend her vote to ban Palestine Action, according to the Hastings Online Times.

Palestine Action, a protest group that opposed UK arms sales to Israel, was banned in July 2025 when MPs, including Ms Dollimore, voted to proscribe it under the Terrorism Act.

The letter says the eight signatories were arrested for displaying signs reading "I oppose genocide. I support Palestine Action", and claims the ban "has already led to the arrest of over 3,000 people" and a de facto ban on new copies of Sally Rooney's books being sold in the UK.

The signatories say it is now more than ten months since they first wrote to the MP, and that they have not received a reply.
