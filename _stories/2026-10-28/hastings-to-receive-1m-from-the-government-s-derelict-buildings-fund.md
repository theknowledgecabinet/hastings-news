Hastings has been awarded £1 million from the government's Derelict Buildings Fund, according to the Hastings Observer.

Hastings and Rye MP Helena Dollimore said the money is intended to bring empty and derelict buildings back into use, put communities in control of valued local assets and help high streets. She named several well-known empty buildings in Hastings, including St Mary's in the Castle, the Azur, Queensbury House and the old Argos building.

The award is in addition to a £21.5 million Pride in Place fund the MP secured to improve Hastings over the next 10 years. She also said she launched a campaign earlier this year calling on Hastings Borough Council to use High Street Rental Auction Powers, which Rother District Council and Thanet Council, among others, are already using.

Residents will have a say in how the money is spent.
