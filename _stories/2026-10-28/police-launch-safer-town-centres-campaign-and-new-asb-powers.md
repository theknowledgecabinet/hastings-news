Sussex Police is launching a month-long Safer Town Centres campaign across Sussex, backed by new powers to tackle anti-social behaviour, a police spokesperson said.

The new powers include what police called 'Respect Orders', which the spokesperson said give police and partners stronger tools to address persistent offending and protect communities.

Police said the campaign is aimed at helping people feel safer on the street, and will be used to explain how the new powers work.
