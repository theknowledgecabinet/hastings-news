Residents have called for the disused public toilets site in Harold Place, Hastings town centre, to be put to use, after stormy weather left a trail of debris at the site, the Observer reported.

A number of residents commented on the Observer's Facebook page, with suggestions including reinstating the public toilets, turning the site into a community garden, or making it green space with benches. One called for it to become a taxi rank or a larger bus stop.

The public conveniences closed in April 2017 after the council shut them to make budget savings. Council-led plans for a two-storey restaurant at the site were turned down by the authority's own planning committee in March 2022, with councillors citing residents' concerns about noise and traffic. The site has remained undeveloped since.

Hastings Borough Council has been approached for a comment.
