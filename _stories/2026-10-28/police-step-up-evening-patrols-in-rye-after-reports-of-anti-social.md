Officers and police community support officers have continued with reassurance patrols in Rye and the surrounding areas, with particular attention given to Love Lane, Sussex Police has said.

The patrols follow reports of anti-social behaviour, police said.

Neighbourhood policing relies on information from the community, a Sussex Police spokesperson said, adding that when issues are reported, police can assess the concerns, increase patrols where appropriate and, where necessary, work with partners or deploy additional resources to address ongoing problems.
