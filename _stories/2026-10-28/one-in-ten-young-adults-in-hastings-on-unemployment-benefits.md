More than one in ten young adults in Hastings are claiming unemployment-related benefits, Office for National Statistics figures show, according to The Argus.

In September 2026, 10.2 per cent of 18 to 24-year-olds in the town were claiming benefits, more than double the 5 per cent average for the South East, against a 6 per cent Great Britain average.

That is a 1.9 percentage point rise on September 2025, when 8.6 per cent of Hastings young people were claiming, after the number had fallen by 2.9 per cent between September 2024 and September 2025.

Across all working-age people, 2,785 residents (5 per cent) were claiming unemployment-related benefits in September, compared with 3.1 per cent in the South East and 3.9 per cent in Great Britain. The most recent job density figures from 2024 show about 0.68 jobs per working-age resident in Hastings, against 0.85 in the South East and Great Britain.

The ONS warned that the shift from previous benefit systems to Universal Credit in March 2026 may have some impact on the claimant count.
