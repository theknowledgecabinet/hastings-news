Hastings Borough Council has launched The Fairness Fund, a £1.2 million programme to support community-led projects that improve opportunities, health and wellbeing in the parts of the town where funding is needed most.

The council said the fund will be predominantly capital funding for projects that deliver lasting social value and help tackle inequality, with a focus on grassroots organisations and community groups. Applications are due to open later this year, with funding available from the spring of 2027.

A flagship project will be a Children's University Academy for Hastings, delivered with the London South East Academies Trust and East Sussex County Council. The out-of-school learning programme supports children aged five to 14 and is expected to bring extra opportunities to children from disadvantaged backgrounds.

Cllr Glenn Haffenden, leader of the council, said the fund was about 'investing in the people, places and communities that make Hastings special'.
