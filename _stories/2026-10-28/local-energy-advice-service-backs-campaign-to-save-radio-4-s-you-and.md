Energise Sussex Coast, the local energy advice service, is urging East Sussex residents to sign a national petition to save Radio 4's consumer programme You and Yours, according to the Hastings Online Times.

The BBC has decided to axe the programme next April as part of cuts aimed at generating £500m in savings for the corporation.

The petition, which already has more than 12,000 signatures, says the programme has been a voice for ordinary consumers for more than 50 years, exposing fraud and unfair treatment.

Kate Meakin, ESC's director, said You and Yours "provides a vital public service for all of us", and urged everyone to sign the petition opposing the decision.

The Hastings Online Times notes ESC, a community benefit cooperative founded in 2012, runs a free energy advice service that provided over £880k in financial benefits to households across East Sussex between March 2025 and April 2026.
