The A2690 Combe Valley Way will close to traffic between 8pm and 6am on Wednesday 18 November and Thursday 19 November, ending at 6am on Friday 20 November, East Sussex Highways has said.

The works involve inspecting a number of bridges to ensure they remain safe for motorists, along with repairs to damaged safety barriers, the authority said.

Work will take place between the Haven Brook Avenue roundabout and Queensway, with the road closed to through traffic. A signed diversion is in place via the A2690, A21, A2102 and A259, East Sussex Highways said.

Access to properties in the works area will be limited and managed during working hours, and motorists may experience delays or be escorted by site marshals.
