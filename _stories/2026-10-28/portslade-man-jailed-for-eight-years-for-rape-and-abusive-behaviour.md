Paul Savage, 50, of Carlton Terrace, Portslade, was sentenced to eight years' imprisonment at Lewes Crown Court, a Sussex Police spokesperson said.

He was found guilty on 10 July of rape, controlling coercive behaviour, harassment and breach of a non-molestation order, and was sentenced when he appeared at court on 23 October, the spokesperson said.

He had been charged on 2 January 2026 with intentional strangulation, possession of Class A and C drugs, stalking involving fear of violence, breach of a non-molestation order and controlling coercive behaviour, and was later charged with rape on 18 February, according to Sussex Police.

Detective Constable Lisa Turner said the complainant "has shown incredible strength and courage to come forward", and that Savage continued his behaviour while on police bail and after a court order was imposed, until he was remanded into custody.
