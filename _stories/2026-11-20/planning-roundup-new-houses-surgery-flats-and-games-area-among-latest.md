Proposals for four new houses, the conversion of a former GP surgery into flats and a new outdoor sports facility are among the latest planning applications submitted to Hastings Borough Council, the Hastings Observer reports.

At 70 Hollington Old Lane, St Leonards, an application (HS/FA/26/00672) seeks permission to build four two-storey houses, each with off-road parking. The former Beaconsfield Surgery at 21 Beaconsfield Road, Hastings, would be converted into three self-contained flats (HS/FA/26/00660), with plans including a rear balcony for the lower ground floor flat, removal of the covered walkway along the side of the building and a new entrance door.

An outdoor multi-use games area is proposed at Clyde House, 258 Sedlescombe Road North (HS/FA/26/00651), and a loft conversion with roof dormers is sought for the second floor flat at 23 Grosvenor Crescent (HS/FA/26/00648).

Also in the list are retrospective permission for replacement windows at 55-55B Gensing Road (HS/FA/26/00616) and a single-storey rear extension at 293 Bexhill Road (HS/FA/26/00596).
