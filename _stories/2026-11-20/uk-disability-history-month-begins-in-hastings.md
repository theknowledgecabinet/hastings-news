UK Disability History Month has begun, running until Saturday 19 December, Hastings Borough Council has said. The month celebrates the achievements and contributions of disabled people and encourages reflection on how to create a more inclusive society.

This year's theme, Disability, Politics and Democracy, explores how disabled people have shaped society, the barriers that can limit participation, and the importance of ensuring everyone's voice is heard.

Councillor Solly Solamito, lead councillor for communities and equalities, said the UK is home to an estimated 16.8 million disabled people, yet up to 80% of disabilities are non-visible. Local activist, poet and writer Penny Pepper, who has contributed to Disability History Month projects since it began in 2010, said the movement's history is "long, contrary, extraordinary and largely untold".
