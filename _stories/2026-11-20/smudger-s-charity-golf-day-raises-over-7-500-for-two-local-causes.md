The 18th Smudger's Charity Golf Day has raised more than £7,500, taking the total raised since the charity launched to more than £110,000, the Hastings Observer reports.

The event was held at Cooden Beach Golf Club in Bexhill with a field of 96 players. Smudger's Legacy was set up in 2007 following the death of Steve 'Smudger' Smith, a local footballer who died in a motorbike accident aged 37, leaving a widow and three children all under six. He played mainly for Hastings United and made his senior debut aged 16.

This year's money was split between Dragon Flies Children's Bereavement Charity, which supported Steve's children after he died, and the Hastings RNLI, with a cheque for £3,750 handed to each.

Steve's brother Darren Smith said the day had been 'really well supported' and thanked the players, sponsors and Cooden Beach general manager John Hatt and his staff, who had a 'can do attitude'. Andy Thompson won the individual stableford competition.
