Hastings Borough Council has issued a call out to consultants and research teams to undertake a study of what the creative and cultural industries bring to the town's economy.

Councillor Julia Hilton, lead councillor for regeneration, shaping place and community wealth building, said the council wants "a baseline for the economic impact of our culture and creative industries", to identify the sector's distinctive strengths and growth potential, and to provide evidence to support investment, regeneration, funding bids and partnership opportunities.

Interested parties can contact culture@hastings.gov.uk. Proposals must be submitted by 5pm on Thursday 31 December.
