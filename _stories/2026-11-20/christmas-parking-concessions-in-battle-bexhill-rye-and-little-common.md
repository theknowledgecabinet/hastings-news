Rother District Council has published its Christmas parking concessions, with charges suspended in a selection of off-street car parks across Battle, Bexhill, Little Common and Rye from late November through December.

In Battle, the Lower Market, Upper Market and Mount Street car parks are free all day (8am to 7pm) on Saturday 28 November, Saturday 5 December and Saturday 12 December, and from 4pm to 7pm on Friday 11 December. In Bexhill, the De La Warr, Eversley Road, Manor Barn & Gardens, Town Hall, Wainwright Road and War Memorial car parks are free all day on Saturday 5 December and on Saturdays 12 and 19 December, and from 4pm to 7pm on Tuesday 8 December, with the De La Warr free from 8am to 10pm on each of those days.

The Little Common car park is free all day (8am to 7pm) on Saturday 5 December, Sunday 13 December and Saturday 19 December, and from 4pm to 7pm on Sunday 6 December. In Rye, the Cinque Ports Street, The Strand, Bedford Place, Lucknow Place and Gibbet Marsh car parks are free from 4pm to 7pm on Friday 4 December and all day on Saturdays 5, 12 and 19 December.
