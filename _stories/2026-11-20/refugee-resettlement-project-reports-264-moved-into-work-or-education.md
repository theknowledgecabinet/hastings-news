Hastings Borough Council's Resettlement Employability Project has supported more than 384 people since launching in 2023, with 264 participants moving into employment or education, the council said. That includes 56 people so far this year.

The project, part of the Government's Support into Work scheme, is delivering one-to-one support and workshops covering employment rights, interview skills, digital learning and workplace awareness across East Sussex. One of its recent workshops focused on employment rights and modern slavery awareness, the council said, noting that many clients have limited awareness of their rights, which can leave them vulnerable to exploitation.

Councillor Yunis Smith, the council's Migrant Champion, said: "Every job secured represents more than employment, it is a step towards independence, stability and a brighter future for both clients and their families."
