More than £20,000 of illegal tobacco has been seized from shops across East Sussex in a major operation to tackle money laundering.

East Sussex Trading Standards officers took part in Operation Machinize, the National Crime Agency coordinated project targeting the criminal use of cash-intensive high street businesses, joined by Sussex Police, Immigration Enforcement and East Sussex Fire and Rescue Service. Officers seized 23,160 cigarettes and 12.9 kilograms of hand-rolling tobacco, together with a substantial quantity of suspected counterfeit Nike and Adidas clothing, footwear and accessories, and illegal and counterfeit vaping products.

Searches were carried out in ten shops and two associated vehicles across Hastings, Uckfield, Crowborough, Seaford, Newhaven and Hailsham during three operational days, with specialist tobacco detection dogs used to help find hidden products.

Cllr Pete Morley, East Sussex County Council's lead member for economy, said the result showed their commitment to stopping the trade in illegal tobacco and counterfeit goods, and that it should send a warning to anyone else involved in selling such goods.

Anyone with information about illegal tobacco sales is asked to report it by calling the illegal tobacco helpline on 0300 999 6999, or online at #StopIllegalTobacco.
