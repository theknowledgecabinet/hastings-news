A Battle resident has won £100 worth of vouchers to spend at independent shops in the town after winning the first prize draw for Battle Solar Town's community solar survey.

Sasha, who will not be identified further, does not have solar panels but wanted to share her views on the technology. "The survey only took a few minutes, and winning the vouchers was a lovely surprise," she said.

Two more £100 voucher draws are still to come. Residents of Battle, Telham and Netherfield can complete the survey, which asks about their views and experiences of solar energy, and opt into the draw at battlesolartown.uk/survey.

Nicky Bishop, chair of Battle Solar Town, said: "We want to hear from the whole community, whatever people's views on solar." The not-for-profit group does not sell panels, and its vision is for at least 25 per cent of Battle's electricity to come from local solar generation. The prize vouchers are bought from local independent shops, keeping the money in the high street.
