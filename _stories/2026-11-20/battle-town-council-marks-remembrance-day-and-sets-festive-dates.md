Around 150 people gathered on the Battle Abbey Apron for the town's Remembrance Day ceremony, Battle Town Council said. Pupils from Battle & Langton CE Primary School, Claverham Community College and Netherfield CE Primary School gave readings, the Battle Abbey School Choir performed, and the Battle Bonfire Boyes fired the maroon to mark the two-minute silence.

Town Mayor Councillor Sue Burton said: "It was moving to see so many people come together to remember those who served and those who gave their lives."

The council's autumn and winter newsletter sets out the festive calendar: Santa's High Street Parade and the lights switch-on at 1.45pm and 5pm on Saturday 28 November, late-night shopping until 9pm on 3 and 11 December, and a Festive Market in Market Square from 4pm to 8pm on Friday 11 December. Santa will tour the town from 6pm on the evenings of 7 to 10 December.
