Residents are being invited to help look after three community micro woods across Rother during National Tree Week, which runs from 21 to 29 November.

The micro woods were planted by community volunteers with funding from the Coronation Living Heritage Fund to commemorate the coronation of King Charles III, and are intended to enhance local green spaces, boost biodiversity and create habitats for wildlife. Rother District Council is hosting volunteer tree care days, where people can help with hand weeding and spreading mulch around the young trees.

No gardening experience is needed, refreshments will be provided, and activities are subject to suitable weather. Volunteers are asked to wear suitable clothing and footwear, bring gloves and a shovel or hand shears if possible, and to travel sustainably where they can.

The days are at Bulverhythe Recreation Ground in Bexhill on Monday 23 November from 10am to 1pm; Mason Field, Mason Road, Rye (opposite Tilling Green Community Centre) on Wednesday 25 November from 10am to 2pm; and the Coronation Gardens Recreation Ground, Battle, on Friday 27 November from 10am to 2pm. Anyone wishing to take part should confirm by emailing elize.manning@rother.gov.uk.
