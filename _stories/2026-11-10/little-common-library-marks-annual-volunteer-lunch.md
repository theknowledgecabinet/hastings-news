Volunteers and supporters came together in Bexhill for Little Common Library's annual fish and chip lunch, its chairman Connor Winter said.

The gathering, held at Little Common Community Centre, was a chance to celebrate the work of the people who give their time to keep the library running, he said. "We are incredibly fortunate to have such a dedicated group of people who give their time to support our library and the wider community," Mr Winter said.

Steve Johnson, from Bexhill Heritage, delivered a presentation on the Brassey family and their connections to the town. The library is open on Tuesdays from 9.30am to 3.30pm, and on Fridays from 9.30am to 12.30pm.
