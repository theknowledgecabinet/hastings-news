Bexhill Leisure Centre has been named a finalist for Regional Centre of the Year at the UK Active Awards, one of the leisure industry's leading national award programmes.

The centre, operated by Freedom Leisure on behalf of Rother District Council, said the recognition comes as the council makes a significant investment in the facility's infrastructure to support its long-term future and help it continue providing leisure, health and wellbeing services for local residents.

Toby Reed, Freedom Leisure's area manager in Rother, said: "We're incredibly proud of the team at Bexhill Leisure Centre."
