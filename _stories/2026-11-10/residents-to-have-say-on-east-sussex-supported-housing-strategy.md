People in supported housing across the district can have their say on how it should improve, Rother District Council has said.

The Supported Housing Strategy for East Sussex is being developed by Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Rother, Wealden and East Sussex Councils. It will set out how the councils work with partners to improve supported housing and make sure it meets the needs of local people, the council said.

The proposed priorities include planning for the supported housing people will need, working with housing providers to continue improving quality, making sure housing is safe, suitable and well managed, and making sure residents receive the right support and have a say in the services they use.

The councils want to hear from supported housing residents, their families and carers, housing providers and staff working in health and care. The survey closes on 21 December 2026, on the East Sussex County Council website.
