No trains will run between Tonbridge and Wadhurst from Saturday 24 October until Sunday 1 November, as Network Rail carries out major work on the Hastings line. Rail replacement buses will operate throughout, and the closure extends to Battle on the final day.

Engineers will use the closure to renew worn slab track in Grove Hill Tunnel, just south of Tunbridge Wells, and to remove three temporary speed restrictions: two between Crowhurst and Battle and one between Etchingham and Robertsbridge. The restrictions were put in place after the record hot and dry summer left soil beneath the track drying out and shrinking.

Buses will also replace trains between Wadhurst and Hastings from 10.30pm on Saturday 31 October until 7am on Sunday 1 November. Scott Brightwell, director of safety and performance at Southeastern, said the work is an investment in the Hastings line and asked passengers to plan ahead and allow extra time for their journeys. Tickets from Hastings and Ore will be accepted through to Ashford International and onto London St Pancras highspeed services.
