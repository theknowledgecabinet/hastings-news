Desert rock voices from New Orleans are making a detour to the Hastings music scene. Bassist-vocalist Stoo Odom and Roger Kunkel, both members of the Californian desert rock band Thin White Rope, will play the Jenny Lind on Friday 20 November, according to the Hastings Online Times.

The pair, reunited as Odom-Kunkel, are touring the UK to release their new EP 'Sniffing Spirits', which marries desert noir guitar with recitation, low-toned bass and improvisation. They will share the stage with Stray Bentos, the Hastings-based evolving collective fronted by Jude Montague and Danny Brittain, on the venue's Latin-themed Friday evenings.

Asked why he has picked Hastings for the tour, Stoo said: "Y'all are real sweet. And it's a tiny version of my beloved San Francisco, but with foxes, slow worms, and jellied eels." The gig starts at 8pm and is free.
