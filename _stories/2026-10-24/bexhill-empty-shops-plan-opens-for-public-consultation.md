Rother District Council has asked residents and businesses to have their say on a plan to tackle long-term empty shops in Bexhill.

The council is consulting on a proposal to designate Bexhill town centre as an area where High Street Rental Auction powers could be used. The powers, introduced by government, are designed to help bring persistently vacant commercial properties back into use and support high street regeneration.

A recent audit of commercial premises in the town centre found 55 vacant units out of 439 properties surveyed, a vacancy rate of 12.5 per cent. The council said the figure is slightly below the national average, but the audit identified clusters of empty premises along London Road, Sackville Road, Sea Road and Devonshire Road. Bexhill is being considered as a pilot location for any future use of the scheme within the district.

Councillor Christine Bayliss, cabinet member for regeneration, said the council wanted to hear directly from residents, businesses, landlords and community partners before any further decisions were made. The council said Bexhill still benefits from a strong mix of independent businesses and hospitality venues, but the audit highlighted concerns around long-term vacant units and ageing buildings.
