A service and wreath laying took place at Rye Harbour Church on Sunday 15 November to remember the 17 crew members of the Mary Stanford lifeboat who lost their lives in 1928.

The men sailed out from Rye Harbour in an early morning gale on 15 November 1928 in response to a ship in distress, the SS Alice of Riga, which had been in a collision. By the time the lifeboat reached heavy seas, the ship's crew had already been rescued by another vessel, but the Mary Stanford could not be recalled and capsized.

Members of the RNLI Rye Harbour and Hastings crews attended the service, alongside the mayors of Rye and Winchelsea. In a symbolic gesture, 17 lifeboatmen each placed a single red rose on one of the 17 named tablets commemorating their predecessors.
