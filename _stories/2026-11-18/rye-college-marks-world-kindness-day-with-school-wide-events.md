Rye College marked World Kindness Day with a series of activities built around its "Be Kind" value, as part of the school's Values Celebration Days.

The day, organised by the Student Leadership Team, included a virtual assembly where each mentor group's Kindness Champion was presented with a certificate and a prize, and students were encouraged to write Kindness Notes to one another.

A "Kindness Surprise and Delight Patrol" ran at break and lunchtime, handing out spot prizes and delivering thank-you messages to staff. Students were awarded Kindness Tokens for acts they were spotted carrying out, which could be exchanged for a reward.

Dom Downes, headteacher of Rye College, which is part of the Aquinas CE Education Trust, said: "It was lovely to see our whole community noticing and recognising the small things that make Rye such a special place."
