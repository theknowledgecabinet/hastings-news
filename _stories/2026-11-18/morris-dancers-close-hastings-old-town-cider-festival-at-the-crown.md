Hastings' newest Morris dance side, The Molly Winklers, danced at the Crown pub in All Saints Street on Saturday as the final act of the Old Town Cider Festival.

Landlady Tess Swan thanked the group for "their amazing performance to close the Cider Festival", the Hastings Observer reported.

The week-long festival saw people sampling still and sparkling ciders at a number of Old Town pubs, including the Crown, the Jolly Fisherman and the Albion. Many of the ciders came from local producers such as Ascension and Nightingale.
