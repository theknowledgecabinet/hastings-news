Police have been visiting East Sussex College to talk to students about knife crime.

Neighbourhood Youth Officers visited the college's Station Plaza and Parker Road campuses this week as part of Operation Sceptre, and police say they had 'honest conversations' with students.

A force spokesperson said it is a common misconception that carrying a knife will keep you safe, but that in reality it can increase the risk of harm and have life-changing consequences.

Officers offered advice on what to do if concerned about a friend or peer carrying a knife, and shared information about the Knives Cost Lives campaign. They also highlighted Fearless, the youth service of Crimestoppers, through which young people can report information about crime, including knife crime, 100% anonymously.

Police say knife crime remains rare in East Sussex.
