Hastings Lions has donated more than £8,000 to local causes.

Officers and members decided on the grants at the group's monthly business meeting at the Holy Redeemer Church Hall in St Leonards-on-Sea.

The club will give £5,000 to the St Leonards Bowls Club to support its new building project, £1,000 to the Homelessness Unity Group, and £1,000 to St Paul's C of E Primary Academy towards a multi-sensory room for neurodiverse children.

A further £1,750 goes to community initiatives organised by local voluntary groups. Lord Brett McLean, the club's president, said Hastings Lions is part of an international humanitarian organisation, with the club providing support across the local area.
