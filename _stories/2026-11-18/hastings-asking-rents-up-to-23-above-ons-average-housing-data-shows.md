Median asking rents in Hastings are running well above the official average, with the gap on three-bedroom homes reaching 23%, housing website Dwellio says.

Taking its figures from six major rental sites on 11 November, Dwellio put the median asking rent for a three-bedroom house in the borough at £1,350 a month, against an Office for National Statistics average of £1,102. The gap is similar for smaller homes, around 17% to 22%, the report says.

The difference matters because Local Housing Allowance rates are set on ONS figures, and the Hastings rate has not been raised since 2024. The report found no two, three or four-bedroom homes in Hastings available at LHA rate, and just two one-bed properties.

Last month the leaders of Hastings, Eastbourne and Lewes councils wrote to the Minister for Homelessness, Democracy, Communities and Faith, calling for LHAs to be increased. Councils' research also reported 525 households presenting as homeless across the three areas in the three months to October, with 1,008 households in temporary accommodation including 1,041 children.
