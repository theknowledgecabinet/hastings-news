Hastings Borough Council has warned dog owners that fines are in force under an updated dog control order.

A new Public Space Protection Order (PSPO) now requires anyone walking a dog to have the means to clean up after it. The council said wardens on patrol may talk to dog owners, and not having the means to clear up could result in a £100 fine.

The order also covers where dogs can be on and off lead, including on the beach and in parks, and requires dogs to be on lead on the promenade at all times. It limits the number of dogs one person can take out to six.

The council has asked residents to report any dog fouling they see through My Hastings, so it can build a picture of problem areas.
