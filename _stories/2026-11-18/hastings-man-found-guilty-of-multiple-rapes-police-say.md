A Hastings man has been found guilty of five rapes and three other sexual offences, police said.

Jason Wren, 34, of Redmayne Drive, was convicted at Hove Crown Court on Thursday, 5 November. A police spokesperson said he had denied the five charges of rape and three charges of causing a person to engage in sexual activity without consent.

Police said Wren is already serving a 22-year sentence for the rape of a woman, and that the latest conviction relates to attacks carried out during a nine-month campaign of abuse on another woman. He is due to be sentenced on 11 December.

Investigating officer Zoe Ayres said: "We want all victims of sexual violence to know we will listen and we will support you."
