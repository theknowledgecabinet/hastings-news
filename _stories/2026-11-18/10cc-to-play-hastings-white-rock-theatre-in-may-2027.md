The 10cc will play a greatest hits show at the White Rock Theatre on Wednesday 20 May 2027, the band's booking for the town has been announced.

The concert is one of 10 new UK dates added to the extended And Another Bloody Greatest Hits Tour after high demand, according to The Argus. The band is led by co-founder Graham Gouldman, who was awarded an MBE in the 2025 King's Birthday Honours for services to music.

The setlist is expected to feature well-known songs including I'm Not In Love, The Wall Street Shuffle and Dreadlock Holiday. Mr Gouldman said the tour title was "a wry acknowledgement that we're out doing it again".

Tickets are on sale via Ticketline.
