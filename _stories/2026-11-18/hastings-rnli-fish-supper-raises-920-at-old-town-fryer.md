The annual Hastings RNLI fish supper has raised £920, with a sell-out crowd at the Old Town Fryer in Hastings Old Town.

The event, held on Wednesday, 11 November, saw the crew from the Hastings lifeboat station serve the meal, with a raffle, a quiz and live music from the Hastings Old Town Ukulele Group.

Paul Freathy, chair of the Hastings Lifeboat Management Group, said: "Our annual fish supper has become a really popular event, with tickets always selling out fast."

He thanked the Old Town Fryer for hosting the event and said the lifeboat group was "already looking forward to doing it all again next year."
