A collision involving multiple vehicles was reported on the A259 Little Common Road in Bexhill on Tuesday evening.

Traffic news was first reported at 5.24pm on Tuesday (17 November) that the road was blocked in both directions between West Down Road and Chandler Road, with traffic moving slowly in the area, according to the Sussex Express. The congestion appears to have eased since.

Sussex Police has been contacted for comment.
