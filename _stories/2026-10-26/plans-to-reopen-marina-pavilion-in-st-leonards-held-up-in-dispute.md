The reopening of the Marina Pavilion on the St Leonards seafront has been delayed amid a row over council repair demands.

Sea Change Sussex, the not-for-profit regeneration company, says it has received multiple bids from firms wanting to take over the landmark leisure venue. All the submissions depend on building works being carried out and require landlord consent from Hastings Borough Council, a Sea Change Sussex spokesman said. The company said the council has served notice demanding the property be refurbished within six months, in a manner that includes the completion of works not due until the lease ends in 2077.

Sea Change Sussex said it has also had issues with vandalism, electrical problems and flooding from overflowing drains connected to the adjoining public toilets. The site was put back on the market earlier this year after long-running power supply failures were repaired, following its closure in January 2024.

The company is now seeking urgent discussions with the council to find a way forward. Hastings Borough Council said it has not received any formal approach about the concerns, but added: "We are really keen to see Marina Pavilion open again, as we know visitors and residents are."
