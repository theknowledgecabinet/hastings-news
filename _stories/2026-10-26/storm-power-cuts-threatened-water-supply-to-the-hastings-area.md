Two water supply works serving the Hastings area shut down over the weekend after the storms knocked out local power, though taps kept running.

Works at Beauport in Hastings and Powdermill at Brede were taken offline after power interruptions, and a number of vulnerable residents on Southern Water's priority list had bottled water delivered to their homes on Sunday as a precaution.

A Southern Water spokesperson said: "Our teams responded to challenging power issues over a 48 hour period and we worked around the clock to keep taps flowing. All customers remained in supply as normal."
