The Red Lion, a 15th century pub in Denbigh Road, Hooe, near Battle, has been taken over by new management.

The pub announced the handover in a social media statement, saying it is open every lunchtime and dinner, Tuesday through Saturday.

According to tourism guide Experience Sussex, the building was originally constructed in 1495 and converted into a pub in the 1600s. It was once a haunt for smugglers and customs officers, and its attic housed a secret tobacco mill that is reputed to have inspired the saying "money for old rope", The Argus reported.

Customers said the reopening got off to a good start, with one guest praising the roast dinner on the pub's first day and another booking a return lunch after visiting on Saturday.
