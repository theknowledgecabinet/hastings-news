Ark Alexandra Academy's Combined Cadet Force had a triple win at the Lord-Lieutenant's Awards Ceremony, held at Lancing College and attended by senior military personnel and civic representatives from across the region.

Captain Nick Burchell was honoured with a Lord-Lieutenant's Award in recognition of his outstanding contribution to the cadet movement over many years. He has recently taken on the role of Chair of the Sussex Combined Cadet Force Leadership Group.

Senior cadet Pippa Styring was appointed the Lord-Lieutenant's Cadet for East Sussex CCF, receiving a certificate and a badge she is entitled to wear on her uniform throughout her appointment.

Rounding off the evening, the unit was awarded the Irlam Trophy for its outstanding support for Combat Stress, the veterans' mental health charity. The trophy is named in honour of Lieutenant Colonel Tom Irlam MBE. The ceremony was reported to the Hastings Online Times by Carol Sales, from Ark Alexandra Academy.
