East Sussex County Council is looking for a local group to take on the lease of Hollington Community Centre in Old Church Road, after saying the building needs significant investment to bring it back into good repair.

The centre opened in 1974 and is a registered charity. It is home to a table tennis club that has run there for 70 years, along with judo, short mat bowls, a social section and walking cricket. It has been listed as an asset of community value by Hastings Borough Council.

In a published report, the county council said the property is recognised as a community asset but requires significant capital investment, and that it cannot carry out the works itself or operates no council service from the building. It will run a bidding process to find a community or voluntary organisation able to take the lease and fund the repairs, the report said.

Local people attended a special meeting at the centre on 31 October to discuss its future. Cllr Will Chapman, a Hollington councillor, said the centre has served local people for decades and the town "cannot afford to lose such a valuable local asset."
