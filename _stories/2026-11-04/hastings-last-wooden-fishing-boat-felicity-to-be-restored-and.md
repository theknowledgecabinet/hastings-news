The last surviving wooden fishing boat from Hastings is to be restored and put on permanent display after a fund-raising campaign reached its target of £5,000. The 'Felicity', RX58, will be displayed on the piece of land opposite the Dolphin pub in Rock-a-Nore Road.

The clinker-built boat was constructed in 1980 by H J Phillips Boatbuilders in Rye and was originally named Our Pam and Peter, her construction once the subject of a BBC documentary. She was worked for many years by the brothers who own her, and was deregistered two years ago when she ceased work.

Peter Carney, who founded the Hastings Wooden Boats group and started the appeal, said the Old Hastings Preservation Society, the Fishermen's Museum and volunteers had worked together to save the boat. "Sincere thanks to everyone who contributed to reaching our target of £5,000 to save her," he said. A plan to turn her into an ornamental building for a private garden, which would have involved cutting her in half, had left her future in doubt.
