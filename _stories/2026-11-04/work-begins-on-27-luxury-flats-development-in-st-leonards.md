Work has started on a scheme of 27 flats in St Leonards. Scaffolding is going up and the first foundations are being built on a West Hill Road site, according to the Hastings Observer.

The plot was first put forward for development by MPG (St Leonards) Limited in 2014 and the proposal was approved by Hastings Borough Council in October 2025. The site, of about 0.7 acres, went on the market for £795,000 earlier this year.

A statement published when the land was marketed described it as a consented site offering "a rare chance to deliver a landmark residential scheme of 27 exclusive luxury flats", with no on-site affordable housing obligations.
