Storms Austen and Boelo have stripped away large sections of Pett Level's shingle beach defences, exposing for the first time in decades an old 1940s stepped concrete sea wall and a tarmac service road that had been buried beneath the shingle, according to the Hastings Independent Press.

The storms brought heavy rain and gusts of 60 to 70mph, and high tides stripped shingle along the stretch from Pett Level to Winchelsea Beach and Cliff End. Erosion has left a sheer five-foot drop near the grassy path on top of the sea wall where the beach used to be.

The speed of the beach's loss has triggered formal requests for emergency government intervention, not just to replace the shingle but to fund permanent defences. The beach and its surrounding land form part of the Dungeness, Romney Marsh and Rye Bay protected area.
