Hastings Borough Council has drawn up an Age Friendly Hastings Strategy and Action Plan, with proposals to make the town more accessible and inclusive for people aged 50 and over.

The plan will be considered by the council's cabinet on 9 November. If approved, it will guide work through to March 2028. The programme would receive £50,000 a year from Public Health, amounting to £150,000 over three years, with most of the money going to staffing.

The proposals include recruiting volunteers to check how accessible shops, pavements and public spaces are, improving community transport links, adding exercise classes aimed at preventing falls, and helping older people remain in their own homes.

The Argus reports that the proportion of Hastings residents aged 65 and over is projected to rise from 22 percent in 2026 to 28 percent by 2047, and that healthy life expectancy in Hastings is around five years below the East Sussex average.
