Self-storage specialist Cuboid Self Storage has opened its second site in Sussex, on Sedlescombe Road in St Leonards-on-Sea. The facility opened on 1 November and is the company's 44th site nationwide, joining its existing facility in Shoreham.

Tim Knowles, founder and managing director of FI Property Group, said Cuboid is the largest self-storage container business in the UK by capacity, and that demand for high-quality, secure and flexible storage facilities has continued to grow.

The site offers 20ft containers providing more than 1,100 cubic feet of secure space, gated access seven days a week from 6am to midnight, 24-hour CCTV monitoring and heavy-duty lock and key security.
