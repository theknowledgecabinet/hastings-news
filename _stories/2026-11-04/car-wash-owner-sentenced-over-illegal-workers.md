A car wash owner in Hastings has been sentenced after illegal workers were repeatedly found at his business, Sussex Police said.

In October 2025, Sussex Police's Discovery team and partner agencies attended the business, operated by Ermir Halilaj, and identified a worker who, due to their immigration status, had no legal right to work in the UK. A second worker was identified at the business on a subsequent visit. Both workers were handed to immigration services.

Halilaj, 38, of no fixed address, pleaded guilty at Hastings Magistrates' Court on 19 August to two counts of employing a person with cause to believe they were disqualified from employment by reason of their immigration status.

On 22 October he was sentenced to six months' imprisonment, suspended for 12 months, and ordered to engage with the Probation Service and complete 100 hours of unpaid work. PC Jon Tree, from the Discovery Team, said: "Employers have a responsibility to carry out appropriate checks to ensure those working for them have the legal right to do so."
