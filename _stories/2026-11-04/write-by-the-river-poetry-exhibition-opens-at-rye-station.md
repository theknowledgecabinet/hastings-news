A new poetry and artwork exhibition called 'Write by the River' has opened at Rye railway station. It features poems and illustrations about the area's waterways, including the River Rother and River Tillingham.

Posters printed by Southern Railway are displayed in the station's ticket office waiting area, according to the Hastings Observer. The project was facilitated by StrongBack productions and supported by the Marshlink community rail partnership, which runs along the line between Hastings and Ashford International.

Writer Morgan Greenhalf said the idea was to bring together people who had never written before as well as those with experience, and introduce them to the river.
