A 27-year-old from St Leonards has won the women's title at the Christmas tree flinging contest at Catsfield Christmas Tree Farm near Battle.

Becky Palmer, 27, reclaimed the women's crown with a throw of 7.88m, ahead of Louise Thompson, 36, also of St Leonards, who threw 5.65m. The men's title went to James Gardiner, 37, of Hailsham, with a throw of 10.45m, which bettered his own winning distance of 9.1m from last year.

Farm owner Clive Collins said the contest, which began in 2020, has become "a much-loved part of our Christmas build-up". Adults had to hurl a six-foot Norway spruce, while smaller trees were used in the under-10 and under-16 categories.

Both champions won £150 and a tree-shaped wooden trophy.
