Swansea Sound, who feature members of Heavenly and Talulah Gosh, are to kick off their tour in St Leonards at The Piper on 13 November.

The show comes on the back of the band's new album Pre-Post-Truth, their third record. A spokesman described it as 'a collection of twelve new punk and power-pop tunes with a lot more lyrical bite than you get from the average indie band'.

The line-up includes Hue Williams (ex-The Pooh Sticks), Amelia Fletcher and Rob Pursey (Heavenly), Bob Collins (The Dentists) and Ian Button (Death In Vegas, Railcard). The band has recorded sessions for 6Music, Radio X and WFMU in New York, and has just completed a tour of the US.
