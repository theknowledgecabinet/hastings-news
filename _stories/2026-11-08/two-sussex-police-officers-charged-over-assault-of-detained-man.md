Two Sussex Police officers have been charged with assault occasioning actual bodily harm over an incident involving a man detained in hospital under the Mental Health Act, according to the Independent Office for Police Conduct (IOPC).

PC Richard Harris, 36, and PC Lorenzo Fenu, 28, are to appear in court on 10 November, jointly charged with the offence. The IOPC said the charge relates to an incident on 16 April 2026, when the officers allegedly used unlawful force on a man who had been detained in hospital under the Mental Health Act, and injured him.

The watchdog said it began its investigation following a mandatory referral from the force. At the end of its investigation in September, it referred a file of evidence to the Crown Prosecution Service, which authorised the charges.
