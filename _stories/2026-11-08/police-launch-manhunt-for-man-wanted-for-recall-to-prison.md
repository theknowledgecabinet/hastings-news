Police have launched a manhunt for a man wanted for recall to prison and are asking anyone who sees him to call 999.

Sussex Police issued an urgent public appeal on Sunday evening, naming the man as Gurvinder Singh, 39. The appeal says he is "wanted for recall to prison for breaching the terms of his licence" and is currently serving a sentence for robbery, and that he has links to Brighton and Crawley.

Anyone who sees him is being asked not to approach him and to call 999 immediately, quoting crime reference 47260234461, the force said.

The appeal was posted on Sussex Police's Facebook page and is aimed at people across Sussex, including Hastings and the Rother valley.
