Hastings United suffered a heavy defeat on a cold Saturday afternoon at The Pilot Field, where Steyning Town produced a second-half comeback to win 4-1 in the Isthmian South East division in front of a crowd of 1,034.

Hastings had gone into the break with the advantage. A corner in the 20th minute caused chaos in the Steyning penalty area and the ball went in off their defender Conrad Honore for an own goal. The visitors, though, came out determined after the break. Alex Brewer headed home to draw level just four minutes in, struck again a couple of minutes later to put Steyning 2-1 ahead, and Charlie Pitcher made it 3-1 on the hour mark.

Jesse Sentamu rounded off the visitors' performance in the 90th minute, slotting past Harry Haines to make it 4-1. Hastings' new signing Tosin Dada, whose arrival from Billingshurst was announced late on Friday night, made his away debut from the bench.

The victory lifts Steyning to 13th in the league, while Hastings drop to fourth. Both sides return to league action on Saturday: Steyning host Punjab and Hastings travel to Broadbridge Heath.
