Police have appealed for help to identify a man found dead on Rottingdean beach in October, who is still unknown.

Sussex Police said emergency services responded to welfare concerns for a man who had been seen at the water's edge on Rottingdean beach at about 9.40am on Sunday, 11 October. He was pronounced deceased at the scene, and the force said it is continuing to try to identify him and his next of kin.

The man is described as being in his late 50s or early 60s and of a slim build, with a number of tattoos. Police have now released images of the tattoos in an updated appeal, asking anyone who recognises them to come forward.

An inquest was opened and adjourned at West Sussex, Brighton and Hove Coroner's Court in Horsham on Tuesday, 10 November, with a forensic pathologist giving the provisional cause of death as drowning, the Brighton and Hove News reported.
