Hastings MP Helena Dollimore has said the town will receive £1.27 million to upgrade its playgrounds.

Ms Dollimore said the funding was secured through the Labour government's Pride in Place programme, and that the Labour group on Hastings Borough Council had also pushed for a further £280,000 to support the work.

She said she had audited the playgrounds in the constituency and found that eight had closed since 2014, and that more than half in Hastings, Rye and the villages needed upgrading. She is now asking children and parents to send their ideas for the new designs, saying many of the play spaces had fallen into disrepair.
