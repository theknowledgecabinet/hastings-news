A well-loved Bexhill seafront kiosk owner has died, her family has announced.

Stella Brennan-Wright, who ran the Old Bathing Station Kiosk in De La Warr Parade, has died aged 79, the family said. A notice on the kiosk said she passed away peacefully on Wednesday, 11 November 2026, surrounded by her family.

Stella bought the kiosk in 2002 and over the years held fundraisers for local charities, including St Michael's Hospice and Macmillan Cancer Support. In a notice on the kiosk, her family thanked people for their kindness, love and support at this difficult time.
