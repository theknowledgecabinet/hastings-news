Bexhill's Christmas lights switch-on and festive market will take place on Saturday, December 5, from 3pm to 7pm in Western Road and Devonshire Square.

Bexhill Lions Club is organising the event in partnership with Bexhill Town Council. Businesses, traders, charities and community groups are invited to showcase their products, with stall fees of £10.

Organisers said they welcome a wide variety of stalls, including crafts, artwork and photography, seasonal gifts and decorations, jewellery, books, pet supplies, and children's toys and clothes. Last year the market hosted 28 traders, along with food stalls and live entertainment.

Market organiser Brian Comber said: "The market plays an important role for the community by creating a festive atmosphere and giving everybody the opportunity to browse and purchase local and artisan products, all in a traffic-free environment." All proceeds will support good causes and community activities, organisers said.

Little Common is also holding a Christmas market and lights switch-on on Sunday, December 6, from 3.30pm, with the lights going on at 5.30pm.
