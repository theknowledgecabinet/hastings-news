Children taking part in an outdoor rewilding scheme based in Hastings have described the sessions as helping them to feel calmer, according to the BBC.

Project Rewild, which receives support from BBC Children in Need, offered children the chance to connect with the natural world during the half-term break. Founder and director Luke said: "It's about understanding and remembering you are part of nature. Without that, they won't be able to grow up and protect and care about it."

The Children in Need funding was "invaluable", he added, because it meant the sessions were free and there were no obstacles to attending. One child, George, said: "I like coming, getting out the house and stop looking at screens and the Xbox." Another, Sophia, said the sessions made her "go outside more often" and climb trees she wouldn't have done before.

Daniel said: "For me it's an escape from everything."
