James Charles, 49, of Roman Crescent, Southwick, has been jailed for 12 years after running a major fraud operation through his companies, Sussex Police said.

At Southwark Crown Court on Thursday, November 12, Charles was sentenced for fraud linked to Olympia Mayfair, which posed as a specialist reseller and repairer of high-value jewellery, watches and precious metals, and to National Gold Buyers, set up after his first arrest. Police said that between August 2022 and 2024, "scammed numerous victims out of over £1.2 million", and that in total, including offences connected to two further companies, Kingsley & Co Private Trader Ltd and Richmond & Sterling Ltd, the losses exceeded £2 million.

Police said Charles arranged meetings with victims, often at their homes, where he persuaded them to hand over their items in return for reimbursement once they were sold. Many received only a fraction of the promised sum, or none at all. He was first arrested in February 2024 and again in August 2024, and pleaded guilty to four counts of fraudulent trading.

Detective Constable Graham Gray, the investigating officer, said: "The scale of harm Charles has caused to numerous victims cannot be understated."
