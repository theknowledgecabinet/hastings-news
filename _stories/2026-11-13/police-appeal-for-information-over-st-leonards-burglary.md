Sussex Police are appealing for information in connection with a burglary in Eversfield Place, St Leonards.

The force posted the appeal on the Hastings Police Facebook page on Thursday, 12 November, saying it would like to talk with a man in connection with the offence.

The burglary took place on the morning of 3 October, when laptops, money, a gym bag, binoculars and other personal items were stolen from an address in Eversfield Place, police said.

Officers said they have made extensive enquiries and are now appealing to the public if they recognise the man, or if they have any information that could help the investigation.
