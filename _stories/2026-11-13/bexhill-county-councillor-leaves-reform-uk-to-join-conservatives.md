A Bexhill county councillor has left Reform UK and joined the Conservative Party, the party's East Sussex group has said.

Cllr Donald Walmsley, who represents Bexhill North, said he could no longer defend the direction the party was taking and that the move was a matter of conscience, the group said. He said his decision was partly prompted by what he described as an inadequate response from Reform leadership to undercover ITV reporting in October about some councillors and candidates using racist language.

The defection brings the number of Conservative councillors at East Sussex County Council to four, with 20 Reform, 10 Greens, 14 Liberal Democrats and two independents making up the rest, the group said. Cllr Paul Redstone, who leads the county council's Conservative group, said he welcomed the move.
