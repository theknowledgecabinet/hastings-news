Hastings White Rock is offering the chance to delve into the lives of Britain's most notorious gangsters with the Krays' official biographer, Fred Dinenage.

Mr Dinenage, whose book The Krays: Ronnie, Reggie and Me is described by the venue as a definitive insight, will be at the venue on Tuesday, 24 November.

For decades the twins were feared as the ultimate gang lords of the London underworld, but their reign came to an end in 1969 when both were given life sentences at the Old Bailey. Reggie went on to serve decades in some of the toughest prisons in the country, while Ronnie became a patient at Broadmoor, the venue said.

Mr Dinenage became the twins' official biographer around 30 years ago after being invited to meet Ronnie at Broadmoor on the Isle of Wight. "We're talking 30 years ago but amazingly the book still sells to this day," he said.
