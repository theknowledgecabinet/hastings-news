Michael Pyke, 79, of Aldwick, has been jailed for 15 years for the murder of his wife, Sussex Police have confirmed.

Emergency services attended the couple's home in Langley Grove, Aldwick, on 31 May after welfare concerns were raised for Beryl Pyke, 84, the force said. Michael Pyke had reported to police after admitting he had stabbed and killed his wife, police said. Officers found Beryl had died with serious injuries to her head and chest, and had a total of 11 puncture wounds.

He was charged with her murder in June and pleaded guilty at Brighton Crown Court in October. At Hove Crown Court on Friday, 13 November, he was sentenced to 15 years, police said.
