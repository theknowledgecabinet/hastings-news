Glenleigh Park Primary Academy in Bexhill hosted a special assembly to celebrate student leadership, with the Mayor of Bexhill, Cllr Memish Huseyin, in attendance. The visit marked the appointment of the school's newly elected school and eco councillors, following an election campaign that involved all pupils.

The mayor spoke with the new school councillors about leadership and his own journey to becoming mayor, while the children asked questions about the role. He praised the pupils' enthusiasm for democracy and citizenship.

Headteacher Libby Barnard said the school council elections give pupils the chance to develop leadership skills and understand democratic processes.

The school also marked its results for the 2025/26 year, with 80% of Key Stage 2 pupils reaching the combined reading, writing and maths expected standard, which puts it in the top 10% nationally.
