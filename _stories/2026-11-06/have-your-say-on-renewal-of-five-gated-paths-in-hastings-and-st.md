Hastings Borough Council is asking for views on plans to renew gating orders for five public rights of way in Hastings and St Leonards.

The gates were introduced in March 2024 to help reduce antisocial behaviour and improve safety in these areas, and government guidance requires gating orders to be reviewed every three years to ensure they continue to deter it.

The five locations are Valentine's Passage, linking Shepherd Street with North Street in St Leonards; Laser Lane, linking Kings Road to Cross Street in St Leonards; Prospect Passage, from Havelock Road to Priory Square in Hastings; Trinity Passage, linking Robertson Street with Claremont; and Hoads Wood Road to Pilot Road.

A council spokesperson said the proposed renewal was "based on considerable evidence".
