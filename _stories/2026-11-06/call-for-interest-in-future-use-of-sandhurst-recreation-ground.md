Hastings Borough Council is asking sports clubs, groups and organisations to register their interest in the future of Sandhurst Recreation Ground. The council wants to know how much demand there is for the sports pitches, which organisations would like to use the site, and whether it could be managed or developed by the community.

The site, traditionally used for football and cricket, is expected to be considered for all sports, and the council is working on a longer-term strategy covering the next three to ten years.

Cllr Glenn Haffenden, the council leader and lead councillor for sport and leisure, said the council wants to bring the pitches back into regular use to provide better sports and leisure opportunities for Hastings residents.

Groups wanting to visit the site can arrange a visit by contacting the council, and full details are on the council's website.
