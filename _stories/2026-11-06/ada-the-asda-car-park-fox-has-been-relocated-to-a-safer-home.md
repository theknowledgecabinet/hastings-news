Ada, the friendly fox that made her home in an Asda car park in Hastings, has been moved to a safer place by a wildlife charity. The fox had become well known for approaching shoppers, begging for food and trying to climb up people's legs.

While popular with many locals, her complete lack of fear of humans worried those who cared about her welfare. A vehicle was deliberately driven at her on one occasion, and as the weather turned she grew bolder.

Wildlife Matters caught her and took her to the Mallydams RSPCA centre at Hastings, where she was checked over. She showed no signs of illness, but when she was released in another area she made her way back to the car park.

With the help of the Fox Angels Foundation, a new home was found. After an earlier capture attempt failed, she was finally contained in a moment of distraction while being fed by a couple, and taken to her new home.
