Helena Dollimore, the MP for Hastings, Rye and the villages, has joined veterans and volunteers at the launch of this year's Hastings & St Leonards Poppy Appeal, held in Tesco, raising money for veterans and their families.

The launch carried a poignant note. Local veteran Bryan Hunter was due to receive an award in recognition of 35 years of dedicated fundraising, having staffed the Tesco stall every year during the poppy appeal period without a single day off. He died just days before the launch.

Last year the Hastings & St Leonards Poppy Appeal raised £66,808 for the Royal British Legion.

Ms Dollimore said: "It was an honour to join the launch of the Hastings and St Leonards Poppy Appeal today and pay tribute to all the local veterans and volunteers who make it happen each year." She added that the launch was a moment to remember Bryan, whom she described as a stalwart of the community who dedicated himself to the appeal every year and who would be so missed.
