Hastings artist Bill Greenhead, known for his cartoon-style paintings of chip-stealing seagulls, has an exhibition in the Snug bar of the Crown in Hastings Old Town.

Bill creates seagull-themed versions of masterpieces such as the Mona Lisa, and his paintings include versions of Admiral Nelson and of David Bowie as Aladdin Sane, transformed into gulls. His 'Gull Culture' series has proved hugely popular, with the paintings invariably including stolen chips.

He moved to Hastings in the early 2020s and began oil painting after receiving an online course voucher as a birthday gift during lockdown. He has exhibited during the Coastal Currents festival and his work featured in an installation at Hastings railway station.

The exhibition can be viewed until 26 November.
