More than £1.5m to improve playgrounds across the town is set to be considered at the council's Cabinet meeting on Monday evening.

The funding comes from the government's Pride in Place Impact Fund, the Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government's Playground Fund, and the council's own budget, which was committed as part of its 2026-27 budget process.

Council leader Cllr Glenn Haffenden said residents had made clear that improving play provision was "really important". Cllr Jo Walker, lead councillor for Climate, Nature and Green Spaces, added that the council is working with housing associations to improve play areas on their land and in their developments.

Following Cabinet approval, work will begin to appoint a contractor and to confirm which playgrounds, alongside Alexandra Park as a destination playground, will benefit from investment.
