Proposals to build up to 125 homes on the Hodson's Mill site in Robertsbridge are set to go before Rother District Council's planning committee on Thursday, 12 November. The plans come from Homes England, the government's housing and regeneration agency.

Part of the development would come from converting the former mill building and redeveloping the Grade II listed oast house on the site. The mill building would be turned into eight two-bed flats, a one-bed flat and two retail or community units, while the oast house would be dismantled and rebuilt as two three-bedroom homes.

The largely brownfield site, vacant since 2001, has long been allocated for development. Homes England took it over in March 2024 after earlier plans were deemed undeliverable.

The current proposal extends into green field land at the north of the site, and council planning officers are recommending approval. A financial viability appraisal says the development could not support affordable housing, and officers say it has been independently scrutinised.
