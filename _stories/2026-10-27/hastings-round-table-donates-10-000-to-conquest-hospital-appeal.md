Hastings Round Table has given £10,000 to the Friends of Conquest Hospital's 2026 Equipment Appeal, one of the largest grants the Round Table has made in recent years.

The donation covers almost a tenth of the £101,000 the Friends are raising for medical equipment at the hospital. The money comes from the proceeds of the Hastings Beer and Music Festival, held at The Oval in July and run by Round Table members with more than 80 volunteers. The festival has raised money for local organisations since 1982 and has now raised more than £1.5million for local charities.

The appeal pays for equipment that sits outside standard NHS budgets, including 12 special care cots for the Special Care Baby Unit, 30 Holter cardiac monitors that let patients have heart tests at home, and a maternity resuscitator for a unit where around 400 babies are born every year.

Jo Clark, general manager at the Friends, said: "Every pound we raise stays at the Conquest, and this grant will help us buy equipment that makes a real difference to patients and the staff caring for them." Jamie Neal of the Round Table said: "Almost everyone in Hastings has a connection to the Conquest, so it was an easy decision to back this appeal."
