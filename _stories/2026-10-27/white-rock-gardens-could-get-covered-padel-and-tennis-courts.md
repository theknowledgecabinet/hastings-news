Hastings Borough Council says it is being considered to receive £1.3million from the Lawn Tennis Association to develop a covered tennis and padel centre at White Rock Gardens.

The council said the funding would be made up of £1million from the Department for Culture, Media and Sport via Sport England, with £300,000 delivered through the LTA's Tennis Foundation. Alongside the LTA money, the council would provide £500,000 for a scheme creating three covered tennis courts and three covered padel courts.

Cllr Glenn Haffenden, leader of the council and lead councillor for sport and leisure, said: "The success of the pop-up padel courts at White Rock Gardens over the summer has shown that there is a demand for padel in Hastings, and this funding would allow us to bring covered courts that can be used all year round to help keep Hastings' residents active."
