Hastings Borough Council has approved plans for 20 flats, six holiday lets and four commercial units on the site of the former amusement arcade in George Street, Old Town, which was destroyed in a fire in January 2023.

The development, by Oasis Developments Hastings Ltd at 39 to 41 George Street, was granted approval on 22 October, with construction required to start within two years.

The site has long been a sore point for local people. Residents have described it as an eyesore and raised safety concerns about an unstable wall and hoardings that have blown down in high winds. In March the council said it would take enforcement action over the state of the site, issuing a Section 215 notice.

The three-storey building was previously an amusement arcade and nightclub. Sussex Police said the fire, tackled through the night by firefighters with 10 pumps, was treated as a deliberate ignition. Demolition was completed in August 2023.
