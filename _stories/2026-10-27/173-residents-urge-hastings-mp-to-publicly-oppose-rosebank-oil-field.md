A letter signed by 173 residents of the Hastings and Rye constituency has called on local MP Helena Dollimore to "get off the fence and publicly oppose all new oil and gas projects in the North Sea".

The letter, reported by the Hastings Online Times, was sent after the government approved the Jackdaw gasfield in the North Sea on Wednesday (21 October). It notes that the MP has not joined the 68 MPs who have signed the #StopRosebank pledge, which calls for opposition to the Rosebank oil field and a properly funded just transition for oil and gas workers and communities.

The letter also quotes UN Secretary General Antonio Guterres: "Every new fossil fuel project approved today makes tomorrow's heatwaves more dangerous, and our world less secure".

Hastings climate campaigner Jane Ripley, who signed the letter, said: "The UK should be a world leader on cheap, clean energy. What sort of message does it send to the other countries we are asking to decarbonise if we renege on the spirit of manifesto pledges at the first opportunity?"

The Hastings Online Times reports that the government has conceded the Jackdaw decision will have little or no impact on people's energy bills and will create only 27 jobs.
