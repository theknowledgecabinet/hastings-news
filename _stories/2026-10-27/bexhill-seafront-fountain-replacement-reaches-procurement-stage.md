A fountain is to return to the front lawns of the De La Warr Pavilion, with the replacement project now at the procurement stage.

In a joint statement, the Bexhill Neighbourhood Board, Bexhill-on-Sea Town Council and Rother District Council said the partnership has moved the scheme forward to identifying a contractor. The fountain was the top priority in community engagement carried out by the Neighbourhood Board and forms part of its four-year Regeneration Investment Plan, funded through the government's Pride in Place programme.

Rother District Council, as landowner and accountable body for the funding, will lead the procurement, while Bexhill-on-Sea Town Council has agreed in principle to take on future maintenance, subject to lease and construction quality arrangements. Repair options for the old fountain were explored, but replacement was identified as the most viable long-term solution.

Abi Newbury, chair of the Bexhill Neighbourhood Board, said: "There is still work to do before delivery can begin, but reaching the procurement stage is a genuinely positive step for Bexhill."
