Southern Water is coming to the town to hear from residents directly, with a drop-in at The Stade Hall on Wednesday 4 November, from 1.30pm to 6pm.

Senior leaders and teams from across the business will be there, the company said. The event will cover what is being done to prevent blockages, how the company is fixing misconnected sewer pipes that cause pollution, and what financial support is available for people struggling to pay bills.

Southern Water will also explain the solutions being introduced to reduce storm overflow releases into local rivers and seas.

A spokesperson for Southern Water said the events are held in communities throughout the South East so customers can meet the team and "learn more about the challenges we face together", and that each one is a chance to ask questions and find out more about how the company is improving its performance.
