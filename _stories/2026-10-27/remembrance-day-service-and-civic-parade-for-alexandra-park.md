Hastings' annual Act of Remembrance will take place at the war memorial in Alexandra Park at 11am on Sunday 8 November.

The Civic Parade, with organisations and uniformed groups, will make its way from the Town Hall, along Queens Road, towards the park, the Hastings Observer reported. At the park there will be a service, wreath laying and a march past, then the parade will re-form and make its way back to the Town Hall.

The Veterans Hub, at 45 Robertson Street, will be open to all for a social gathering following the event. People are asked to dress for the weather, as the ground in Alexandra Park can get muddy and slippery in bad weather, and the lights in Bottle Alley will be red in honour of Remembrance Day.
