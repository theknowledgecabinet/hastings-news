The Hastings Winkle Club has paid tribute to Mike Trigg, a committee member for more than 30 years who died on 6 October.

The club, founded by Old Town fishermen to fund a Christmas party for their children, says that tradition still survives today, and that Mike was a regular sponsor of it and of many other Winkle Club fundraising events. He was awarded the club's Silver Winkle for distinguished service and made a vice president two years ago.

President Richard Stevens said: "Mike's passing was tragic and happened so suddenly that it has left our membership in complete shock. I have also heard from many members of our Old Town community who were equally stunned to hear the sad news. All our thoughts are with Mike's family and those closest to him."

The Winkle Club will be represented at any future thanksgiving for Mike's life and will dedicate a plaque to him.

His daughter Melinda, who says he was "a kind, supportive and caring person who touched the lives of so many", invites friends and family to a gathering to remember him at 2pm on 7 November at the Hastings and St Leonards Angling Association, Marine Parade.
