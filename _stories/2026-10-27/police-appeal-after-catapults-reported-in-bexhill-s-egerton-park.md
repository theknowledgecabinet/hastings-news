Police are appealing for information after reports of animals being targeted with catapults in Egerton Park, Bexhill-on-Sea.

Sussex Police said incidents of catapult use in the park had been reported by staff at Rother District Council and that there are concerns wildlife may have been targeted. A force statement posted online asked anyone who witnesses a catapult being used to target wildlife, or who has information about incidents in the park, to report it at the time so officers can attend and take action.

Neighbourhood officers have increased daytime and evening patrols in and around the park, the force said. In the UK, catapults are not illegal to own or carry in public, but using them to harm people, animals or property, or for anti-social behaviour, can be criminal.

These reports are not isolated. Last month, 25 birds were found dead at Chichester railway station following a suspected catapult attack, and similar incidents raised concerns in Hailsham earlier this year, the BBC said.
