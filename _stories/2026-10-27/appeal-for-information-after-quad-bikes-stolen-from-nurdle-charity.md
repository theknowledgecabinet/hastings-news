Beach cleaning charity Nurdle is appealing for information after three quad bikes and specialist equipment were stolen from secure shipping containers in the early hours of Sunday 25 October.

Rother District Council has shared the charity's appeal, saying the offenders cut their way into the containers with heavy-duty power cutting tools at about 3:00am. The stolen equipment included two brand-new Honda quad bikes donated to Nurdle by Honda, as well as the charity's own operational quad bike, along with specialist tools.

Police are actively investigating and have reviewed extensive local CCTV footage, which shows the offenders leaving in a westerly direction towards Rye, the council said.

The theft has severely impacted Nurdle's environmental work. The charity has been helping Southern Water respond to the ongoing bio-bead pollution affecting Camber and the surrounding coastline.

Anyone with information, or who has been offered any of the stolen equipment for sale, is urged to contact Sussex Police or the Rother District Council Coastal Office.
