The improvement works around Hastings railway station have been completed, East Sussex County Council has said.

The project included new and relocated crossings, accessible kerbs, resurfaced roads and pavements, upgraded street lighting and targeted drainage improvements, the council said.

New Puffin crossings and upgraded traffic signals have created safer, wider crossing points for residents and rail passengers, and high-friction surfacing has been installed on steeper sections and sharper bends to improve safety for drivers and cyclists.

Cllr Peter Griffiths, the council's lead member for transport and environment, marked the completion of the works, the council said.
