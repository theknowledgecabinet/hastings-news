Police are appealing to the public to help find a missing 15-year-old boy who was last seen in St Leonards on Saturday (31 October).

Orion, who was reported missing from Ninfield, was seen at 11.10am in the Crouch Lane area, a police spokesperson said.

He was seen in the afternoon leaving St Leonards Warrior Square railway station, and a released image shows him at Hastings seafront. He has links to Bexhill and Eastbourne, but police say he may have travelled to the Ashford or Romney Marsh areas of Kent.

He is described as slim, with short dark hair, and was last seen wearing a burgundy hooded top, blue jeans and dark grey trainers. Police said they were concerned for his welfare.

Anyone with information, or who has seen him, is urged to call 999 and quote Operation Shortgate.
