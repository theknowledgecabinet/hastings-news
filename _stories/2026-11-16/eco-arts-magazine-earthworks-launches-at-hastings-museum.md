A new eco-arts magazine is launching in Hastings on the same day the town's museum opens its new environmentally themed exhibition.

Earthworks, a one-off international arts publication founded by poet Patricia Scanlan, holds its free launch event at Hastings Museum on Saturday 21 November, the same day the museum opens "Fixing our Broken Planet - Material Worlds", a new environmentally focused exhibition.

The day starts with a family-friendly clay workshop from 11am to 1pm, run by Radiator Arts artists Peter Quinnell and Rebecca Child, before a panel discussion from 2pm to 3.30pm. The panel includes novelist Iain Sinclair, a local resident; Kate MacGarry, a founding member of the Gallery Climate Coalition; Polly Gifford; actor and director Gopala Davies; and artist Mark Lawson Bell, with the conversation curated by Scanlan.

The magazine is not-for-profit and says all artists are paid equally for their contribution. Submissions are due by the end of 2026, with the finished magazine launching in February, March and April 2027.
