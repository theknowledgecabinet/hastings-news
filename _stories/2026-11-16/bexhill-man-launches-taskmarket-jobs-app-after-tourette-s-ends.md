Luke Jones, 38, from Bexhill, spent more than ten years filming and photographing weddings through his company, Bexhill Film Company. Then in February 2024, at the age of 35, he was diagnosed with Tourette's syndrome, and a job where he had to be in the room all day became impossible, he said.

He closed the business and built Taskmarket, an app that went live in October. People post a job around the house for free, from cleaning and oven cleans to gardening, handyman work, flat-pack and removals. Local tradespeople and cleaners then send their prices, and the customer picks who they like. The customer's money is held safely until the job is done, and only then is the tradesperson paid, Mr Jones said.

The first job, an oven clean in early November, was booked by a customer he had never met, he said, and both sides left five-star reviews. He registered the company, Taskmarket App Ltd, the same month, and tradespeople from Bexhill, Hastings and Eastbourne have been signing up since. Posting a job and joining as a tradesperson are both free, and while it started in the three towns, anyone in the UK can use it.
