Sidley Medical Practice, the Bexhill GP surgery on Turkey Road and Albert Road, is closed today. The practice said on its website that "due to some unforeseen circumstances we are unable to operate from our building today".

It said it would contact patients with appointments today to offer to see them at another location or change the appointment to an online one, and that its telephone lines remain open. Patients requiring urgent medical help should call NHS 111 or 999, the practice said.

A spokesperson for NHS Surrey and Sussex said the integrated care board was working urgently with Operose Health, which has been responsible for the practice under interim arrangements since October 2025, to minimise disruption and would provide further information as soon as possible. The current arrangements follow Care Quality Commission action in April 2025, when the registration of the previous provider, Dr Asare and Partners, was suspended over concerns about patient care and leadership, and was subsequently permanently cancelled, the spokesperson said.
