Royal Navy aircraft carrier HMS Prince of Wales passed the Hastings coast on the morning of Monday 16 November, according to local photographer Marie Richardson, who captured the picture.

She said helicopters, possibly Merlin or Wildcat, could clearly be seen on the ship's deck. "It was probably on its way to Portsmouth," she said.

HMS Prince of Wales is the second Queen Elizabeth-class aircraft carrier and the Fleet Flagship of the Royal Navy. She was formally commissioned into the Royal Navy at a ceremony in Portsmouth on 10 December 2019, and she is the eighth Royal Navy ship to carry the name. The carrier can take up to 48 F-35B Lightning II stealth multirole fighters and Merlin helicopters, as well as accommodate 250 Royal Marines.
