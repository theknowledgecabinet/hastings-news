UK Power Networks has been working on an underground electrical main in the Pilot Field area of Hastings, and the works have caused a number of power cuts and noisy temporary generators in the neighbourhood in recent months.

Hastings and Rye MP Helena Dollimore called an emergency public meeting on Friday evening at the Hastings United Pilot Field ground. She said many residents were living with generators creating loud noise and disruption, and that many of the affected people were vulnerable, including the elderly, sick residents and families with new-born babies. She said it was not acceptable how UK Power Networks had handled the situation, and that maintenance was needed but the company had not communicated properly.

After the meeting, she said UK Power Networks had agreed to bring the works forward, with the aim of finishing by early December instead of 2027, after which all generators will be removed. In the meantime the company will keep residents updated by hand-delivered letters and make direct contact by phone or door-knocking with residents on the priority services register. All residents impacted will receive a compensation payment, with details to follow.

Residents who could not attend the meeting were asked to email Helena.Dollimore.MP@Parliament.uk with their address, mobile number and the subject line "TN34 Powercuts" to receive updates.
