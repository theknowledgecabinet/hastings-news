A psychedelic rock trio fronted by a Hastings-born singer plays a free gig at the Jenny Lind in December for the last Adventures in Pop! music evening of the year.

Minor Dents, whose singer Holly Finch is from Hastings but now lives in Brighton, appear at the AiP! Club at the Jenny Lind on Tuesday 1 December at 8pm, with the house electro folk band Montague Armstrong Rippengal in support.

The band, which also features Ryan Bollard on drums and Al Strachan on synth, are pushing out their new album Sitting with the Fish, released by an Italian label and engineered in Hove. They opened the last Lewes Psych Festival, and vinyl copies of the record will be on sale at the gig.
