A series of planning applications have been submitted to Hastings Borough Council, including proposals for three houses in multiple occupation in St Leonards, according to a round-up of applications published by the Hastings Observer.

The HMO proposals concern 15A Cloudesley Road, for a five-bedroom HMO, neighbouring 15B Cloudesley Road, for a six-bedroom HMO, and Eileens, 12 Tower Road, for a change of use from a dwellinghouse to an HMO. The round-up also includes the conversion of the second and third-floor maisonette at 27 Carisbrooke Road, St Leonards, into two self-contained flats; the replacement of four mobile classrooms at Saxon Mount School in Edinburgh Road with a two-storey modular building holding eight classrooms; works at Christ Church Blacklands in Laton Road, Hastings, including regrading at the rear entrance, repaving and new traditional fencing; a Victorian restoration programme at 34 St Mary's Terrace, Hastings; and a listed-building application for Sinnock Cottage, Sinnock Passage, Croft Road, Hastings.

Members of the public can view full details of applications and submit comments via the council's planning portal, within the statutory consultation period.
