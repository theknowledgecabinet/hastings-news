Sussex Police are appealing for witnesses after two vehicle fires in Bewbush, which are being treated as deliberate.

Police received reports of both fires in the early hours of the morning on 21 October. One took place in Basildon Way and the other in Salvington Road.

Officers are pursuing a number of lines of enquiry and are appealing for anyone who witnessed the incidents, or who has any relevant information which could aid the ongoing investigation, to come forward. People with information can report it online or by calling 101, quoting serial 39 of 21/10.
