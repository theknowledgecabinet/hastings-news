Southern Housing has chosen a new name for its proposed major redevelopment of the Four Courts tower blocks in St Leonards. The 1960s social housing scheme, comprising Bevin, Roosevelt, Kennedy, and Churchill Courts, is set to be demolished and rebuilt.

The new development will be called Stonehouse View. A Southern Housing spokesperson said the name was chosen with the support of the Four Courts Steering Group, the recognised body representing all residents during the regeneration.

The project aims to create about 400 high-quality social rented homes. The housing association stated that the current design does not meet modern mobility standards and that the new build will better meet the needs of current and future residents.
