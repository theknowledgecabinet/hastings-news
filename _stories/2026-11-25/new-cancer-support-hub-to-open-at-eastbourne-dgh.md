A new dedicated cancer support hub is set to open at Eastbourne District General Hospital, providing a space for patients and families across East Sussex.

East Sussex Healthcare NHS Trust said the bespoke building was funded by a £200,000 grant from Macmillan Cancer Support. The hub will offer signposting, social prescribing, and a private counselling room, allowing people to receive support away from busy clinical areas.

The structure, built off-site in Lancashire, is due to be delivered to the hospital on Saturday 28 November. The trust advised that the road leading to the relevant car park area will be closed between 7am and 10am to allow for safe delivery.
