East Sussex Libraries are holding a series of workshops and events about grief throughout December. The programme includes sessions in Hastings, Bexhill, Battle, Rye, and Newhaven, designed for those dealing with personal loss or interested in learning more about bereavement.

Henry Young from Hastings Library said the events offer supportive outlets such as mindfulness, journaling, and bibliotherapy. He noted that these practices can provide a supportive and helpful outlet to help people meet and process grief and loss.

On 2 December, Hastings Library will host a talk by photojournalist John Cole. Cole documented life with HIV/AIDS in the 1980s, including photographs of filmmaker Derek Jarman at Dungeness. His presentation explores how the epidemic confronted people with mortality, running from 2pm to 3pm.
