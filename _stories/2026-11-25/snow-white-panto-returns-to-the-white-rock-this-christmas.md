The White Rock in Hastings is set to host its Christmas panto, Snow White, from Saturday 12 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027. The production features a cast led by Shona McGarty, who plays Snow White, and Matt Terry, who takes the role of Prince Henry.

McGarty is best known for her role as Whitney Dean in BBC’s EastEnders, while Terry has appeared in productions such as Rock of Ages and The Grinch. Local favourites Ben Watson and Tim McArthur return to the stage, playing Muddles and Herman the Henchman respectively. The company also includes Ayo Sodeinde as Fairy Fortune and Tuppence A’Bag as Queen Morgana.

The venue offers relaxed and British Sign Language signed performances on Tuesday 29 December 2026 to support audiences with additional needs. Youth groups and schools can book tickets for £14 via the box office.
