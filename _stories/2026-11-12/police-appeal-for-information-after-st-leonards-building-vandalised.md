Police have appealed for witnesses after windows were smashed and graffiti was daubed on a building in St Leonards.

The incident happened in Brunel Road in the early hours of November 5, at around 3.30am, Sussex Police said.

Detective Inspector Barry Chandler said the force is actively investigating and believes three people came onto the site and caused the damage. Officers are proactively patrolling the area.

Anyone with information, or who has CCTV or dashcam footage, is asked to contact police on 101, quoting serial 120 of 05/11.
