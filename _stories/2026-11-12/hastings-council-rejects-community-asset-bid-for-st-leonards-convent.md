Hastings Borough Council has turned down a bid to list the former Convent of the Holy Child Jesus on Magdalen Road, St Leonards, as an Asset of Community Value.

The application was made by the Save the Convent Group. The 4.76-hectare site has been allocated within the Local Plan for a potential 200 homes and is currently being offered for sale. After closing as a language school it fell into disrepair, and in July a major fire broke out on the third floor of the disused complex in an arson attack.

Stephen Dodson, head of Strategic Programmes at the council, said the panel determined the application failed to demonstrate a non-ancillary use that furthers the social well-being of the local community. He said this was a statutory test the council had no discretion to depart from, and that the community events referred to in the nomination were considered ancillary to the site's previous use as a language school.

Members of the group expressed disappointment, with one describing the decision as 'disgraceful' and another saying the historic building and its land would disappear, gone forever, with nothing being done to stop it.

Cllr Mark Etherington, lead councillor for planning, said in August that more than 2,300 comments from more than 540 individuals and organisations had been received during the Local Plan consultation, and that the development brief for the site seeks to balance the protection of its historic buildings with the need to provide homes.
