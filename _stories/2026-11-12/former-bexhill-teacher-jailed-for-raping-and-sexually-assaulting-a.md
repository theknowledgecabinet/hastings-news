A former teacher from Bexhill has been jailed for raping and sexually assaulting a child, Sussex Police said.

Steven Hardie, 65, of Woodsgate Park, Bexhill, was arrested and charged with two counts of rape, four counts of indecent assault and three counts of indecency with a child, police said. Officers seized electronic devices belonging to Mr Hardie and found historic sexual messages between him and a child, they said. Police then contacted the victim, who is now an adult, who disclosed the abuse, including being raped on two occasions.

Following a trial at Lewes Crown Court that concluded on 25 August, Mr Hardie was found guilty of all but one count of indecency with a child, police said. At Brighton Crown Court on 26 October he was jailed for nine years and nine months, given a restraining order against his victims and made subject of an indefinite Sexual Harm Prevention Order. Police said he had previously received a police caution for grooming a girl.
