A Hastings couple is to celebrate its 70th, or Platinum, wedding anniversary next month.

Mildred and Rodney Pelling, both 90, married on 1 December 1956 at Mount Pleasant Congregational Church, Mount Pleasant Road, with the reception at the Royal British Legion Hall in Haig House, Station Road, the Hastings Observer reported. They met at a dance hall; Mildred, born in Broomgrove Road, worked at Pullman Press, while Rodney, born in Cornwallis Gardens, was serving with 511 Squadron, Transport Command, of the Royal Air Force.

The couple moved to Swindon while Rodney was stationed at RAF Lyneham, and returned to Hastings in 1968 after moving around in Sussex and Kent.

Their family said the couple have held key fundraising roles for local schools and the Army Cadet Force, that both helped raise funds to refurbish the War Memorial in Alexandra Park, and that both received the Order of 1066 Award in October 2012, the first and only time the town's award had been given to a couple. Giving their secret to a long marriage, they said: "Tolerance, all couples have ups and downs but it's about working your way through it together."
