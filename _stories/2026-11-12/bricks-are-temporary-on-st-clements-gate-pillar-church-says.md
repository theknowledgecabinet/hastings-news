Concerns raised this week about a modern red brick repair to the gate pillar at St Clements Church in Hastings Old Town have been answered by the church, which said the bricks are a temporary measure.

The stone pillar was badly damaged on October 18 after a supermarket delivery van struck it in Croft Road, witnesses said, while its driver was attempting a three-point turn.

Reverend Mat Phipps, the parish's priest in charge, said some of the stonework and pointing still needed to be done and that the project was not expected to be complete until January. Some of the ironwork had been refitted earlier than expected, he said, and the pointing work is particularly weather-dependent.

The repairs are being carried out at the cost of the insurers of the vehicle which caused the damage, with the project overseen by a heritage architect who was on site on November 9 to inspect progress.
