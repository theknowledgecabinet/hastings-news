As the UN climate summit, COP31, begins in Turkey, the local community benefit society Energise Sussex Coast is calling on the government to restore heat pump funding, keep its current electric vehicle targets and reject the Rosebank oil field in the North Sea, according to the Hastings Online Times.

The group said last month's budget cut nearly £800m from the Boiler Upgrade Scheme that subsidises replacing gas boilers with heat pumps, and that the government is expected to announce a reduction in the share of new car sales that must be fully electric by 2030, from 80% to 50 to 60 percent.

Kate Meakin, director of Energise Sussex Coast, said: "If the UK government is serious about tackling both the climate and cost-of-living crises, then it must stop undermining the clean-tech industries - like heat pumps and EVs - that help to reduce greenhouse gas emissions and save people money."

The group, founded in 2012, is encouraging East Sussex residents to contact their MPs during COP31, with a template letter available on its website. Its sister project Energise South has raised community funding to install solar panels on eleven sites across East Sussex, including six schools in Hastings and St Leonards.
