A new support group for residents who use wheelchairs or mobility scooters is being launched in Hastings.

Cllr Solly Solamito is working with Hastings Voluntary Action, Hastings Disability Rights and Cllr John Lee of Rother District Council, who has worked with the successful Bexhill Rollin' Crew, commonly known as the Bexhill Rollers, the council said. The group aims to combat isolation for wheelchair and mobility scooter users and improve town-wide accessibility in Hastings.

The first meeting is on Thursday, 3 December, at 2pm in the Central Hall, 6 Bank Buildings, Station Road, and is open to anyone who would like to attend.

Cllr Solamito, lead councillor for Corporate Services, Communities and Equalities, said: "Groups like this help bring people together, reduce isolation and provide a valuable voice in highlighting any accessibility issues and opportunities."
