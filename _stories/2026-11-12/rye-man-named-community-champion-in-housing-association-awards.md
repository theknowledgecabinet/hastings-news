Dan Lake, who lives in Tilling Green near Rye, has been named Community Champion in Southern Housing's 2026 Shine Bright Awards.

The awards were introduced to mark the housing association's 125th anniversary and recognise people who go above and beyond to make their communities better places to live. As chairman of the Tilling Green Residents' Association, Dan has spent years bringing people together, helping to organise the Green Waves Community Festival and encouraging residents to have a voice.

Dan said he was "shocked and very humbled" by the honour. "I don't do what I do for awards but I'm so proud to receive this and want to thank every volunteer for everything they have helped me with over the years," he said.

One fellow Rye resident called him "A Shining Light in Rye", praising a man who "quietly gets on with making life better for those around them".
