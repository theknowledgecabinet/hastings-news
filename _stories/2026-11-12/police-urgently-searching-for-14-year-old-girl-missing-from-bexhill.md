Sussex Police are urgently searching for a 14-year-old girl who has been missing from Bexhill since Tuesday (November 10).

The force said on Facebook on Wednesday (November 11) that Darcie, 14, is 5ft 7in and has a nose piercing. She was last seen wearing pink leggings and a black puffa jacket and was carrying a large white bag. Police said Darcie also has links to Worthing.

The force is appealing to the public for information about her whereabouts.
