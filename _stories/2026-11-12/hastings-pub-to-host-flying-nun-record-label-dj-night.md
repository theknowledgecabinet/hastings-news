A DJ night celebrating the music of New Zealand's Flying Nun record label is coming to Hastings.

Founded in Christchurch in 1981 by Roger Shepherd, Flying Nun is described as New Zealand's most influential independent record label, renowned for pioneering the jangly, lo-fi post-punk aesthetic known as the Dunedin Sound.

For the second year running, Twelve Hundred Postcards in Queens Road will host the one-night event, with a particular focus on the label's formative 1980s and 45 years of classic 45s. It takes place on Saturday, November 21, from 7pm.

David Brumley, who runs the bar, said: "Whether you're a lifelong fan or discovering Flying Nun for the first time, there'll be something for everyone."
