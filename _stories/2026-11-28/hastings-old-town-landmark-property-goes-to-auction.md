A mixed commercial and residential property in Hastings Old Town is coming up for auction in December. The end-terrace at 58A & 58B High Street is listed with a freehold guide price of £260,000-plus. It is famously known as the Shirley Leaf & Petal Company and home to the Flower Makers Museum.

The building currently has commercial space on the ground floor and basement, with residential accommodation on the three floors above. Auction Appraiser Jade Flood said: “The property has history throughout but now needs repair and refurbishment.” She noted it may offer potential for alternative uses, subject to necessary consents.

Also listed is Haig House in Devonshire Road, offered with a guide price of £150,000 to £170,000. Director Sam Kinloch described it as ideal for continued investment, noting easy access to the mainline railway station and Priory Meadow shopping centre.
