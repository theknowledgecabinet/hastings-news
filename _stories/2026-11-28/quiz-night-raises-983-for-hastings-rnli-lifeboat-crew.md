Hastings RNLI has raised £983 to support its lifeboat crew following a quiz night held on Sunday, November 22. The event took place at the East Hastings Sea Angling Association.

Paul Freathy, chair of the Hastings RNLI Lifeboat Management Group, described the result as brilliant. He said: "Every penny raised helps us to save lives at sea, so I’m really grateful to everyone who supported us."

Mr Freathy also thanked the volunteer organising team and encouraged the public to support future RNLI fundraising events.
