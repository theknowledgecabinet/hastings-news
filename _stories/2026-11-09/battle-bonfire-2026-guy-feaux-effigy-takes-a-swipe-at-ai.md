Battle High Street became a 'river of fire' on Saturday night as people turned out for the town's annual bonfire celebrations, the Hastings Observer reported.

Following a torch-lit procession down the High Street, crowds gathered around a large bonfire on the Abbey Green to watch a fireworks display. Bonfire societies from across the county took part, including Hastings, Rye, Lewes, Robertsbridge and Staplecross.

This year's effigy was a robotic Guy named 'Guy Feaux', a swipe at artificial intelligence. Battle Bonfire is organised by the Battel Bonfire Boyes and traces its traditions back 380 years, with the earliest recorded celebration dating to 1646.
