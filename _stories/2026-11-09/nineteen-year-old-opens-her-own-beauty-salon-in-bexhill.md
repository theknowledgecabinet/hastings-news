Grace Guenigault, 19, has opened her own beauty salon in Bexhill, the Hastings Observer reported.

The Glam Studio is in Village Mews, Little Common, and opened on 31 October. Grace said she has had a passion for the beauty industry and wanted to create 'a welcoming space where clients could feel relaxed, comfortable and confident'.

She offers eyelash extensions, lash lifts and tints. 'I'm 19 and feel really excited and proud about starting my own business,' she said, and added that it is important for locals to support independent businesses because 'they help bring character, diversity and a sense of community to the area'.
