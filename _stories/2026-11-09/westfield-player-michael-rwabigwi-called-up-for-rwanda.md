A player at a tenth-tier English football club has been called up to represent his country.

Westfield FC, of the Southern Combination League Division 1, said Michael Rwabigwi has been named in Rwanda's under-23 squad for the Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers against Cameroon.

The club said: "It's an incredible achievement for Mikey and a hugely proud moment for everyone connected with Westfield FC." A spokesman added the club was contacted by the federation asking if it could release him, calling it "a deserved call-up".

Rwanda play Cameroon at home on Wednesday 11 November, with the return fixture six days later.
