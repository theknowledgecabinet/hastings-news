The Rye and District Bonfire has been named among the best Bonfire Night displays in the country, The Argus reports.

The Telegraph featured the event in a list of top displays across the UK, describing it as one that rivals the better-known Lewes Bonfire Night and praising its drums, fire torches and fireworks finale. The tradition is entirely self-funded by volunteers through collections, sales and a crowdfunding page.

This year's bonfire takes place on Saturday 14 November. The torchlit procession starts at 7pm through the cobbled streets, passing under the Landgate Arch, and ends at Rye Salts, where the bonfire is lit at 8pm and fireworks follow at 8.15pm.

Organisers say it is a family-oriented evening, and ask that no fireworks are discharged before, during or after the procession, and that discarded torches are not picked up.
