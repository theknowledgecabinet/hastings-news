Outline permission has been submitted to build up to 42 homes on land at Bannatyne's Spa Hotel in Hastings Road, St Leonards, according to the latest Hastings Borough Council planning applications.

The application (reference HS/OA/26/00608) seeks residential development, including associated roads and amenity spaces, with all matters reserved for future consideration apart from the means of access.

The submission is one of several in the latest round-up, which also lists a single-storey rear extension at 16A Branksome Road, St Leonards, and plans to install four condensers at 6 Bank Buildings, Station Road, Hastings.
