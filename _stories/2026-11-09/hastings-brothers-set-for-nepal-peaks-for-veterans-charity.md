Two brothers from Hastings are heading to the Himalayas to raise money for a local veterans' charity.

Josh and Jake Bruton, both former British Army soldiers, told BBC Radio Sussex they are setting off on Friday to climb Lobuche East and Island Peak in Nepal, both of which are more than 6,000 metres high. They are raising funds for the East Sussex Veterans Hub, a Hastings-based charity that supports former military personnel, including those who are homeless and those with mental health issues and addictions.

"We want to raise as much money as we can climbing heights. For us, it's important to us to help veterans in our home town," they said, as reported by the BBC.

It is not their first challenge: in 2025 Joshua cycled across America from Miami to San Diego, and the brothers completed the Hastings Half Marathon in March carrying a 40kg log on their shoulders.
