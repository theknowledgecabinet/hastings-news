A new youth hub has opened at 6-7 Cornwallis Street in Hastings. The Department for Work and Pensions (DWP) said the facility is open Monday to Friday and is part of a government plan to deliver a youth hub in every area of the UK.

A DWP spokesperson said the hubs bring together Jobcentre Plus, local authority services, employers, and training providers under one roof. The aim is to provide help for young people aged 16 to 24 to get back on track.

Work and Pensions Secretary Pat McFadden said the new hub marks a major step forward for young people in Hastings. He added: “We are determined to bring opportunity to every corner of the country because no matter where you grow up, you deserve the chance to earn, to learn and to get on in life.”

The DWP said the Hastings Youth Hub will work with local partners, including CXK, Hastings Borough Council, and the ESCC Young Person’s Housing & Accommodation Service. This collaboration is designed to deliver tailored support that reflects the needs of the local economy.
