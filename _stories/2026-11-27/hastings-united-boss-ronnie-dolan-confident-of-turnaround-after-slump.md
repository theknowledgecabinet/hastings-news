Hastings United manager Ronnie Dolan says the team will "start firing again" as they look to end a slump that has seen them lose five Isthmian League East games in six.

The Pilot Field side sits in sixth place, nine points behind leaders Faversham, following a 2-0 defeat at Broadbridge Heath last Saturday. Dolan acknowledged the loss but maintained his faith in the squad, noting that the team has been working on keeping things simple in training.

The club has been boosted by the arrival of ex-Crowborough striker Elliott Duncan, who made his debut at Broadbridge Heath. However, they have lost Mitch Chapman to league rivals Margate. Dolan said Chapman moved because the move was in line with where he lives and where they train, and that the club hopes to have another one or two added to the squad ready for Saturday.
