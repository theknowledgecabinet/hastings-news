East Sussex County Council is working to find new accommodation for 28 residents of Old Hastings House after failing to agree on increased placement costs.

The council said it was "unable to support" a requested increase of between £550 and £600 per person per week, which would add roughly £800,000 to its annual expenditure. A spokesperson described the situation as "extremely disappointing" and acknowledged the short notice and timing of the news.

Old Hastings House, which offers residential care and secure dementia support, said it had been in discussions with the council for several months. The home stated that the fee increase seeks to bridge the gap between its costs and the rates currently being paid, while emphasising its focus on the wellbeing and dignity of those in its care.
