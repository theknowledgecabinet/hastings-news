East Sussex Healthcare NHS Trust has asked people to wear face masks in some areas of its hospitals.

The trust, which runs the Conquest Hospital in Hastings and Bexhill Hospital, said it is seeing a sharp rise in respiratory illnesses, including flu and COVID.

Masks are now requested in emergency departments and some admission areas. Staff may also ask visitors to wear them on wards where respiratory viruses are present.

The trust said masks will be available in these areas and that teams will let people know when they are required. It also advised people not to visit if they have symptoms of a respiratory or other infectious illness, unless they need medical care.
