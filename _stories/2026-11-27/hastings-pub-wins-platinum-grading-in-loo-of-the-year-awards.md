The John Logie Baird pub in Havelock Road, Hastings, has been awarded a platinum grading in the Loo of the Year Awards 2026. The accolade recognises the quality and standards of the facility's toilets, which were judged by inspectors during unannounced visits.

According to a spokesperson, the awards aim to highlight and improve standards of 'away from home' toilet provision across the UK. Inspectors assessed the facilities against 101 criteria, including décor, maintenance, cleanliness, accessibility, and hand washing equipment. The pub was graded platinum, a level above gold but below platinum plus and diamond.

Alex Thomas, who manages the pub, said the staff ensure the toilets are in excellent condition at all times. Becky Wall, managing director of the Loo of the Year Awards 2026, stated that the pub thoroughly deserves its grading for the standard of its facilities and overall presentation.
