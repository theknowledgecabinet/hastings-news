Crowhurst Park Lodges in Battle has been named the Relax & Explore Small Park destination in the South of England. The holiday park received the honour at Hoseasons’ 21st annual Diamond Awards, which celebrate UK holiday parks and lodge resorts rated highest by guests during the 2026 season.

The awards ceremony took place at Celtic Manor Resort in Newport, South Wales. A spokesperson for Crowhurst Park Lodges said: "We are very proud of this award."

Lloyd Sceats-Page, account director at Hoseasons, described the team as "richly deserving winners". He noted that the awards recognise the very best in UK holiday accommodation and that the park's excellent feedback scores reflect their efforts to delight guests.
