The Marianne North Centre is hosting a talk and Q&A session with author Professor Sarah Bilston on Sunday 6 December.

Titled “The Lost Orchid: a story of Victorian plunder and obsession”, the event explores the global pursuit of the rare Cattleya Labiata orchid in the 1800s. Alexander Wilberforce of the Marianne North Centre invites the public to join him for the discussion.

The talk highlights how colonial exploitation and consumer greed damaged the orchid’s habitat. It also notes local connections, including the North family’s greenhouse in Hastings and 30 paintings of the plant held at the Marianne North Gallery at Kew.
