Councillor Glenn Haffenden, leader of Hastings Borough Council, is visiting Active Hastings sessions to mark the organisation's 20th anniversary. The council leader, who is also the lead councillor for sport and leisure, will meet participants at various venues across the town to hear about the impact of the activities on their health and wellbeing.

The visits began at the weekly Urban Football session at the Torfield MUGA. Cllr Haffenden is also scheduled to join the team for seated strength, Waistband of Brothers, Exercise of Parkinson’s, and Home Ed multi-sports sessions. The campaign will be featured on Active Hastings’ social media channels weekly until the end of the year.

Cath Donovan, Physical Activity and Health Strategic Lead, said the council leader’s presence helps celebrate the positive impact on residents. Active Hastings uses a sliding ticket system to ensure inclusivity, with prices ranging from free to £3, supported by funding from the Hastings & St Leonards Primary Care Network, East Sussex County Council Public Health, and Active Sussex.
