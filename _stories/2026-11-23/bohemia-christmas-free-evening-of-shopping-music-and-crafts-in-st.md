A free community celebration is set for the Bohemia area of St Leonards on Thursday 3 December. From 4pm to 7pm, residents can enjoy late-night shopping, live music, Santa, and drop-in craft workshops.

The event, organised by Kat Beveridge of Salt Guide and Katie Whyman, aims to bring together local residents and businesses. It follows the closure of the Bohemia Creative Quarter in 2024, with organisers hoping to revive the sense of community that defined the area between 2022 and 2024.

Participating venues include Sugarpie Honeybuns, Bank House Studio, and St Leonards-on-Sea Methodist Church. The church will host free workshops and carols, while local choir KIN will headline with pop songs and Christmas tunes. A 'Bohemia Trail' map invites visitors to collect a code word at each location; anyone who visits at least five will have a chance to win a £200 hamper.
