A local Christmas comedy tradition is back in Hastings for its ninth year. Penny Pincher’s Christmas Carol, performed by local theatre duo Kate Tym and John Knowles, returns to the White Rock Hotel this December.

The show is loosely based on Charles Dickens’ A Christmas Carol. It features two performers taking on multiple characters in 75 minutes, including songs, dancing, and audience participation. The production is described as having "shoestring production values" and a deliberately ridiculous approach to the classic tale.

Kate Tym said the duo rewrites bits and adds new jokes every year. "We're always amazed that people keep coming back!" she said. John Knowles added that the show doesn't take itself remotely seriously, noting that you don't need to know anything about Dickens.

The Christmas Eve performance is already sold out. The show runs from 18 to 24 December at 7.30pm, downstairs at the White Rock Hotel, Hastings.
