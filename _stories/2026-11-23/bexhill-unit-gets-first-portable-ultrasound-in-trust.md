The Bexhill Irvine Unit has received the trust’s first SonoSite MT Ultrasound System. East Sussex Healthcare NHS Trust says the new equipment supports specialist rehabilitation and injection services for patients with complex needs.

The portable technology allows clinicians to perform image-guided joint injections and complex cannulation with greater accuracy. It also supports Botulinum Toxin injection management for patients living with spasticity following a stroke, brain injury, or spinal cord injury.

Karen Poole, Rehabilitation Consultant, said the system enables treatments to be delivered with increased precision and safety. The trust notes this helps reduce risks associated with contractures and allows care to be provided without transferring patients to acute sites.
