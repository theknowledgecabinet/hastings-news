Actor Jud Charlton is set to perform a one-man adaptation of Charles Dickens’s A Christmas Carol at the Old Church in St Leonards. The production runs from 8 to 17 December, with two shows daily: a matinee for local schools and an evening performance for families.

Organiser Jamie De’Ath described the venue as a “fantastic space” and noted that the audience will sit at candle-lit tables, with food and drink available from Gather by Plates. De’Ath stated that this is the first theatrical event for the venue, so the capacity is limited to 150 people per performance.

The show marks the 165th anniversary of Dickens performing his own one-man reading in Hastings. Charlton, who plays every character, said the story’s message remains timeless because modern issues like poverty and homelessness persist. Local schools were invited to matinee performances because Dickens is studied by Key Stages 3 and 4.
