Hastings Rowing Club has marked the end of a successful year at its annual general meeting and awards night. The event brought together members to recognise the contributions of rowers, coaches, and volunteers throughout 2026.

Charlotte Ashcroft has been appointed as the new Chair, with Hannah serving as Vice-Chair, Colin Chatfield as Treasurer, and Holly Cavill as Facilities Manager. The club thanked outgoing committee members Jim, Alan, Simon, and Ray for their years of service.

The evening also featured traditional fun awards and recognised sporting achievements, including John Webb as Rower of the Year and Roland Rebek as Coach of the Year. The club, founded in 1868, is looking forward to building on its racing successes and growing Learn to Row programme in 2027.
