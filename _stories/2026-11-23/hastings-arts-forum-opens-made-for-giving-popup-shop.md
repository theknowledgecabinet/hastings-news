A popup shop called Made for Giving opens at the Hastings Arts Forum in Marine Court on Wednesday 25 November. The exhibition features original gifts from 20 local artists, makers, and designers, including paintings, ceramics, textiles, and jewellery.

The event runs until Sunday 13 December, with a launch evening scheduled for Thursday 26 November from 6pm to 8pm. The shop will be open to the public from Wednesday to Sunday, 11am to 5pm.

According to the Hastings Online Times, the collection highlights local talent and includes works inspired by Hastings spots and experiences. Featured makers include Faye Moorhouse, Fiona Granelli, Lorraine Ford, Olivia Morris, Siew Lee Vorley, and Erica Smith.
