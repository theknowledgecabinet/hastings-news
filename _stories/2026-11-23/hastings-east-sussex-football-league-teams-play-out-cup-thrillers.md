Teams in the Macron Sports Hub Hastings East Sussex Football League enjoyed an action-packed weekend as the ESFL Challenge Cup took centre stage. With most clubs involved in cup competition, there was plenty of excitement as teams battled for places in the next round.

Junior Club Tackleway produced a standout result in the Premier Division, defeating Battle Town 3-1. Rye Town also continued their excellent campaign with a 3-1 away victory over Ninfield. In the East Sussex League, Bexhill AAC booked their place in the next round with a hard-fought 2-1 home victory over SC Pass & Move Arrows.

Further drama unfolded at St Leonards Social, where the hosts overcame Herstmonceux following a penalty shootout. Stanley Dhaura scored a hat-trick as Social drew 3-3 in normal time before winning the shootout 3-0.
