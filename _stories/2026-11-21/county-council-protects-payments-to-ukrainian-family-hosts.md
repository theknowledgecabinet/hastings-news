East Sussex County Council has stepped in to protect payments to families hosting Ukrainian refugees after the government said the 'thank you' payment would fall from £350 to £100 a month from January 2027.

The council's lead member for adult social care and health has agreed to fund the shortfall, keeping payments at £350 a month until the end of March. Hosts welcoming new arrivals will be guaranteed £350 a month for their first six months of hosting, the council said.

Cllr Mark Estcourt said the government's decision, made with only three months' notice and immediately after Christmas, "will be deeply concerning for many hosts and guests who have opened their homes and supported Ukrainian families over the last four years".

He said maintaining the payments would help reduce pressure on those households and give Ukrainian guests more time to secure suitable independent accommodation and make longer-term plans.

The council said the existing eligibility rules for Ukraine Permission Extension visa holders continue to apply. Hosts and guests can contact the Homes for Ukraine Contact Centre on 01273 337010 or by email at Ukraine@eastsussex.gov.uk.
