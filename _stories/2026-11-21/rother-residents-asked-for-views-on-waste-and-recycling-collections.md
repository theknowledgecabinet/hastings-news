Residents across Rother are being asked for their views on the district's waste and recycling collections, Rother District Council has said.

M·E·L Research is carrying out a satisfaction survey on behalf of Biffa Municipal Ltd, the contractor that provides domestic waste and recycling collections in the district, the council said. The survey is also being run in the neighbouring authorities of Hastings and Wealden.

Information from the survey will be used by Biffa and the councils to understand levels of satisfaction with the services. The council said the survey should take no longer than ten minutes to complete, that all responses will be completely confidential, and that residents can take part through an online survey linked from the council's website.
