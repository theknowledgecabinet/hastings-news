A zebra crossing in Bexhill has been moved and widened after work to improve pedestrian safety on Sea Road was completed, the county council has confirmed.

The crossing has been relocated further south along the road, in an area that serves the town centre and the railway station. East Sussex County Council said the change allows motorists to make a full turn from Endwell Road before stopping at the crossing, if required. The new, wider crossing was funded by the county council and built by Balfour Beatty Living Places, its highways contractor.

Cllr Peter Griffiths, the county council's lead member for transport and environment, said the work has made the area safer for pedestrians and other road users, and that the crossing is also used by pupils walking to and from St Richard's Catholic College. Additional work on Sea Road during the summer included patching the carriageway, reconstructing sections of the footway and minor drainage improvements, with new road markings and updated signage.
