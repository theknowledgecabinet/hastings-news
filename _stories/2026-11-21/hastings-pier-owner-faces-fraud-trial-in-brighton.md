A trial by jury has opened for the owner of Hastings Pier over allegations of fraud, a court heard.

Sheikh Abid Gulzar, 81, and his former business associate Manasdeep Singh are defending up to ten charges of fraud brought by water supplier Castle Water Limited, according to the Hastings Independent Press. The charges relate to what is said to be 150,000 pounds worth of water supplied to a number of hotels owned by Gulzar's companies.

The two defendants, who are separately represented, deny all of the charges. The case is being presided over by Judge Swirsky, sitting as a Crown Court judge at Brighton Magistrates' Court, and is expected to run for at least three weeks.
