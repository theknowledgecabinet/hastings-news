More than 30 women took to the Hastings seafront on a Wednesday evening at the end of October, torches and hi-vis in hand, to back a national campaign on women's safety when exercising outdoors in the dark.

The event was held in support of Let's Lift the Curfew, a campaign run by This Girl Can, the Sport England initiative, which highlights the barriers women face when being active in the darker winter months. Research from the campaign found that 72 per cent of women change how they exercise outdoors in winter because of safety concerns, according to the Hastings Independent Press.

The evening was organised by This Woman Runs, a local running community, and brought in local organisations including Sussex Police and Active Hastings. Kirsty, a community leader with the group, said: 'This was about much more than running. It's about women feeling confident enough to go outside and not feeling that they have to change their lives because it's getting dark.'
