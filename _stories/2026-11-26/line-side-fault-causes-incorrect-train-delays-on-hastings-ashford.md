Trains between Hastings and Ashford International are showing as delayed on journey planners this morning, Wednesday 25 November, due to a line-side equipment fault.

Southern Rail said the issue affects the tracking system in the Appledore area, causing lines to show as disrupted or services to incorrectly show as delayed on journey planners. A normal service is running, and passengers can use their usual routes.

Network Rail manages the system and its staff are investigating the fault as quickly as possible. The disruption is expected to last until 8.30am.
