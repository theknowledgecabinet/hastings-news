Drawing Life, run by Judy Parkinson, is hosting a Christmas exhibition at Electro Studios in St Leonards on 4, 5 and 6 December. The show features artworks created this year by unpaid carers and the people they care for.

Artist facilitators Theresa Caruana, Sineid Codd, James Mackinnon, Peter Quinnell and Ian Shillito are also displaying their own work. Special guests included Helena Dollimore MP and local resident Sue Tilley, who sat for life-size portraits.

Janis, a carer and Emeritus Professor of Visual Art at Goldsmiths, said the classes offer "space to do something enjoyable and enriching in a welcoming environment." Drawing Life aims to provide a social lifeline for unpaid carers through creative activities.
