Hastings & Bexhill Harriers have introduced a new Monday evening initiative for women across the local community. The group aims to create a welcoming environment where participants can enjoy running in company, build confidence and feel safer.

According to the club, many women hesitate to run alone, particularly during darker evenings. The initiative is designed to address this by fostering a sense of community and friendship, rather than focusing solely on safety.

The sessions are open to women of all abilities, from experienced runners to those just starting out. Participants do not need to be members of the Harriers or belong to any specific running club. The initiative is described as a way to bring women together, not to recruit members or compete with other groups.
