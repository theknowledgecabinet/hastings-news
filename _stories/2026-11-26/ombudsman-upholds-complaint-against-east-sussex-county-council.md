The Local Government and Social Care Ombudsman has upheld a complaint against East Sussex County Council regarding the handling of a child’s special educational needs.

The decision, issued on 13 October 2026, found that the council caused distress and uncertainty by failing to properly consider information from a review of the child’s Education, Health and Care (EHC) Plan. The Ombudsman said the council rigidly applied its policy not to amend a plan within 12 months of a Tribunal decision, despite new evidence.

The council also failed to properly consider its duty to make alternative educational provision in early 2026 when the child could not attend the named school.
