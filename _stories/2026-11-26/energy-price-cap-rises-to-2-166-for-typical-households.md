Ofgem has announced that the energy price cap will rise to £2,166 for a typical dual-fuel household paying by direct debit from 1 January to 31 March 2027. The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Energy Security and Net Zero, Ms Polly Billington, stated in the Commons that this increase is driven primarily by soaring international wholesale gas prices resulting from the war in Iran.

The Government is providing support to mitigate the impact, including an automatic £100 payment to 20 million households straight into their bank accounts where councils hold direct debit details. Ms Billington noted that this is in addition to previous measures, such as removing £150 of costs from bills and extending the temporary VAT cut on electricity until June 2027. She also mentioned that local authorities have £100 million available for discretionary support to struggling households not eligible for the core rebate.

The MP for Hastings and Rye, Helena Dollimore, welcomed the £100 payment for over 40,000 local households. She highlighted local initiatives, including a new electric vehicle training centre and a campaign for Hastings borough council to install solar panels on retail parks along Bexhill Road. Ms Billington added that she looks forward to seeing more about the local power plan, which will be outlined by her departmental colleagues, as well as ensuring that they develop community benefit that really works for communities like those in Hastings and Rye.
