A debate has emerged in the Hastings Online Times over the interpretation of journalist Gareth Jones' 1933 reporting on the Soviet famine.

Philip Colley, a local resident and Jones' great-nephew, argues that Jones' testimony describes a Soviet-wide famine affecting the peasantry as a social class. He contends that Jones' accounts have been distorted to support a specific 'Holodomor' genocide narrative focused solely on Ukraine.

Professor Jeremy Hicks of Queen Mary University of London disagrees. He argues that Jones' unpublished diaries reveal punitive grain confiscations and cultural repression specifically targeting the Ukrainian nation, distinguishing the Ukrainian famine from the wider Soviet crisis. Hicks suggests Colley's interpretation aligns with political positions opposing UK support for Ukraine.
