The Hastings Pride in Place Board met on 13 November to decide how to allocate capacity funding for community groups. The board agreed to release up to £75,000 for the current funding round, with the remaining £25,000 from the total £100,000 allocation carried forward to future rounds.

The funding is split into two categories: building community capacity and supporting future applications. The board prioritised applications from the community and voluntary sector over individual consultancy services. It also agreed to set aside £25,000 specifically to develop a process for involving young people in project delivery.

According to the minutes, 38 applications under £5,000 and eight over £5,000 have progressed to due diligence. The board noted that young people remain under-represented in both applications and programme engagement, prompting the specific focus on youth involvement.
