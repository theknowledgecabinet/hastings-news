The main local item this week came from the Licensing and General Purposes Committee's General Licensing Panel on Monday 16 November at 10.00 am, whose minutes have been published. The panel appointed Councillor Biggs as chair and considered the report on an application for a premises licence at Battle Abbey School Boarding House, Whatlington Road, Battle. The application covers regulated entertainment, late night refreshment and alcohol sales indoors and outdoors within the premises, Monday to Sunday, 12.00 to 00.30.

Three meetings were held this week. Attendance as recorded was: at the General Licensing Panel, one Conservative councillor present with one apology (Jimmy Stanger) and one Green Party councillor present; at the Overview and Scrutiny Committee on the same evening, four Conservatives, one Green Party, one Labour with an apology (Sam Coleman), two Liberal Democrats with an apology (Kathryn Field) and one Rother Association of Independent Councillors member present, with an apology from Charles Clark (Independent); and at the Shareholder Executive Committee on Wednesday, three RAIC members, two Liberal Democrats with an apology (Teresa Killeen), two Labour with an apology (Ruairi McCourt) and one Green Party member present.

The council has 38 councillors, with 20 needed for a majority: Conservative 13, Liberal Democrat 8, Labour 6, Rother Association of Independent Councillors 6, Green Party 3 and Independent 2. Doug Oliver (RAIC) leads the council. Susan Prochak (Liberal Democrats) leads the Liberal Democrat group, Carl Maynard the Conservative group, Gareth Delany the Labour group and Polly Gray the Green group. There were no named votes or splits between groups this week.

Minutes were not yet published for the other two meetings. The Overview and Scrutiny Committee's agenda listed the second quarter performance report, budget monitoring for 2026/27 and an update on the housing development strategy. The Shareholder Executive Committee, a sub-committee of Cabinet, listed a performance monitoring report and a mitigations and options appraisal.

Coming up in the next two weeks: the Climate Change Steering Group meets on Thursday 26 November, due to consider the 2024 Rother district-wide emissions and the 2025/26 organisational greenhouse gas emissions, and to hear a verbal update on the East Sussex housing retrofit strategy and on climate action plan progress for buildings and energy efficiency. The Audit Committee meets on Monday 30 November, due to consider, among other items, the internal audit report to 30 September 2026 and the review of procurement exemptions. The General Licensing Panel meets on Tuesday 1 December at 10.00 am, which includes an application for a premises licence at Mermaidian Rye Tea Rooms, 13 West Street, Rye. The Standards Committee meets the same day at 3.30 pm; its agenda has not yet been published.
