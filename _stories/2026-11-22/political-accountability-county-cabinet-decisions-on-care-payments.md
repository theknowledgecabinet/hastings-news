The most significant items for the area this week were decisions published by the county council's lead cabinet members. At the Lead Member for Adult Social Care and Health meeting on Thursday 19 November at 2.00 pm, three decisions were published: the net to gross residential and nursing provider payments business case, the extension of the Integrated Health and Wellbeing Service contract, and proposed changes to Disability Related Expenditure. Area councillor Mark Estcourt was present. Councillor Johnny Denis (Green Party) declared a personal interest as the Lewes District Council cabinet member for community wellbeing, arts, culture, tourism and leisure, and did not consider it to be prejudicial.

At the Lead Member for Transport and Environment meeting on Monday 16 November at 10.00 am, the decisions published covered the Pan-Sussex Minerals and Waste Plan Proposal and the review of the Highway Vegetation Management Policy, alongside decisions made by the lead cabinet member on 20 July 2026.

Six meetings were held this week. The only meetings with attendance recorded in the record were the Lead Member meetings, in which the lead member alone was present, and the SBSA Audit and Governance Committee on Tuesday 17 November, where one Green Party, one Liberal Democrat and one Reform UK councillor were present, five others were present where no party was listed, with an apology from Nigel Dennis. That committee was due to consider the appointment of a chair and vice-chair, the introduction of Grant Thornton as external auditor, the internal audit strategy and the 2027/28 treasury management and capital strategies, and a fire and rescue transfer update.

Other meetings this week had no attendance recorded in the record: the County Consultative Committee (Governors) on Tuesday 17 November, where the agenda listed an academy update, a school resilience preparedness item and the school organisation plan; the Discretionary Transport Appeal Panel on Friday 20 November; and the East Sussex Schools Forum on the same day, which listed an update on the local SEND reform plan.

The council has 50 councillors, with 26 needed for a majority: Reform UK 20, Liberal Democrat 14, Green Party 10, Conservative 4 and Independent 2. Andy Woolley (Reform UK) leads the council; Kathryn Field (Liberal Democrats) leads the Liberal Democrat group, Julia Hilton (Green Party) the Green group and Paul Redstone (Conservative) the Conservative group. There were no named votes or splits between groups this week.

Coming up in the next two weeks: the People Scrutiny Committee meets on Monday 23 November, due to consider the East Sussex Safeguarding Children Partnership annual report, the Crisis and Resilience Fund and the carers partnership plan. The East Sussex Health and Wellbeing Board meets on Tuesday 24 November, with a local response to the national plan to end homelessness on its agenda. The Pension Committee meets on Thursday 26 November. The Audit Committee meets on Friday 27 November, due to consider, among other items, the internal audit progress report and the auditor's annual value for money report. Lead members for education and children's services meet the same day; the Place Scrutiny Committee meets on Monday 30 November; Full Council meets on Tuesday 1 December at 10.00 am; and the SBSA Overview and Scrutiny Committee meets on Thursday 3 December. Agendas for some of these had not yet been published.
