A man from Hastings has been jailed for raping a woman, police said.

Harun Yildiz, 54, a Turkish national of St Helen's Road, was sentenced at Hove Crown Court, where Recorder Nicholson-Pratt imposed a prison sentence of eight years, Sussex Police said. The trial, which concluded on 12 November, followed Yildiz denying having sexual intercourse with the victim. Police said forensic evidence confirmed that it had taken place, and that the evidence gathered during the investigation enabled detectives to present a strong case.

Police added that he will be required to serve two-thirds of his sentence before becoming eligible for parole, subject to licence conditions, and that he has been made subject to lifetime notification requirements under the sex offenders register.

Detective Inspector Carol Shoesmith of Sussex Police said the victim showed immense courage throughout the investigation and court process, and that the outcome sends a clear message that Sussex Police will pursue all available evidence to hold perpetrators of sexual offences accountable.
