People in East Sussex who pay towards adult social care will get a simpler way to claim support for disability-related costs, the county council has approved.

Residents who are financially assessed to contribute towards their care will be able to claim a standard Disability Related Expenditure allowance of 20 pounds a week through a self-assessment, East Sussex County Council said. Anyone whose disability-related costs are higher can request a full individual assessment, with no upper limit on the allowance that may be awarded.

The new approach replaces the current system of allowance bandings, the council said. Lead member for adult social care Cllr Mark Estcourt said the changes bring quicker certainty about care contributions and bring the council into line with approaches used by many other councils and with Care Act charging guidance.

The council said it consulted people who contribute towards their care costs and received feedback that a simpler process would make access easier, and that the new arrangements are expected to deliver savings to be reinvested in adult social care services.
