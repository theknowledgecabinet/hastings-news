Bexhill United ran out comfortable winners at Polegrove on Saturday, beating Midhurst and Easebourne 5-1 in the latest of a long run of fixtures at the ground that has seen the Pirates climb steadily up the table.

It went their way from the first minute, when Charlie Playford headed in from point blank range off a corner after a save. Three minutes later Ash Latuske beat two players and set up Bexhill's record goalscorer, who made no mistake, and the Pirates led 2-0 at half-time.

Midhurst pulled one back five minutes into the second half, but it did not last long. Theo Coombs made it 3-1 on 55 minutes after fine play from Latuske, Coombs then chipped in a cross for Shonk to head home 4-1, and substitute Jack Samways wrapped it up with a half volley on 76 minutes.

The win takes Bexhill up to sixth, with the visitors in the relegation mire following a change of manager and a run of poor results.
