Smokers in Conquest Hospital's emergency department can now get support to quit while they are in hospital.

East Sussex County Council has funded a tobacco dependency adviser for the emergency department at the hospital in Hastings, the council said. The adviser can help patients take the first steps towards giving up or manage nicotine withdrawal, with nicotine replacement therapy offered as soon as they are admitted, and continued support after discharge, the council added.

Hastings has the highest smoking rate in the county, with 14 per cent of adults in the borough smoking, compared with 11.4 per cent in Rother, the council said.

Darrell Gale, the council's Director of Public Health, said people who make it to 28 days smoke-free are five times more likely to quit for good. Sarah Keeling of East Sussex Healthcare said the dedicated adviser helps patients at a time when they may be thinking differently about their health and wellbeing.
