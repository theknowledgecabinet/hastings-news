Four performers are turning the Downstairs Bar at the White Rock Hotel into a day of comedy and creative writing on the Hastings seafront.

Amuse Bouche, comprising Lindsay Jayne, Nicky Torode, Nicola Reading and Stella Valente, is running Funny You Should Say That! from 12 noon to 2pm on Saturday, November 28.

Sessions run from ad libbing and improv to turning moments of your life into a 100-word story, drama techniques for writers, and finding stand-up material in everyday life. Spokeswoman Lindsay Jayne said the group has a "riot of activities" for the audience, and volunteer members of the audience will join in an ad lib exercise on light-hearted topical subjects.

Tickets are available from the Diamond Minds ticket site.
