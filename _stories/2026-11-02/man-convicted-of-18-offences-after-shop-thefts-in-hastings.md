Tom Marchant, 44, of no fixed address, has been convicted of 18 offences after a series of shop thefts in Hastings, Sussex Police have reported.

He was charged with 17 shop theft offences, 16 of which were committed during October at stores in Station Plaza and Castle Street. The offences involved goods worth more than £1,300, made up of meat, sweets and chocolate. He was also charged with possession of a Class B drug.

Marchant was arrested on 26 October and appeared before Brighton & Hove Magistrates' Court the following day, where he pleaded guilty to all 18 offences. He was remanded into custody.

Sergeant Sam Turner said business crime "can have a significant effect on those who live and work in our communities", and added that the result reflects the value of businesses reporting incidents and working with local officers.
