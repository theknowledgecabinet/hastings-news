Energise Sussex Coast, a local community benefit society, is holding free workshops on off-grid solar power in Hastings and Battle this month. The sessions are aimed at anyone who wants to build a small, fully off-grid solar system for a shed, workshop, van or truck, and no technical background is needed.

In Hastings, two workshops run from 6pm to 8pm on Thursday 26 November and Thursday 3 December at Central Hall on Station Road. In Battle, the workshops take place from 6pm to 8pm on Tuesday 17 November and Tuesday 24 November at the Battle Memorial Hall, run in partnership with Battle Solar Town.

Each location hosts a beginners' session on how off-grid solar works and how to plan one, followed by a session on sizing the system. Both are led by Energise Sussex Coast's 'green handyman', who lives off-grid himself and has built solar systems for more than 20 years.
