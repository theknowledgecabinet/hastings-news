Elizabeth Court Rest Home in Hastings Road, Bexhill, has won the Pet Friendly Care Home Award for 2026. The home, which provides residential care for older people, lets its residents bring their own pets with them when they move in.

Annie Parker, who manages the home, said pets are 'a big part of our lives, and it's heartbreaking when we have to give them up', so the home allows residents to keep them. At the moment there are three dogs, called Gizmo, Poppy and Pringle, and one cat, Tash.

Martin Ballard, who looks after the animals, said they bring 'so much joy to everyone', adding that the whole home benefits, not just the owners. 'As soon as you enter the front door, you're greeted with lots of wagging tails and happy dogs.'
