Bestselling philosophy writer Martin Cohen is returning to Hastings to launch his latest book on Wednesday 11 November.

Cohen, who has written more than 20 books that have sold over a million copies, returned to the East Sussex coast 18 months ago after years working in Australia and France. His new book, 'Israel-Palestine and The Case for One State', takes what the article calls an 'uncompromising look at the foundational philosophies of the Israeli state'.

The event, hosted by the Hastings & District Palestine Solidarity Campaign, will be held at Stade Hall in Hastings from 7pm. Readers will be able to hear from Cohen about the book, buy a copy and share an authentic Palestinian meal.

Cohen, who was born in Brighton, said: 'It is the journalist's responsibility not only to tell the truth but to make sense of events, and I felt this wasn't happening in the case of Gaza.'
