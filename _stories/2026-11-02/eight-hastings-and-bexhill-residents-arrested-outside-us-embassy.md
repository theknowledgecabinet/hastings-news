Eight residents of Hastings and Bexhill were arrested outside the US embassy in London on Saturday 31 October, the Hastings Online Times reported. The group was peacefully holding signs reading 'I oppose genocide. I support Palestine Action'.

The Metropolitan Police put the total number of arrests made that day at 325, most or all of which took place under near-identical circumstances, the article said.

The arrests came as the UK Supreme Court is due to hear an appeal by Palestine Action co-founder Huda Ammori on the legality of the ban on the organisation, on 4 and 5 November.
