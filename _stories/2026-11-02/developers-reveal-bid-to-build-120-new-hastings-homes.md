Catesby Estates and Rurban Estates have revealed a bid to build about 120 new homes in Hastings.

The developers say they are preparing plans for a care home and housing on land east of Beaney's Lane. A spokesperson described the vision as "a high quality, sustainable and well connected development providing a range of house types and sizes, including much needed affordable housing".

The proposal would include approximately 120 homes, of which 30 per cent would be affordable housing, together with a care home, a landscaped public open green space, a children's play area, and pedestrian and cycle routes.

The developers are preparing an outline planning application and are inviting the public to comment on the proposals before finalising them and submitting applications to Hastings Borough Council and Rother District Council, via a consultation at catesby-hastings.co.uk.
