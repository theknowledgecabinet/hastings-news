A new autumn ritual called 'Dusking' has reached the Hastings seafront, bringing morris dancing to the town's darkening days.

Invented by Leeds-based folk artist and morris dancer Lucy Wright as an autumn complement to May Day, the ritual has no rules: people dance or do their own thing as the sun goes down on Hallowe'en night.

Members of the Rheda's Dream morris side and friends gathered at the beach at the foot of London Road for the occasion, where they danced, sang and shared hot chocolate and soul cakes around a fire.

More 'dusking' took place at Hastings Castle on 1 November, the article said. Hastings, it notes, is always enthusiastic to welcome new rituals.
