Residents have branded large metal supports in St Leonards an 'eyesore'.

They were put up following the demolition of the Battle Road arches in Silverhill, which the borough council said cost more than £1.16 million. The structure, nicknamed The Cage, has been the subject of complaints for several years.

On the Hastings Observer's Facebook page, Judith Haffenden called it an "absolute eyesore", while Catherine Scott suggested "just put some big planted pots there and clean it up". Others proposed a market or community allotments.

A council spokesperson said the arches were demolished to prevent them collapsing into the road, and that the support structure remains in place for safety reasons. Because the land is privately owned, responsibility for its maintenance, including clearing litter and vegetation, rests with the landowner.
