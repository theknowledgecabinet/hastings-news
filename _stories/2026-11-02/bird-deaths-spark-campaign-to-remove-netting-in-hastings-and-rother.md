A new local campaign is calling for safer ways to manage birds around buildings after three incidents in which birds became trapped in netting.

Building Better for Birds was set up following incidents at Friston House and Waverley Court in St Leonards, Hastings Library, and B&Q at Ravenside, Bexhill. The group has secured a commitment from East Sussex County Council to remove the netting at Hastings Library, and has asked the council to review netting across its other buildings.

At the library, at least nine pigeons were found dead behind the netting. Seven bodies were recovered and two remained beyond reach, while a surviving pigeon, named Hattrick, was rescued with the help of Coussens Cranes and taken into the care of RSPCA Mallydams Wood.

David Walters, from the group, said its aim was to prevent further suffering and reduce the need for difficult rescues at height. Residents who have seen birds trapped in netting are asked to contact the group by email.
