Brendan Brain, 39, of Waterlooville, Hampshire, has been jailed for a spree of shoplifting and burglary that stretched across Sussex and ten other counties, Sussex Police said.

His offending ran from September 2023 to July 2025, with thefts including designer fragrances, Dyson products, candles, sunglasses and Nintendo Switch consoles, the force said. The total bill was over £31,000, with more than £3,500 stolen on one occasion in Sussex.

Brain pleaded guilty to 37 offences of theft and burglary. He was sentenced to three years and four months in prison and given a six-year Criminal Behaviour Order banning him from any Boots, Currys, John Lewis or Tesco store in England and Wales.
