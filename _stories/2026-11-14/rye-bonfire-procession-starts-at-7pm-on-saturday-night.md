Rye holds its annual bonfire celebrations on Saturday, with a torchlit procession starting at 7pm, according to a guide published by the local press.

A loud maroon signals the start of the procession from Mason Road, Tilling Green Estate, which winds through the town's cobbled streets before ending at the bonfire site on The Salts. The chairing down of Rye Fawkes at 7.40pm lights the big bonfire, followed by a fireworks display at around 8.15pm.

All streets on the processional route in Rye town centre close to traffic from 5.30pm to 8pm, and the A259 trunk road from 7pm until 10pm. Rye railway station closes from 3pm until services resume the following day.
