Southern services between Bexhill and Eastbourne have been disrupted by a signalling fault, The Argus has reported.

Travellers heading to Ore via Eastbourne were told to find alternative routes after the fault, and the line between Bexhill and Eastbourne was left affected.

The Argus reported the fault in a live feed that has since finished, so the duration of the disruption is not yet clear.
