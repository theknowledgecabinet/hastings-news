An 80-minute documentary about the Nova Convention in 1978 New York is coming to Hastings this weekend.

Nova '78, directed by Aaron Brookner with Rodrigo Areias, revisits the three-day celebration of the writer William S. Burroughs' forward-looking mindset. It is lovingly assembled from recently re-discovered 16mm footage shot by the director's uncle, Howard Brookner, who went on to make acclaimed documentaries before his death at the age of 34.

The film features Merce Cunningham, Philip Glass, Laurie Anderson and Patti Smith performing, with backstage exchanges that reveal the mood of the time. Ben Thompson of the Hastings Online Times called it "an atmospheric and thought-provoking" portrait of a high point of late seventies US counterculture.

It screens at the Electric Palace on Sunday 15 November and Thursday 19 November.
