More than £500,000 has been allocated to create a new community hub in Bexhill town centre, Rother District Council said.

A sum of £519,584 will go towards turning a disused building at 34 Devonshire Road into a flexible community space for activities, events, training and service delivery, the council said. The money comes from Rother's Community Infrastructure Levy (CIL) fund totalling £1.3 million, a charge paid on certain new developments to help fund infrastructure.

The award goes to Rother Voluntary Action. Councillor Teresa Killeen, chair of the Strategic CIL Allocations Panel, said the investment showed how developer contributions could be used to deliver lasting benefits for communities across Rother.
