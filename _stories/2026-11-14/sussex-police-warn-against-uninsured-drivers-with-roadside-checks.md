Sussex Police have warned motorists against uninsured driving, with officers carrying out roadside checks this week in the Eastbourne and Wealden area.

A force spokesperson said at least one person is killed by an uninsured driver every week in the UK. The force is continuing to work with the Motor Insurance Bureau (MIB) to identify and remove uninsured vehicles, it said.

Officers are also advising drivers to make sure they have the right cover in place.
