Hastings Borough Council has been awarded £288,500 from Historic England's Heritage at Risk Capital Fund for repairs to Hastings Castle.

The grant, combined with £200,000 from the council, will pay for urgent conservation work, including stabilising vulnerable sections of the curtain walls, conserving the tower wall, chapel arch and Collegiate Church remains, repairing the Ladies' Parlour wall, and addressing vegetation, masonry defects and other vulnerable historic features.

Cllr Julia Hilton, the council's deputy leader and lead councillor for shaping place, regeneration and community wealth building, said the castle was a key part of the town's and the country's heritage, and that the work was needed to protect both the scheduled monument and the public visiting the site.

Louise Brennan, director of regions at Historic England, said the funding would support urgent conservation needs while helping projects move towards sustainable futures.
