Police have launched a manhunt for a convicted prisoner with links to Sussex who escaped from a medical facility in South London on Friday.

The Metropolitan Police said Anthony Lloyd, 38, absconded from the unit in Lewisham at about 4.10pm on Friday after forcing his way out. He was last seen heading towards Catford, wearing a grey-blue T-shirt, black tracksuit bottoms and white trainers.

Detective Superintendent Melanie Pressley, who is leading the search, said officers were conducting various significant inquiries to find Lloyd and return him to custody. She said she was concerned he posed a risk both to himself and the public.

Officers believe he could be anywhere in London, but said he also has links to Sussex, Surrey, Thames Valley, Hampshire, Nottinghamshire, Derbyshire and Merseyside.

Lloyd was convicted in 2025 of actual bodily harm following an incident in Nottinghamshire involving three emergency workers. He was serving a 12-month custodial sentence and was transferred to the medical facility in September.
