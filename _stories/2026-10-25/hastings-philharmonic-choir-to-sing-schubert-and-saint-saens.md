The Hastings Philharmonic Choir's next concert, Songs of Light, brings two choral masterpieces to All Saints Church in the Old Town on Saturday 7 November.

The programme is built around Schubert's Mass in G and Saint-Saëns' Oratorio de Noël, the Christmas Oratorio. The choir will be joined by soloists including Beth Yates and Helen May (sopranos), Kerry Gentleman (contralto), John Twitchen (tenor) and Geoff Williams (baritone), with the strings of the Hastings Philharmonic Orchestra conducted by Marcio da Silva.

Both composers were young when they wrote the works. Schubert, aged 18, composed the Mass in G in six days in March 1815, and Saint-Saëns, 23, finished his oratorio in under two weeks before its first performance in Paris in December 1858.

Tickets range from free to £22.50 and are on sale via the choir's website, The Bookkeeper on Kings Road, St Leonards, and Bookbuster on Queens Road, Hastings.
