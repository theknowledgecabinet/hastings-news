The Hastings Storytelling Festival comes to a close on Sunday, October 25, with a free Children's Day on the Stade and a Gruffalo themed parade through the Old Town.

Produced by 18 Hours in partnership with the Hastings Storytelling Festival charity, this year's festival, themed Connections, has been running in venues across the town over recent days.

On Sunday, the Stade hosts puppetry, music, walkabout acts and creative workshops, including Pirate Gem's Swashbuckling Adventure show from CBeebies, Long Nose Puppets, Circo Rum Baba's Pesky Pirates and Mermaid Tales on giant seahorses.

Festival patron Axel Scheffler, the illustrator of The Gruffalo, has invited guest author and illustrator Tor Freeman to join the celebrations. A spokesperson for 18 Hours said everyone is welcome to dress up and join the parade, lining up from 2.45pm to parade with local drumming groups and hand painted costumes created by carnival artist Donna Fox.
