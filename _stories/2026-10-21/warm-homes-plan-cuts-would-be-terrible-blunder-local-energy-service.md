A local energy advice service has warned that cuts to the government's Warm Homes Plan would be a 'terrible blunder' for households across East Sussex.

The Hastings Online Times reports the government is set to slash funding for the plan in next week's budget, following a Guardian report that ministers were 'preparing to slash the amount of money available', including hundreds of millions of pounds from the Boiler Upgrade Scheme, which helps households switch from gas boilers to cleaner heating such as heat pumps.

Energise Sussex Coast, which runs a free energy advice service across the county, says the cut would hurt households, the climate and national security. Its director, Kate Meakin, said that in the midst of the current gas crisis it was worse for the government to cut funding for a plan that would reduce Britain's exposure to fossil fuel markets.

The move has also been criticised by the innovation charity Nesta, which said it would 'take the wind out of an industry at a critical stage of development'.
