Hastings artist Helen Slater will launch her book Uninvited on the afternoon of Sunday 25 October, hosted by The Peripheral Space Project.

Ms Slater, who works in acrylic, began painting on 31 July, the day she was told she had breast cancer. Colour, texture and abstract form became a way to express what she described as a cascade of words no one expected to hear: tumour, scans, hormone blockers, operation.

She kept creating throughout her experience, producing 21 paintings and a short diary that began as private acts of processing and calm. They were first shared at The Peripheral Space in November 2025, an event where the idea for the book was born.

Uninvited gathers the work into an illustrated collection exploring fear, vulnerability, hope, resilience and gratitude. Erica Smith, who worked with Helen on the design of the book, calls it 'a remarkable combination of words and her paintings'.
