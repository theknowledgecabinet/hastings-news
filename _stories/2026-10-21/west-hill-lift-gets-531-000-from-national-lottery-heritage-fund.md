The National Lottery Heritage Fund has offered £531,000 in development funding to Hastings Borough Council to secure the long-term future of the West Hill Lift, the Grade II listed lift that runs up the cliff from George Street to Hastings Castle.

The money will pay for the detailed design work and planning consents the council says it needs before making a further bid for funding to fully restore the lift, which requires major work to improve accessibility and reliability.

A council spokesperson indicated the full restoration is likely to cost around £3.8 million, 'but it very much depends on what the development stage shows'.

Cllr Julia Hilton, deputy leader of the council and lead councillor for regeneration, said the future of the lift is 'a vital part' of the Hastings Castle project funded by the Town Deal, and thanked the Heritage Fund and National Lottery players for the grant.
