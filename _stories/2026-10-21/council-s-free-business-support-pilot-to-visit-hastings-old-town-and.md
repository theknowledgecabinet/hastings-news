A new free business support pilot will reach independent shops and businesses in selected East Sussex high streets, including Hastings Old Town and Rye, the county council said.

According to an East Sussex County Council spokesperson, the pilot brings free, practical one-to-one support directly to independent businesses in Rye, Seaford, Newhaven, Mayfield, Eastbourne Old Town and Hastings Old Town. Businesses can receive tailored advice and a clear action plan focused on their own priorities, with support on topics including sales, customer loyalty, marketing, pricing, profitability and making better use of retail space.

The outreach team will visit businesses directly, the council said. The programme is funded by HM Government, East Sussex County Council and the East Sussex Growth Hub, and is managed by the county council and delivered by Smarter Society.
