The Hastings Old Town Carnival Association has donated more than £5,000 to local charities after a successful Old Town Week.

A total of 25 local groups received donations at an evening held at the East Hastings Sea Angling Club on Sunday November 15. Among those to benefit were the Hastings RNLI, local scouts, the St Mary in the Castle Project, the Fishermen's Museum, the Y Centre and two local twirler groups.

Carnival chairman Reg Wood said: "Having had a very successful Old Town Week this year we were able to donate over £5,000 to local good causes."

He added: "It was a lovely evening and so rewarding being able to support so many deserving organisations."
