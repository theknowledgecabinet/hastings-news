A Hastings housing co-operative has announced it is to be dissolved, having been unable to continue.

Maslow Housing Co-operative, based at 5 Harold Place in the town centre, was launched in August 2025, a housing project the co-operative said was five years in the making. Sharon Lisa Rhodes was behind its creation.

A statement on its Facebook page, posted on November 11, said: "This really is the end, as we are unable to continue the hub at 5 Harold Place and will be vacating the premises."

The group said it had met at Hastings Voluntary Action to finalise details of dissolving the organisation, adding that "everyone involved housing situation-wise is sorted and nobody has been made homeless." It described "so much red tape" and said it could not afford its advisor, whose support cost £1,000 per 24 hours. The statement said Ms Dollimore, MP for Hastings and Rye, tried to help, and thanked Cllr Glenn Haffenden and John Whittington of Hastings Borough Council for trying to save the hub.

The co-operative said it had housed five people in a year and had been a meeting place for support workers and clients in the town.
