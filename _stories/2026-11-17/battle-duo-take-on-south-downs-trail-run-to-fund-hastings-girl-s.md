Two bricklayers from Battle are taking on the South Downs Trail Run on 28 November to help an 11-year-old Hastings gymnast continue her rehabilitation.

Scarlett Stockley collapsed during gymnastics in May 2024 after a brain bleed caused by a ruptured arteriovenous malformation, and has since had two life-saving surgeries, followed by seven more over the following year. She has been left with left-sided weakness and foot drop, and her family is paying around £200 a week for specialist therapy sessions, more than £10,000 a year.

Mike Collard, 38, and his stepson Finley Kent, 18, are running the trail after hearing about Scarlett's progress from Mike's partner, who worked as a teaching assistant at her school. The family is being supported by children's charity Tree of Hope, with a specialist intensive therapy programme for 2027 as its goal.
