Mulberry Academy Bexhill has launched Ready Set Perform, a weekly performing arts class open to any child in Years 4 to 7, whatever school they attend.

The sessions are run by Keren Moore, a performing arts teacher trained at the BRIT School, who has performed on Britain's Got Talent at the London Palladium and recorded at Abbey Road Studios. They take place every Wednesday in term time from 5pm to 7pm. Families can try the first two sessions free, then pay £15 a session, billed per term.

'We have so much opportunity for our own performers here at the academy, and we wanted to extend that to our wider community,' said Matt Lewis, Associate Assistant Headteacher and Head of Music.
