Fiona Yurteri, Home Manager at Care UK's Southlands Place on Hastings Road in Bexhill-on-Sea, has been shortlisted for the Leader of the Year Award at the East Sussex Care Awards 2026.

The category recognises those who deliver outstanding team leadership and high standards of care for residents. Ms Yurteri has introduced an open-door policy and person-led care throughout the home, as well as a run of 'Silly Awards' celebrating colleagues, alongside team-building activities such as picnics and cultural days.

'I'm very thankful to have been nominated for such a prestigious award. I'm proud of all we do at Southlands Place and feel incredibly lucky to do a job that is fulfilling,' she said.
