A property company and its sole director have been fined over fire safety breaches at premises in Hastings.

East Sussex Fire and Rescue Service said it brought action against J&R Real Estate and Mr Jagmit Singh Ahudja, its sole director, following offences under the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005.

The fire service said the prosecution related to premises at York Gardens, part of the Queens Arcade, after an ESFRS investigation into fire safety arrangements there. A fire safety inspector exercised statutory powers under Article 27 of the order, which allow inspectors to enter and inspect premises and require documents. The court heard Mr Ahudja, as freeholder, failed without reasonable excuse to provide documents and information required by the inspector.

Both J&R Real Estate and Mr Ahudja were found guilty at Brighton and Hove Magistrates' Court on October 6. Following a hearing on November 9, they were ordered to pay a total of £20,500 in fines and costs, the fire service said.
