Hastings council has confirmed plans to freeze most pay-and-display parking charges for 2027/28, following three years of increases. Cabinet members backed the proposals on Monday (9 November) ahead of a full council vote.

The freeze extends to most sports court and pitch hire, while longer-term parking permits are still expected to rise, by varying amounts depending on the ticket and location. Council beach huts and chalets would go up by 3.8 per cent, and commercial event fees by a 2.8 per cent inflationary increase.

Cabinet member for finance Councillor Darren Mackenzie (Green) said the council 'has deliberately avoided a blanket increase', with more than 600 individual charges reviewed. The plan is expected to raise about £7.4 million in 2027/28, a slight fall on this year's income, the council said.
