A St Leonards boarding school has put forward plans to build an outdoor sports facility on a property it owns.

Claremont School has submitted a planning application to Hastings Borough Council for a multi-use games area and associated works at Clyde House, in Sedlescombe Road North.

A design and access statement by RSA Architecture, on behalf of the application, describes an outdoor games area "nestled in the rear garden" that could be used throughout the year. It says the remaining grassed areas would be re-wilded with a meadow and new trees, improving the ecology.

The school bought Clyde House shortly after it closed in 2015 and converted it into residential accommodation. The statement says the building houses five staff members and 64 students aged 13 to 18 during term times.

The application says the games area has been designed to be low impact and non-intrusive to neighbouring properties.
