An application for an alcohol licence for a new tea room and wine bar in Rye is to be considered by Rother District Council.

A licensing panel is due to hear a premises licence application for 13 West Street, a Grade II listed building in the Rye Citadel, at a hearing on Tuesday, December 1.

The application, submitted by The Great British Wine Company Ltd, seeks on- and off-sales of alcohol between 10am and 8pm from a business trading as the Mermaidian Rye Tea Rooms.

The hearing has been called following objections from a number of local residents, the council said. A report to the panel said the representations relate principally to the premises being in a predominantly residential area, raising matters including noise, deliveries, refuse storage, customer congregation outside and the narrow pavements and road layout around the building.

The report also noted the building has planning permission as a traditional tea room from a 1992 application, and that a 2014 application to use it as a wine bar was withdrawn before a decision was made.
