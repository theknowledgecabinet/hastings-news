Police are urgently searching for a 39-year-old woman who is missing from St Leonards.

Sussex Police issued a social media appeal shortly before midnight. The post described Kim as about 5ft 7in, with blonde shoulder-length hair, likely worn in a ponytail or a bun.

She was last seen on Monday afternoon (November 16) at around 2.15pm, wearing a grey tracksuit and Crocs, the appeal said.

Police said Kim has links in St Leonards, but could be using public transport to travel.

Anyone who has seen her should call 999 quoting reference 1308 of 16/11.
