A former convent in St Leonards that was damaged in a major fire earlier this summer is going on the market, with a guide price of £1.7 million and a potential major housing scheme for the land.

The Convent of the Holy Child of Jesus in Magdalen Road is being offered by auctioneer Allsop, with the vacant freehold site under the hammer on 16 December.

The sale follows a fire on 9 July which police treated as arson, at which eight fire engines were called and two teenage boys were later arrested, the Argus reports. The site covers about 11.7 acres and includes the former convent and chapel, a Gothic building, a gateway lodge and a gateway, with structures listed at Grade II and Grade II*. The Grade II* chapel was not affected by the fire.

According to the sale documents, the land is allocated for a heritage, landscape and residential-led regeneration with an indicative capacity of 200 homes. Any development would have to secure repair and restoration of the listed buildings and protect the nuns' cemetery. Hastings and Rye MP Helena Dollimore said the community was 'deeply distressed' by the fire.
