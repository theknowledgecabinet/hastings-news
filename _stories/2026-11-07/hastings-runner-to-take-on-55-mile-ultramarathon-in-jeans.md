A 21-year-old from Hastings is about to take on one of the country's toughest challenges, and he is doing it in denim.

Peter Nagle, 21, will run 55 miles across the South Downs in a pair of jeans next month to raise money for men's mental health. On 5 December he will tackle UK Ultra's Winter on the Downs, a single-loop race with more than 7,000ft of climbing. That is over two marathons back to back, the Hastings Observer reports, in actual jeans rather than just a layer of denim over running kit.

The jeans come from The Perfect Jean, a New York-based stretch denim brand that has backed the challenge and will follow his training.

All the money raised goes to Andy's Man Club, a men's mental health charity that runs free, judgement-free talking groups for men across the UK every Monday at 7pm. Peter, who previously ran the Hastings Half Marathon in jeans for the same cause, said: 'If this gets one person talking to a mate who's struggling, that matters just as much as any donation.'
