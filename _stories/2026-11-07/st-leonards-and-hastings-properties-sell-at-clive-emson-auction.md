A mixed-use property at 32 Kings Road, St Leonards, has been sold before the auction in one of the latest Clive Emson Auctioneers sales, which listed 174 lots across southern England.

The freehold had been offered with a guide price of £310,000 to £320,000 at the auction, which ended on Thursday 5 November, but was sold in advance. It comprises two ground floor commercial units and two separately accessed residential flats above, all let under a full repair and insuring lease for a 16-year term at £33,282.64 per annum, ending in February next year.

Auction appraiser Jade Flood said the property was in a prominent position on Kings Road, "within easy reach of local shops, amenities and the seafront", and that her buyer agreed by completing the purchase ahead of the sale.

At the same auction, a four-storey mixed-use property at 187 Queens Road, Hastings, went under the hammer at £178,000 freehold, exceeding its guide price. It comprises ground floor retail accommodation with a mezzanine storage room, a maisonette over three upper floors, plus basement accommodation and a rear yard.

Director and senior auction appraiser Sam Kinloch said there had been "considerable pre-sale interest" in the property, which "translated into competitive bidding".
