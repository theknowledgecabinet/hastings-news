Hastings swimmer Sarah Milne has completed an endurance training camp in Dover with The King's Swimmers and, in her latest diary for the Hastings Independent Press, says she is now preparing for a solo Channel crossing attempt in 2028.

During the week-long camp she swum 35 kilometres back and forth from Swimmer's Beach near the ferry port. Kevin Murphy, one of The King's Swimmers' directors and coaches, told her that he and the team believed she could swim the Channel solo in 2028, and offered to write her weekly training plans.

Ms Milne had not swum proper front crawl in years before May, when she discovered Fish 2 Water open-water training in Eastbourne. She has since joined Hastings Seagulls Masters, and is now in her second week of longer pool sessions three times a week, alongside back-to-back weekend endurance swims.
