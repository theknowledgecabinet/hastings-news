A pub in Rye has been named among the best in the country, with recognition for what the guide calls an unpretentious country pub.

The Plough was named in the top 100 of The Good Food Guide's 2026 list of the country's best pubs. The guide ranked it at number 38, the highest in Sussex, and described it as 'an unpretentious country pub noted for its seasonal, locally sourced food'.

It won high marks for uniqueness, deliciousness and strength of recommendation, and was rated 'very good' for warmth.

Set on a rural stretch of road three miles from Rye, the pub is praised for its 'glorious' location, a garden where, in fine weather, 'sheep baa-ing while you eat' beckons, and 'scuffed floorboards, plain tables and wood-burners' inside. Paintings by Sussex artist William Warden are said to 'bring a lovely local connection to the venue'.
