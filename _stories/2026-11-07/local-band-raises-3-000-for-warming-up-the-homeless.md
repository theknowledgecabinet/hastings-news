A group of local musicians have raised around £3,000 for Warming Up the Homeless in the last couple of years by giving their CDs away free and encouraging audiences to donate to the charity instead.

The Other Band, which has been playing for over a decade, performs an eclectic mix of Americana from many different decades. It consists of Julian Graff on mandolin, guitar and vocals, and Lee Hill on guitar, harmonica and vocals, with frequent guest musicians Dave Fricker on bass and Stu Alexander on accordion.

Warming Up the Homeless is a registered frontline charity supporting homeless people on the South Coast from Hastings to Eastbourne. Operating since 2015, it supports over 1,400 men, women and children a week, providing daily outreach support, food banks and welfare advocacy.

The arrangement means the band gets its music heard while the charity benefits from the donations, according to a feature in the Hastings Independent Press. The Albatross Club in Bexhill and Barnaby's Lounge in Hastings have been particularly supportive, promoting both the band and the charity.
