Bexhill Artists' Workspace is holding its Christmas Makers' Fair at The Studio, De La Warr Pavilion, Bexhill, on Saturday 7 November.

The fair will run from 10am to 4pm. Items on sale include watercolours, ceramics, fused glass, textiles, children's books, bead jewellery and Christmas decorations, according to the Sussex Express.

Admission is free and there is disabled access. The 99 bus stops right outside the Pavilion.
