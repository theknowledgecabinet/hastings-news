A village pub that has been closed for years could reopen with three new guest rooms, after renovation plans were submitted to Rother District Council.

The Bell Inn Iden Community Benefit Society has applied to refurbish the Grade II-listed pub in Iden, near Rye, The Argus reports. The former landlord's accommodation on the first floor would become three en suite guest rooms, while two existing letting rooms in the garden barn would be retained. The plans also include repairs to the building, a new fire escape door and external staircase, a single-storey timber-clad toilet extension, solar panels on the outbuilding roof and an air source heat pump to replace the gas boiler.

The pub closed in 2023 after tenants had lost money, and the previous owners said it was financially unviable. Residents formed the Save The Bell group after a village meeting in August 2023. A sale price of £425,000 was agreed in December 2025, and the society bought the pub in March 2026, having raised £312,000 through a community appeal that included £291,000 in share investment and £21,000 in donations.

The society aims to reopen the pub in summer 2027, with planning permission and listed building consent still being sought.
