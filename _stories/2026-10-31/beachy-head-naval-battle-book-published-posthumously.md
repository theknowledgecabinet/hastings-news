The final book from late Shoreham-based lawyer Quintin Barry, Beachy Head 1690: The Grand Alliance at Bay, has been published after his death in January 2025, aged 88.

According to the Hastings Observer, Barry was born in Worthing and educated at Eastbourne College. He qualified as a solicitor in 1958 and in 1970 led the merger of three Brighton firms to create Donne Mileham & Haddock, now DMH Stallard, serving as managing partner until 1988.

In retirement he published 20 books, mainly military history. His friend and former colleague Martin Allen, chairman of Shoreham's Ropetackle, said Barry had a lifelong interest in military history and that the final book continues his work on naval conflicts of the 18th and 19th centuries.
