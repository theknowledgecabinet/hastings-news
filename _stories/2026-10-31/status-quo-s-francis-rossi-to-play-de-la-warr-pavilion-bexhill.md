Francis Rossi is to bring his solo Status Quo show to Bexhill this month. The guitarist will perform his Songbook: Quo – Past, Present, Future tour at the De La Warr Pavilion on Tuesday, November 10, the Sussex Express reports.

The set will include hand-picked songs from Status Quo's evergreen classics plus some deep cuts, and Rossi said he is also working up a new medley. "The pressure is on once more as I have put together a really packed new show for autumn of this year," he said.

He will be joined on tour by guitarist Andy Brook, who co-produced the 2026 album The Accidental with Rossi and appeared on the sold-out 2025 Songbook tour.
