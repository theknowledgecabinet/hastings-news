A new book by locally based author Elizabeth Webb has been reviewed by Michael Salmon in the Hastings Online Times. Dexter's Lot, published by BiblioMuso, follows its protagonist as he stumbles "from the exciting world of rock music to the mundane world of the supermarket," enduring many trials along the way.

Salmon describes it as an "exciting romp" that contrasts Dexter's extrovert, hedonistic nature with his vulnerability, and says he would like a sequel. The book is available in paperback at £9.95 on eBay and from local bookshops: Bookbuster on Queens Road in Hastings, Printed Matter on Queens Road, and The Bookkeeper on Kings Road in St Leonards.

It will also be available on buythebook from Friday, 30 October, the review says.
