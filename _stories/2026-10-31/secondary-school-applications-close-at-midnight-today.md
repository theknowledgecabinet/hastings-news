Parents and carers in East Sussex have until midnight today to submit applications for secondary school places for September 2027.

Cllr Paul Soane, the county council's lead member for education, said last year 95 per cent of children were allocated a place at one of their preferred schools, but some families missed out because they applied after the deadline.

Applicants can choose up to three schools and use the council's online mapping tool to search for schools in their community area. Anyone who needs help can visit a local library, email admissions@eastsussex.gov.uk, or call 0300 33 09 472 between 10am and 3pm, Monday to Friday.
