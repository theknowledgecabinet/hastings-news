A group of 18 young people under 19 have taken part in a six-day film course in St Leonards, making an original short film from scratch as part of the BFI Film Academy South East Introductory Course.

The programme, part of a national push to attract fresh talent to the industry, is delivered locally by The Bright Foundation, an arts education charity set up by Academy Award-winning costume designer John Bright, and taught by industry professionals including writing mentor Trix Worrall, creator of the Channel 4 show Desmond's, who live in Hastings.

The course covered scriptwriting, direction, acting, production, post-production and costume and prop design, with no previous film experience required. The film, called Going Out West, is a dystopian thriller in which AI has taken over the world, with the script mainly written by a Rye College student, and team members included a student at East Sussex College's Hastings campus working as assistant director.

Bright Foundation CEO Polly Risbridger said the course hands over creative control to the young people, who 'literally take on every role, learning all the skills along the way'.
