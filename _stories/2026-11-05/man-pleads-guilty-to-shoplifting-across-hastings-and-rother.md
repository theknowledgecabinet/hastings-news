A 45-year-old man from Winchelsea Beach has been charged in connection with a series of shop theft offences across Hastings and Rother.

His arrest and charges follow an investigation by local officers. Hastings Police said the charges relate to the theft of approximately £100 worth of clothing from Marks & Spencer in Hastings, the theft of approximately £330 worth of food and alcohol from Jempson's in Rye, and the theft of approximately £261 worth of food from Jempson's in Peasmarsh.

He appeared before Hastings Magistrates' Court on 27 October 2026, where he pleaded guilty to three counts of shoplifting. He was granted unconditional bail to allow for the preparation of pre-sentencing reports.

A Hastings Police spokesperson said: 'Shop theft has a significant impact on local businesses, staff and the wider community.' The force said it will continue to work closely with retailers across Hastings and Rother.
