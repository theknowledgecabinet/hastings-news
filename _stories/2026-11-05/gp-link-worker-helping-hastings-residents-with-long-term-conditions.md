Active Hastings' specialised 'Link Worker' is helping residents with long-term health conditions become more active, bridging the gap between healthcare and community-based activity, according to the Hastings Independent Press.

The role was created in 2022. It was originally funded by the Hastings and St Leonards Primary Care Network through to 2026 and is delivered in partnership with Hastings Borough Council and East Sussex County Council.

A simplified referral system lets GPs and other health professionals refer people to Active Hastings, opening up a wide range of activity opportunities across Hastings rather than a single activity, with the link worker matching each person to what suits their needs.

Jas, the GP link worker, has introduced new sessions where provision was lacking, including chair-based yoga, seated strength and gym and circuit sessions for people with neurological conditions, and helped set up 'safe' exercise spaces for women. One 56-year-old woman living with chronic back pain, who had felt disconnected from activities she once enjoyed, is now back into regular yoga.
