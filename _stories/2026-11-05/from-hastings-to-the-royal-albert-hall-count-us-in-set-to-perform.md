Fifty-one young musicians from Hastings and St Leonards will take the stage at the Royal Albert Hall on 10 November as part of the Music for Youth Proms.

They will join more than 1,000 young performers from across the UK and are the only group from East Sussex selected to perform. All are members of Count Us IN Community Music, a programme run by ExploreTheArch and supported by Youth Music that provides free instruments, equipment and tuition.

Count Us IN began within the Four Courts Connect community programme on Stonehouse Drive and now reaches 11 schools and colleges across the area. Music for Youth waived the group's participation fees in recognition of the barriers many participants face, and the trip has been helped by the Chalk Cliff Trust.
