A disqualified driver has been jailed for 13 months after leading police on a 31-minute pursuit through Hastings, Winchelsea and Rye that ended in a ditch in Iden.

Alexander Williams, 31, of Bembrook Road, Hastings, was sentenced at Lewes Crown Court on 28 October. He had been indicated to stop in The Ridge, Hastings, and crashed off the road through an apple orchard as the chase ended. A police stinger device had deflated his tyres before the black Range Rover came to rest.

Police said he was travelling at up to 104mph in a 60mph zone in wet conditions. He was also given 4 years and 5 months of a driving ban. Police said he had been disqualified only 18 days before the incident on 28 September.

Williams told police he had fled because he was on probation. He had also been caught driving while disqualified before, the court heard.
