The family of a six-year-old Bexhill boy who died of a brain condition has featured in a new film as part of a campaign to show the vital role of hospices.

Kai was supported by Chestnut Tree House for several years until he died just before Christmas 2025. His mums, Leanne and Sara, describe how the hospice helped them make memories over his life, from hydrotherapy to a summer festival and dry-slope skiing.

Kai's story is part of the Show Your Love appeal by Southern Hospice Group, which also runs Martlets in Hove and St Barnabas House in Worthing. The charity said just 14 per cent of its costs are covered by NHS grants, with funding relying heavily on local communities.
