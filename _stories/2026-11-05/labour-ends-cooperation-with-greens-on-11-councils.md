Labour has withdrawn from power-sharing agreements with Green councillors in 11 local authorities, including Arun, Lewes and Wealden district councils in Sussex, over the party's ‘Zionism is Racism’ policy.

The Labour Party's national executive committee has ordered its councillors to end cooperation with the Greens in those 11 councils. The move follows an October ultimatum aimed at 18 councils; cooperation is to continue in Rother District Council, among others, according to the Hastings Online Times.

Labour chair Bridget Phillipson said: “Labour has been clear from the start that the ‘Zionism is Racism’ policy crosses a line and we cannot work with any party that supports it – at any level of government.” Groups with cooperation arrangements were asked to seek a “clear and unambiguous public statement” from the Greens rejecting the policy.

The Green Party said it was “hugely disappointed in moves designed by national parties to force local council leaders into jeopardising successful progressive partnerships that are delivering benefits for residents.”
