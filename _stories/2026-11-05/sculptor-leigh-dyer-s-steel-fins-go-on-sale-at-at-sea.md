The metal sculptor behind several well-known works around Hastings has opened a new line for sale at the At Sea gallery in the Old Town.

Leigh Dyer made the commemorative winkle on Winkle Island, the Bale House Canopy at Hastings Country Park, the shiny snakes on the outsized chess pieces in George Street and the boat sculpture sunk into Pelham Beach, according to the Hastings Independent Press.

At Sea, an art gallery gift shop that opened in July at 52 George Street, is showing Dyer's work, which is not generally made for public sale, alongside a group of mainly Sussex-based painters and sculptors. Twelve steel fins, made of crib steel and about a foot high, are on sale there.

“I want the work I make in Hastings to help people see the beauty of the place, perhaps with fresh eyes,” Dyer said. “The fins were enjoyable to make and have sold quickly, I'll no doubt be making a new edition soon.”
