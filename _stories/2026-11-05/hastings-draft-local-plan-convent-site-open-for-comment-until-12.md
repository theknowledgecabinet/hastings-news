The deadline for the public to comment on the Hastings Borough Council's Draft Local Plan is Thursday 12 November, and one of the most argued-over parts of it is what happens to the Convent of the Holy Child Jesus in Lower Bohemia.

The convent caught fire in July 2026. For months beforehand, residents made complaints to the council about the site's vulnerability, the Hastings Online Times reports. The Draft Local Plan now proposes demolishing the East Wing, the red and white building near White Rock Gardens, to make way for more and higher-density housing.

Under the process known as Regulation 19, the public have six weeks to comment on the plan, which will be finalised in June 2028 and will guide planning in the borough until 2041. The Save the Convent group has put forward its own proposals for the site.
