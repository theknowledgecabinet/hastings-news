The West Hill Lift has been closed for essential surveys and repairs, according to the Hastings Independent Press.

The funicular railway, which opened in 1891 and retains its original wooden Victorian coaches, runs through a tunnel, which makes maintenance particularly difficult.

It is one of the town's 'crown jewels' and a popular tourist attraction, with scenic views stretching round to Beachy Head in the west and out across the English Channel.

A Hastings Borough Council spokesperson apologised for the inconvenience and said the council could not provide a timeline for when it will reopen, due to the nature of the works.
