Mountside Care Home in Hastings has been rated 'Good' in all five areas assessed by the Care Quality Commission.

The home on Laton Road, part of ACI Care, achieved 'Good' for being safe, effective, caring, responsive and well-led, as well as its overall 'Good' rating.

The home manager, Lisa, said: "I am incredibly proud of our team. Achieving Good in all five areas is a wonderful recognition of the care, effort and dedication colleagues bring to Mountside every day."
