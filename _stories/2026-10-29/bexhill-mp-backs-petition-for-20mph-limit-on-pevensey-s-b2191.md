The MP for Bexhill, Battle and the villages has backed a petition calling for a 20mph speed limit and weight restrictions on heavy goods vehicles through Pevensey and Westham.

Dr Kieran Mullan met local campaigners during a recent visit to Pevensey to discuss concerns about road safety and the impact of heavy traffic on the area's historic buildings, according to the Hastings Observer.

The petition, supported by Pevensey and Westham Parish Councils, The Mint House, English Heritage and local county and district councillors, asks East Sussex County Council to introduce the restrictions along the B2191, from Pevensey High Street to Westham High Street. Cllr Peter Lowton, chairman of Pevensey Parish Council, said the area was "suffering from more and more traffic", citing the Mint House's Tudor wall paintings and the 1,700-year-old walls of Pevensey Castle.
