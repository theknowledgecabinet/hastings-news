Conor O'Brien from Hastings has organised a 75km run from Brighton Pier to Hastings Pier in memory of his father.

The RUN4SMH event for St Michael's Hospice, which is based in St Leonards, takes place on 13 February 2027, with a group of 10 running the entire distance and others joining for stints along the way, O'Brien said.

His father, John, was 70 when diagnosed with oesophageal cancer in February 2025 and died in March this year after receiving care at the hospice. O'Brien said he first raised £1,700 for the hospice in a week by entering the Hastings Half Marathon. Further donations can be made through the JustGiving page.
