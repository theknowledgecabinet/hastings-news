Steve Fisher of P A Fisher & Son Family Butchers in Hastings is publishing a calendar featuring 12 independent butchers from across Great Britain to raise money for Mental Health UK.

Fisher said the idea started after he saw a Facebook post by Shaun of Fairweather's Butchers and Deli, and he wanted to shine a spotlight on independent butchers as traditional shops disappear from high streets.

Calendars are £10 each, with 1,200 being produced and 100 going to each participating butcher, and can be reserved directly from the shop. Fisher, who is now over four years sober, said he chose Mental Health UK because it was "something incredibly personal to me", and hoped his openness would encourage others to speak up about their struggles.
