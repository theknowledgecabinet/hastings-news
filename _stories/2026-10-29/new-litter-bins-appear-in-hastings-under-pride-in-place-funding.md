New litter bins are being installed around Hastings as part of the government's Pride in Place scheme, which saw the town awarded £20 million.

Projects are chosen by the Neighbourhood Funding Board, a panel of local residents chaired by Kevin Boorman, under three categories: up to £250,000 a year for public realm improvements such as bins, bus shelters and benches; up to £500,000 a year for community and leisure facilities; and up to £1 million a year for heritage restorations from year two.

Bins are already in place near The Stade in the Old Town, displaying the Pride in Place logo, with more on the way. Mr Boorman said people told the board they wanted cleaner streets. MP Helena Dollimore said more bins and cleaning equipment were coming, with vans later in the year emptying seafront bins more often and removing graffiti.
