Business representatives in Hastings have welcomed the government's package of rates relief for small businesses, announced by the chancellor, John Healey, in his Autumn Budget on Wednesday.

The Treasury said the package includes an extra 20 per cent relief for pubs, social clubs and all but the largest live music venues, in addition to support introduced earlier in 2026, bringing the total relief to 35 per cent. It said this support will be provided in 2027/28 and 2028/29. The Treasury also raised the Small Business Rates Relief thresholds for qualifying retail, hospitality and leisure properties to £18,000 for full relief and £21,000 for tapered relief.

John Bownas, manager of Love Hastings Ltd, which represents town centre businesses, said the group "definitely welcomes this news for many of our smaller shops and hospitality businesses", with the savings for some being "quite significant – thousands of pounds a year". Looking at the Business Improvement District area, he said there could be around 72 town centre shops seeing savings of around £250k collectively, rising to closer to £300k if the taper that currently applies extends to those with rateable values over £18,000.
