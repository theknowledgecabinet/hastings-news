Plans to convert the former Lloyds Bank building in Bexhill town centre into two restaurants and eight apartments have been unveiled.

Bozyel Construction Ltd has submitted the proposals to Rother District Council. The scheme involves turning the empty building at 11-13 Devonshire Road into two restaurant units at ground floor level, eight one- to two-bedroom apartments on the upper floors, and a rooftop extension.

A design and access statement from RX Architects, on behalf of the applicant, said the building occupies a prominent corner site close to Bexhill station and the seafront. The proposal aims to retain an active commercial frontage while bringing the vacant upper floors back into residential use, working within the constraints of the existing building and minimising intervention to its external appearance.

Lloyds Bank closed the Devonshire Road branch in September 2024 after it said visits had fallen over recent years.
