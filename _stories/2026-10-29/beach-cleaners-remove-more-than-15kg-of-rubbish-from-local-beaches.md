The Strandliners ecology group has collected more than 15 kilogrammes of plastic and rubbish from beaches at Hastings, Bexhill and Pett Level this year.

Volunteers took part in the sessions as part of the Great British Beach Clean for the Marine Conservation Society. The clean-ups also included a survey of what washes up on the beaches, and the litter data recorded by citizen scientists can drive ocean conservation campaigns and policy changes at local and national levels.

Unusual items found in the surveys included a sweet packet from Taiwan, a complete Bal-Ban egg from a 2007 container spill, and a message in a bottle from an Irish ferry.

The Marine Conservation Society said 4,126 volunteers removed a total of 2,231 kg of litter across the UK and Channel Islands during the ten-day campaign.
