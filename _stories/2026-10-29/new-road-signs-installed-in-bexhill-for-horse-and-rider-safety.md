New road signs have been installed in Bexhill as part of a campaign to make roads safer for horses and riders, a town councillor said.

Warning signs have been placed on Peartree Lane and St Mary's Lane, roads frequently used by horses and riders, Cllr Connor Winter said.

He said the signs serve as a reminder to motorists to reduce speed where appropriate, remain patient and give horses and riders sufficient space when passing.

"This was a commitment I made to residents during my election campaign, and I'm pleased to be delivering on it through continued work with the county and town councils," he said.
