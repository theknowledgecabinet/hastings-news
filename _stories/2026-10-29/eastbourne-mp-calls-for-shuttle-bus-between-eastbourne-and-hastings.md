Eastbourne MP Josh Babarinde has called for a dedicated shuttle bus between District General Hospital in Eastbourne and the Conquest Hospital in Hastings.

Mr Babarinde said the government "continues to drag its heels" over funding for a new hospital in Eastbourne. The Conservative government in 2020 pledged to build 40 new hospitals by 2035, including a rebuild of District General Hospital, while the Labour government revised the scheme in 2025, instead pledging to spend between £1.5 billion and £2 billion on refurbishing buildings at East Sussex Healthcare NHS Trust's hospitals.

"In the meantime, we need practical solutions to improve access to healthcare," Mr Babarinde said. He said getting between the two main hospital sites by public transport can take hours, forcing many residents to rely on expensive taxis to attend appointments, and called on the government to work with the NHS trust and local bus operators to make journeys between the hospitals "quicker, more reliable and more affordable for patients, visitors and NHS staff".

The call came before the autumn Budget was delivered on Wednesday.
