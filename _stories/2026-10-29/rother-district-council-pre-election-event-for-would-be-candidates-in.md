Rother District Council is holding an online pre-election event for people considering standing as a councillor in May 2027.

Following the announcement that Local Government Reorganisation in East Sussex is paused and under review, the council said local elections scheduled for May 2027 are going ahead using the current district and borough boundaries, with all 38 seats on Rother District Council to be contested on Thursday 6 May 2027.

The session, via Microsoft Teams, takes place on Tuesday 24 November 2026 at 6pm. It is open to anyone considering standing, regardless of background or experience, and will hear from current councillors and senior officers about the role and the support available. Councillor Gareth Delany, chair of the Member Development Task Group, said becoming a district councillor was one of the best ways of influencing decisions about the community.

Places can be reserved by emailing democraticservices@rother.gov.uk by Friday 20 November 2026, and the session will be recorded and put on the council's website for those who cannot attend.
