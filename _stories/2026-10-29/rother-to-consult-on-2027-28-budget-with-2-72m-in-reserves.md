Residents of Rother are set to be consulted on how the council will balance its 2027/28 budget, after Cabinet agreed on 26 October to consult staff, the public and, for the first time, individual organisations likely to be affected by savings decisions.

The council's general reserves stand at £2.72m, and it said it will need to increase discretionary fees and charges, increase council tax and make savings to deliver a balanced budget.

Cllr Doug Oliver, the council's leader, said extending the consultation to outside bodies was the right thing to do, helping the council make "an informed decision" that takes into account the views of communities across Rother.
