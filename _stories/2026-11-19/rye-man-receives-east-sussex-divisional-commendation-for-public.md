A Rye resident has received a special award from Sussex Police for his services to community safety across Hastings and Rother.

Neale East was presented with an East Sussex Divisional Commendation at a ceremony at Herstmonceux Castle on Tuesday, November 17.

Neale joined the Hastings and Rother Community Safety Partnership in 2018, and since then has played an integral role in helping partners work together to tackle crime, anti-social behaviour and other issues affecting local residents.

The award ceremony was held after Neale retired in March 2026, and the citation said his contribution to the partnership had been invaluable.
