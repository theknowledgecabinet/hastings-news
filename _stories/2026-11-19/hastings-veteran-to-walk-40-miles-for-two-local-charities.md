John Holland-Hobbs from Hastings will walk 40 miles in 12 hours on Saturday 28 November to raise funds for the East Sussex Veterans Hub and the Hastings and Rother Ukrainian Branch.

Now in his 81st year, John has walked for charities since 1984, including 200- and 100-mile treks. He will set off from Rye at 0600 and go cross-country using roads and cycle paths, with designated checkpoints at Jury's Gap near the coastguard cottages and the entrance to the Lydd military training area. The Veterans Hub will support him at each checkpoint and be on hand to respond in an emergency.

His route is Rye to Lydd and back (20 miles), then Rye to Camber and back twice (20 miles).

John served with the Royal Army Medical Corps in the 1960s. He has pledge sheets at the Stand Easy Coffee Stop on Robertson Street in Hastings, where people can pledge an amount to be collected after the event or donate on the spot.
