Work is set to begin on a new restaurant at the Colonnade in Bexhill after plans were approved by Rother District Council.

Loungers was selected as the principal restaurant tenant at the seafront site in August. In a planning report presented to the district council's planning committee on November 12, the council gave permission for the removal of existing doors, shutters and screening and the installation of a new glazed shopfront to the three central bays.

Nia Russell, the planning agent for the applicant, said the restaurant would create more than 30 new jobs, recruited locally, and involved a fit-out of around £1.2 million. The site is expected to be in active use 363 days a year, with the venue open for craft events, quiz nights, music and comedy.

Cllr Christine Bayliss, portfolio holder for regeneration and economic development at Rother District Council, said the Colonnade was often dark and unlit in the evenings, and that the new tenant would bring a more vibrant atmosphere.
