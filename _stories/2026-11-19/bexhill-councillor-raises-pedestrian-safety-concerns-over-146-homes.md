A Bexhill councillor has raised concerns over pedestrian safety as part of proposals to build 146 new homes on land off Spindlewood Drive in Little Common.

Charles Church Developments Ltd has submitted an amended application to Rother District Council for the houses, with an access road off the A259 on Barnhorn Road. The proposal includes a pledge that 30 per cent of the homes will be affordable.

Cllr Connor Winter, Rother district councillor for Bexhill St Mark's ward, said he has visited the proposed access and met with residents, raising concerns about the impact on existing driveways, inadequate visibility, vehicle turning movements and pedestrian safety. He said road safety audits dating back to 2018 identified concerns about the proposed entrance, while National Highways' September 2026 response highlighted further unresolved issues, including substandard junction dimensions and difficulties accommodating larger vehicles.

Cllr Winter said: "Having already objected over flooding and drainage, I am now challenging the safety of the proposed access." Outline planning permission was granted in summer 2021 for up to 160 houses on the land, after the developer won an appeal against the council's refusal in 2020.
