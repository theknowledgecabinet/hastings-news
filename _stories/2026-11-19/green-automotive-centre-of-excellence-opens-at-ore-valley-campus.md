A new Green Automotive Centre of Excellence has opened at East Sussex College Group's Ore Valley campus in Hastings.

The purpose-built facility was opened on Thursday by Rebecca Conroy, CEO and Principal of East Sussex College, alongside representatives from Hastings Borough Council and the Hastings Town Deal Board. It is a key project funded through the Hastings Town Deal.

The centre brings the college's motor vehicle training provision from its former off-site location at Ivy House Lane onto the campus. Students will train in specialist workshops covering traditional vehicle maintenance, electric vehicles, hybrid systems and emerging low-emission technologies. The building also incorporates solar panels and a heat pump.

Cllr Julia Hilton, Deputy Leader of Hastings Borough Council and lead councillor for Shaping Places and Community Wealth Building, said the centre would provide essential training in green skills for local people. Graham Peters, Chair of the Hastings Town Deal Board, said the facility was delivering real benefits for the town.
