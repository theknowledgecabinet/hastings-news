Sussex Police are asking for witnesses after an alleged assault in Hastings town centre.

Police say the incident happened in Breeds Place, shortly after midnight, following the bonfire celebrations on Saturday, October 17.

A force spokesperson said the incident involved three men and a woman, all aged in their early to mid-twenties. Police believe the people shown in the appeal images may be able to help with the investigation.

Anyone with information is asked to call 101 and quote serial 1113 of 23/10.
