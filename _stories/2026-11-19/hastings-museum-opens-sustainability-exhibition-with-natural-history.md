Hastings Museum & Art Gallery is opening a new sustainability exhibition in partnership with the Natural History Museum and local students.

Fixing Our Broken Planet, Material Worlds opens on Saturday, November 21. The exhibition explores what the museum's mineral collection can tell us about the resources we rely on, where they come from, and how more sustainable choices could help protect the planet.

The exhibition was co-produced with students from The Mulberry Academy Hastings, who worked alongside museum staff, scientists and artists to shape the project.

Cllr Julia Hilton, lead councillor for shaping places, regeneration and community wealth building, said the exhibition brings together local perspectives and world-leading research to explore how the materials we use every day affect people and the environment.

The exhibition forms part of the Natural History Museum's Fixing Our Broken Planet initiative.
