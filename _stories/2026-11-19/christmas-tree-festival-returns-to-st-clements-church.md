St Clements Church in Hastings Old Town is holding its popular Christmas Tree Festival from Saturday 28 November.

Decorated trees, this year with the theme 'Christmas Characters', can be admired from 11am to 3pm on Saturday 28 November and Sunday 29 November, with a craft fair and seasonal refreshments.

The festival continues the following weekend with the Trees By Treelight event on Friday 4 December from 6pm, performances by the Two Towers handbells and the Winter Warmers border Morris side on Saturday 5 December, and live music from Glashin with a performance by The Hospice Choir on Sunday 6 December.

Last year the well-attended event raised £1,400 for The Two Towers Trust, the charity which raises funds to preserve the two mediaeval church buildings in Hastings Old Town.
