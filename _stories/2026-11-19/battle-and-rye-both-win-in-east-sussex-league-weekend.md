Battle Town and Rye Town both won in Saturday's East Sussex Football League Premier Division matches, according to the Hastings Observer.

Battle Town took a convincing 4-1 home victory over SC Pass & Move Arrows, level at half-time before taking control in the second half. Rye Town had to work for a narrow 1-0 win at Sandhurst, with Ben Reader scoring the decisive goal.

Elsewhere in the league, Parkfield recorded a remarkable 8-0 away victory at Sedlescombe Rangers, while Bexhill Amateur Athletic Club comfortably beat Westfield Reserves 6-0 in the Sussex Intermediate Challenge Cup.
