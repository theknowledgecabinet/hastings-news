Alabama 3 are bringing their acoustic show to St Leonard's Church, St Leonards-on-Sea, on Saturday 14 November, the Hastings Independent Press reported.

Formed in Brixton in the mid-1990s, the band fused country, blues, gospel, dub and electronica. Their 1997 debut album Exile on Coldharbour Lane established them as British originals, and its opening track 'Woke Up This Morning' became known around the world as the theme to HBO's The Sopranos.

Presented as Alabama 3 Acoustic, the concert strips away the festival scale for an intimate performance in the church on Pevensey Road, with Lol Hammond of Drum Club joining for a DJ set.

Tickets are on sale now for the 14 November show.
