The chair of Hastings Greens, Justin Lee Wynne, resigned his post and cancelled his party membership on Friday, citing an internal dispute over alleged bullying and his 'deeply uncomfortable' reaction to the national party's October 4 conference vote declaring the party 'anti-Zionist' and labelling Zionism as racism, the Hastings Independent Press reported.

National leader Zack Polanski has said he would not personally have voted for the motion, and several local Greens have made statements of regret. Cllr Glenn Haffenden, leader of Hastings Borough Council, endorsed a Facebook post by national deputy leader Rachel Milward, who said the motion would have 'a divisive and distracting impact'.

The Hastings & Rye Liberal Democrats wrote an open letter describing the motion as 'reckless, divisive and counter-productive', while local Labour councillors, led by MP Helena Dollimore, asked Green councillors to reject the policy in a public statement within seven days.

The Hastings and District Jewish Community, calling itself 'proud supporters of Israel', questioned whether it could expect to be discriminated against by Green councillors, while five members of Hastings Jews for Justice published a statement objecting to 'the weaponisation of anti-Semitism'.
