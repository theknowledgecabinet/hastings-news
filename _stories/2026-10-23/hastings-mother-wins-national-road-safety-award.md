A Hastings mother has won a national award for her campaign to make the roads safer, following the death of her son in a road accident.

Maria Dennis's 11-year-old son Harry died in December 2022 after being struck by an unsecured scaffolding board that fell from a flatbed truck while he was a passenger in his father's car in Hooe, near Bexhill.

Since then Maria has campaigned for tougher safety standards for commercial vehicles and safe load security, travelling the length of the country for conferences, presentations and workshops. She has also worked with Hastings MP Helena Dollimore, who earlier this year presented a petition signed by more than 2,000 people to Parliament calling for better safety standards in the scaffolding industry.

She and PC Barry Freeman of Sussex Police's commercial vehicle unit were named 'Outstanding Contribution to Road Safety 2026' winners at the UKROEd/NPCC Roads Policing Conference at the Radisson Blu Hotel, Manchester Airport.

"This award is for my boy, Harry," Maria said. "I will never stop championing for higher load security standards and road safety."
