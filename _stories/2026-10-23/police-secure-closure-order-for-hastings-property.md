Officers from the Hastings Neighbourhood Policing Team have secured a Closure Order for a property in Cornwallis Terrace, Hastings.

Sussex Police say the order was granted under Section 80 of the Anti-Social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014 following ongoing reports of anti-social behaviour, drug-related activity, and offensive and criminal conduct at the property.

The order came into effect on 16 October and will remain in force for three months. During this period access to the property is restricted to the landlord, emergency services and utility providers, and any unauthorised entry is a criminal offence that can result in arrest.

Sergeant Turner of Hastings Police said: "This action reflects our ongoing commitment to tackling the issues that matter most to our communities."
