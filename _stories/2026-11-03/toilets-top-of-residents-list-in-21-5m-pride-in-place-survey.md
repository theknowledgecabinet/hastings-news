New toilets have topped the list of improvements residents want in Hastings, after MP Helena Dollimore asked how the £21.5 million of Government Pride in Place funding should be spent.

More than 100 residents responded to the MP's appeal, and many called for new town centre toilets. The main town centre toilet block was closed in April 2017 after Hastings Borough Council shut the facilities to make budget savings, and the site has been boarded up since.

The only toilets currently in the town centre are a small unit at the top of Priory Meadow, closer to Hastings railway station. Recent improvements from the funding have included more litter bins appearing and a new seafront sign directing people to the shops, restaurants and galleries beneath Earls Court.

One resident said: “Our lack of toilets and beach showers is a disgrace. Businesses cannot be expected to offer all the toilet facilities in the town.”
