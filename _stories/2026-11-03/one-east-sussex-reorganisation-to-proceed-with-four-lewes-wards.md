The Government has announced it is minded to proceed with local government reorganisation in East Sussex, Hastings Borough Council has said.

It proposes to implement the One East Sussex plan submitted jointly by Hastings and four other East Sussex councils, but with a significant change: four wards would be transferred from Lewes district into an expanded Brighton & Hove authority.

Under the revised timetable, elections to the new shadow authorities are expected in May 2028, with the new councils becoming operational in April 2029. The Government said both the original One East Sussex proposal and the modified option met all six of its criteria.

Councillor Glenn Haffenden, leader of Hastings Borough Council, said the One East Sussex plan would bring together the services of Hastings Borough Council and East Sussex County Council within a single new authority, but warned the transfer of four Lewes wards would be “a significant change for residents, staff and local services”.
