Police said they are increasing patrols in and around Eldridge Way, St Leonards, near The Ridge, following reports of anti-social behaviour from local residents.

A police spokesperson said officers understood the effect anti-social behaviour could have on residents and the wider community, and that they were increasing their presence in the area, listening to local concerns and working with partner agencies.

The spokesperson said residents affected by anti-social behaviour should report it, as reports help build a clearer picture and target patrols. Concerns can be reported online or by calling 101, and 999 should be called in an emergency.
