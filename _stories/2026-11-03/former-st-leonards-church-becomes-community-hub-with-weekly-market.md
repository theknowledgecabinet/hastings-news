A former church in St Leonards has become a thriving community hub, hosting a popular weekly market and serving as a venue for concerts and events.

In 2019, Chris Ostwald bought the St Leonards-on-Sea Congregational Church at the corner of London Road and Pevensey Road, after other people interested in the building wanted to turn it into flats. He has since been restoring it largely on his own.

The church market, which started just over two years ago, has become a weekly attraction with food, drink and independent stallholders. The venue is planning classical concerts and gigs, with more lighting, stage and sound equipment on the way, and there are plans for a big café on the front steps overlooking the sea.

Ostwald's fiancée, Rong Jiang, oversees social media and event organisation, and the pair have recently taken on a market and venue manager. The project was highlighted in an episode of Homes Under the Hammer in October 2022. Ostwald said he plans to put in the deeds that the building can never be turned into flats, guaranteeing its future as a community asset.
