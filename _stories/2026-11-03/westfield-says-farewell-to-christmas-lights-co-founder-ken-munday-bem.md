Westfield, outside Hastings, has paid its respects to Ken Munday, who was awarded the British Empire Medal for his services to the village.

He died aged 96 on 5 October and was one of the people who started the annual Westfield Christmas Lights, which has raised more than £130,000 for charity over its more than 50 years.

A procession left Churchfields on Monday and made its way through the village to Westfield Church, where his funeral service took place. A further service was held at Hastings Crematorium for those unable to attend, and the family invited people to The New Inn afterwards to share memories and raise a glass to Ken.

Ken and his wife Brenda, with their neighbours Wilf and Jean, put Christmas lights in their windows in December 1975, which led to the annual village event, the family said.
