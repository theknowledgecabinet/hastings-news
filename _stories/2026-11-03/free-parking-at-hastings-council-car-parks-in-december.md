Hastings Borough Council has agreed plans to provide free parking at its council-owned car parks on specific dates in the run-up to Christmas.

Parking will be free after 2pm on Saturdays 5, 12 and 19 December, and on Friday 25 December, Saturday 26 December and Friday 1 January. On the Christmas and New Year dates, parking will be free until charges restart the following morning.

The initiative is part of a wider package of measures to support the local economy. The council has also written to East Sussex County Council asking for free on-street parking, and Southeastern and Stagecoach have been asked to consider offering free travel initiatives.

The announcement came as Hastings MP Helena Dollimore was pressing for free Saturday parking dates in December to bring Hastings into line with surrounding towns such as Bexhill, Battle and Rye.
