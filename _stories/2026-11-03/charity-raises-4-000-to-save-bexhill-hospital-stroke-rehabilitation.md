Becky, Sarah and Owen from the #DoItForJosh charity met the team at Bexhill Hospital’s Irvine Unit on Monday to mark the continuation of a vital local stroke rehabilitation programme.

The charity had reached its fundraising target of £4,000, stepping in to rescue a service that was facing closure due to a lack of funding. The money will enable East Sussex Healthcare NHS Trust and Active Sussex to continue delivering structured activity sessions for stroke survivors within the Irvine Unit.

The target was reached through community events earlier this year, including the Three Castles Ultra Marathon in May and the 4th annual Josh Alexander Cup in September, alongside public donations.

Josh’s mother and the charity’s chair, Becky, said the cause held a special place for them, as the charity’s co-founder and Josh’s father died following a stroke last year.
