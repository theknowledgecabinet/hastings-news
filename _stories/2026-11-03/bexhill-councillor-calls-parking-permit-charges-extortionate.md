A Bexhill town councillor has branded the price of residential parking permits in the town 'extortionate and over the top'.

Cllr John Lee raised the issue at a town council meeting on October 28 and tabled a motion asking the council to write to East Sussex County Council to raise 'serious concerns'. He said the recent restructuring of permit fees has seen some residential permits rise drastically, with residents forced to pay up to £115 for a first vehicle and £154 for a second, which he called 'an unfair tax on simply living in central Bexhill'.

He said the charges were £27 in 2019 and asked for a formal review of why they have gone up, saying he wanted to know where the money is going.

Cllr Tony Carroll said residents need to see what value they are getting for their money. Cllr Paul Wilson said the parking controls were brought in a few years ago after issues affecting staff working in town shops and businesses, and that the council would use some other measure if it did not use emissions.
