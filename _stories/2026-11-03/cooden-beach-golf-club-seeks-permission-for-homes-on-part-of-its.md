Cooden Beach Golf Club is seeking planning permission to build housing on part of its course on Cooden Sea Road, according to an application to Rother District Council.

The proposal would be for four detached five-bedroom houses fronting Cooden Sea Road and a five-unit apartment building, two-and-a-half storeys tall with a communal garden and a private parking court.

The application says the plans would involve shortening the course’s 18th hole, with the loss to be offset by funding for “much-needed improvements” to other club facilities. National planning policy seeks to resist the loss of sports facilities unless they are surplus to requirement or would be replaced by equal or better facilities.

The club was established in 1912 and says the site is in an “exceptionally sustainable location” close to Cooden Beach railway station.
