Sussex Police made 18 arrests in Hastings and Rother between 30 October and 1 November, a spokesperson said.

The arrests included seven for assault, two for theft of a motor vehicle, two for possession of class A drugs with intent to supply, and one each for drunk and disorderly behaviour, drink driving, shop theft, recall to prison and breach of a harm order.

Police said officers had remained on patrol over the weekend and responded to a range of incidents, alongside continued proactive work to identify and deal with those suspected of committing offences.
