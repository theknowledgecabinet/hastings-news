Southern has announced that Rye railway station will be closed from 3pm on the day of the town's bonfire celebrations, Saturday, 14 November, until the regular start of service the next day, Sunday.

Southern Rail Area Manager Paul Wyborn said the closure would follow previous years and would be for reasons of public safety.

Chief Inspector Sarah Taylor of Sussex Police said police officers would work closely with partners to maintain order, prevent crime and respond swiftly to any incidents during the event.
