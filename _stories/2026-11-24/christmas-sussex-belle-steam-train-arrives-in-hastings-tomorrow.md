The Christmas Sussex Belle, a historic steam train operated by The Railway Touring Company, is due to visit Hastings tomorrow, November 25. The train will arrive at Hastings station at 3:53pm, hauled by a diesel locomotive from Eastbourne.

According to the Hastings Observer, the train will reverse direction in Hastings before being hauled back to London Victoria by steam. It is expected to depart Hastings at 4:07pm, passing through West St Leonards at 4:12pm.

The event is part of a popular pre-Christmas tour across Kent and Sussex. The train is noted as being highly popular among spectators and photographers who travel to locations along the route to await its arrival.
