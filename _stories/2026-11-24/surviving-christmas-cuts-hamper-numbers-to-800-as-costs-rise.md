Hastings charity Surviving Christmas has reduced the number of festive food hampers it distributes this year, citing the impact of rising costs.

The charity, which has supported vulnerable and homeless people in Hastings and Rother since 1985, is producing 800 hampers instead of the over 1,000 sent out last year. Project coordinator Megan Skinner said that while the total cost for food has remained the same, the real-term increase is about 45 per cent.

Despite the cut, the project will proceed with food already ordered and volunteers ready to pack on December 14. The charity is appealing for monetary donations to cover the shortfall, noting that it spent £6,000 more than it raised last year.
