Broadbridge Heath took the latest Sussex derby honours by beating Hastings United 2-0 and moving into the Isthmian South East play-off places. The victory saw Broadbridge Heath move into one of the play-off places in seventh – just one point behind Hastings United.

According to the Hastings Observer, Broadbridge Heath took the lead in the 12th minute when Zac Young struck a left-foot shot from distance that beat goalkeeper Harry Haines. Young added a second goal on the hour mark after the referee awarded a penalty for a foul on him inside the box.

The visitors' best chance of the half came five minutes before the break when Sam Pidgeon’s shot deflected off the goalkeeper and went narrowly wide. The result keeps both teams close in the table as the season progresses.
