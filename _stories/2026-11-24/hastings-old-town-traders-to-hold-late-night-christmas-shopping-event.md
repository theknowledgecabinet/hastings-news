Hastings Old Town traders are inviting residents to a late-night Christmas shopping event on Thursday, 3 December. The shops will remain open until 8pm.

A spokesperson for the traders said the streets will be “twinkling with fairy lights” and feature a Christmas tree, mulled wine, and special events in many of the shops.

At 5.30pm, the Kin Choir will perform a Yule-themed set in Butlers Gap. The evening will conclude with a gathering for sea shanties in the Jenny Lind pub. Almost all shops in the area are expected to participate in the late opening.
