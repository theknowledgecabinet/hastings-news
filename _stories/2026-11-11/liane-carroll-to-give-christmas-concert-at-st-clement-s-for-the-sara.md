Award-winning jazz artist Liane Carroll is to give a Christmas concert in Hastings to raise vital funds for the Sara Lee Trust, the local cancer charity.

The Hastings music legend, with special guests, will perform at St Clement's Church in Old Town on Saturday 5 December.

The evening is being billed as a festive night of live music, festive cheer and community spirit, with the proceeds going to support the charity's work.
