A 45-year-old man has been charged with theft after stealing alcohol worth £130 from Jempsons supermarket at Rye.

Rother Police said the man, of no fixed address, was arrested following a swift response from shop staff and officers. After leaving the store, he attempted to evade police by running to Rye Railway Station, crossing the railway line and hiding on a low roof, the force said.

When officers located him, he made a further attempt to escape by trying to jump from the roof and over a fence using a tree branch. The attempt resulted in him falling at the officers' feet. No medical attention was required, and he was subsequently arrested and charged with theft.

All stolen items were recovered and returned to the store. The man has been released on court bail, with a condition not to enter Jempson's, Station Approach, Rye, and is due to appear in court on 3 February 2027.
