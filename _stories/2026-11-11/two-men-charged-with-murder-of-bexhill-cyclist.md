Two men have appeared at Lewes Crown Court, charged with the murder of a Bexhill cyclist, Sussex Police have said.

Stephen McMurdo, 71, was struck by a ball bearing as he cycled along Bexhill Road, St Leonards, on the evening of 10 July 2020, police said. He suffered a serious head injury that resulted in left-side paralysis.

Mr McMurdo died in October 2025. Joshua O'Toole, 29, of Maidstone, and Lloyd Powell, 32, of no fixed address, were originally charged with grievous bodily harm in connection with the incident on 28 July 2023, police said.

Both men pleaded not guilty to the offence of murder and were released on court bail, with a further hearing date to be confirmed, police added.

Stephen's wife, Jaki, paid tribute to him. She said: "Steve was a wonderful husband, father and brother who loved, supported and understood me. He made me laugh a lot. We were married for 25 years and although the last five were different, he never lost his sense of humour. We will always miss and love him."
