Saxon Mount School, in Edinburgh Road, St Leonards, has submitted a planning application to replace its four single-storey mobile classrooms with a new two-storey building.

Torfield and Saxon Mount Academy Trust, which runs the school, has asked Hastings Borough Council for permission to remove the four portables and erect a two-storey modular building containing eight classrooms and a link bridge on the first floor.

The school caters for more than 180 pupils aged 11 to 16 with special educational needs, particularly autism and moderate learning difficulties, according to a design and access statement submitted with the application. It said the new building is needed to address existing overcrowding and provide adequate teaching facilities.

Previous applications for a two-court sports hall and a ball games enclosure, approved in September 2025, have not been built due to cost and funding, the statement said, and the school has changed its priorities to address teaching space. A planning statement prepared by Rackham Planning Ltd said the existing accommodation has reached the end of its useful life and is no longer suitable as a long-term learning environment.
