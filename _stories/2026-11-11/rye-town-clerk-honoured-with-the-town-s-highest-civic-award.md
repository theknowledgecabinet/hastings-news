Richard Farhall has been made an Honorary Freeman of the Ancient Town of Rye, the town's highest civic award, in recognition of his 26 years of service as Rye Town Clerk.

Mr Farhall received the honour on Monday 9 November from Rye Mayor Chris Hoggart. The nomination was proposed by Councillor Andy Stuart, who described him as the "engine room" of the council, keeping everything running smoothly while guiding the town through years of change.

Mr Farhall stepped down after 26 years, bringing to a close what the Hastings Observer called a remarkable chapter of quiet leadership and steadfast service. In a speech full of warmth and wit, he shared anecdotes and fond memories from his time in office and remarked that the years had simply flown by.
