Two kiosks at the Grade II listed Colonnade in Bexhill are available to let for new businesses, offered through commercial property agents Dyer & Hobbis.

The larger unit is 186 sq ft and is available to let for £7,000 per annum; the smaller unit is 73 sq ft and on offer for £3,000 a year. Dyer & Hobbis said the Colonnade presents "a unique opportunity to secure a prime beachfront location" within one of Bexhill's most distinctive Grade II listed buildings, and that these are the last two of the smaller kiosk units remaining.

The other spaces opened in July after refurbishment work that started in October 2025, with Rother District Council agreeing new leases in June. The Colonnade was built in 1911 to commemorate the coronation of George V and was given Grade II listed status in 2008.
