Donations totalling more than £10,000 have flowed in to help a family farm in Isfield after thieves ransacked its shop, the owners said.

Balaclava-wearing men broke into South Brockwells Farm Shop on Friday night and took all the chilled and frozen produce, including packages made up for Christmas, along with kitchen and butchery equipment including knives, prompting owners Arron and Chrissy Wells to close the business.

Ms Wells said the pair had asked friends to help them raise about £1,500 to make good some of their losses, and were 'shocked and delighted' to find the total quickly rose above £10,000 and could top £11,000.

The family now plans to open the farm shop in December and to hold a party 'for all the amazing people who have helped us'.

Ms Wells said the dogs woke them to the intrusion: 'It was absolutely terrifying. Neither of us can sleep now, wondering if this is going to happen again.' Police were called but could not track down the car that left the scene. On the same night, a garage at Allens Farm, Five Ashes, was also broken into, with power tools and meat taken from freezers.
