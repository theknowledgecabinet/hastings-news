A Bexhill town councillor has described proposals for a potential 500 new homes in the town as a 'disaster zone waiting to happen'.

Cllr Paul Wilson, who represents Collington ward, raised his concerns at a town council meeting discussing Rother District Council's Local Plan, which sets out where new homes, businesses and shops can be built between now and 2042.

He said he was astonished that the draft plan still included 'the nearly 500 houses on and around the back of Chestnut Meadow in Ninfield Road', and questioned how the roads could cope: 'It is absolutely astonishing that they're relying upon Peartree Lane and St Mary's Lane to back up Ninfield Road. It is a congestion and a disaster zone waiting to happen.'

Cllr Wilson said building up to 500 homes would swell the area's population to around 3,900 residents, and that the site is near the Sidley stream, which links down to Egerton Park and the Polegrove, where flood prevention work is under way.

Cllr Paul Plim, who represents Central ward, said somebody planning the development 'has got to bite the bullet and say, right, if you want these houses you've got to build the road structure to support it'.
