Travellers between Eastbourne and Hastings faced delays on Wednesday afternoon after trespassers were found on the railway.

Southern said the power to the line had been cut in the affected area and no trains would run, which led to delays and possible alterations to services.

It said everyone had been ensured to be in a position of safety and the power had been recharged, with trains back on the move in the area.

Passengers were told some late-running trains could be altered, meaning changes onto more trains than usual or slightly longer waits, and were advised to travel on their usual route but allow an additional 20 minutes for their journey.
