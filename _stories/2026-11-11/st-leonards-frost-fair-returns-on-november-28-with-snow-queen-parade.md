The St Leonards Frost Fair returns on Saturday 28 November with a parade of colourful fairy-tale characters, led by the Snow Queen, processing through the streets.

The parade forms up at Christchurch on London Road at 12.30pm and sets off at 1pm, and local people are being invited to dress up and join in. Organiser Anton Burton-Windsor is asking participants to think folklore for their costumes: English and European, Brothers Grimm style, Krampus, Wildmen.

Entertainment is expected to include music and performances by the Raven Drummers and the Winter Warmers Border Morris side, centring in and around Kings Road. Following the parade, the Rheda's Dream Morris side will be hosting pub carols at the Nag's Head pub in Gensing Road from 2.30pm to 4.30pm.
