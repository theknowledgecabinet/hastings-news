Sussex Police is seeking a man in connection with the theft of cash from the Marks and Spencer store in Ravenside Retail Park, Bexhill.

The incident is believed to have taken place on October 14 at approximately 7.40am. The force posted the appeal on the Rother Police Facebook page on Tuesday (November 10), stating that it "would like to speak with him".

Police said anyone who recognises the individual or has information that could help the investigation should contact police online via 101, quoting serial 284 of 14/10.
